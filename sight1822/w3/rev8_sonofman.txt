- "Son of man". Christ calls himself the Son of man dozens of times in the four Gospels, in his own words. Five of them, each a man's life:
  - Matthew 8:20: "And Jesus saith unto him, The foxes have holes, and the birds of the air [have] nests; but the Son of man hath not where to lay [his] head."
  - Matthew 11:19: "The Son of man came eating and drinking, and they say, Behold a man gluttonous, and a winebibber, a friend of publicans and sinners. But wisdom is justified of her children."
  - Mark 10:45: "For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many."
  - Matthew 17:22-23: "And while they abode in Galilee, Jesus said unto them, The Son of man shall be betrayed into the hands of men: And they shall kill him, and the third day he shall be raised again. And they were exceeding sorry."
  - John 6:53: "Then Jesus said unto them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except ye eat the flesh of the Son of man, and drink his blood, ye have no life in you."
  Read them with Hebrews 2:14-17 above: "he also himself likewise took part of the same" (flesh and blood), and "in all things it behoved him to be made like unto [his] brethren".
- If Daniel 7:13 is pressed ("[one] like the Son of man", Sight 66), meet it in its own words. Daniel 7:13: "I saw in the night visions, and, behold, [one] like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him." Its "Son of man" is bar (H1247) + ʼĕnâsh (H606) (kjv_word_map). ʼĕnâsh (H606) is "(Aramaic) or אֱנַשׁ; (Aramaic), corresponding to אֱנוֹשׁ; a man; man, [phrase] whosoever." So the vision's own word for him is man.
  - The same name is spoken to Daniel, a man. Daniel 8:17: "So he came near where I stood: and when he came, I was afraid, and fell upon my face: but he said unto me, Understand, O son of man: for at the time of the end [shall be] the vision." Its "man" is ʼâdâm (H120): "from אָדַם; ruddy i.e. a human being (an individual or the species, mankind, etc.); [idiom] another, [phrase] hypocrite, [phrase] common sort, [idiom] low, man (mean, of low degree), person."
  - Hebrews 2:6 quotes Psalm 8:4, and Hebrews 2:9 names him, in the chapter that says he took part of flesh and blood. Hebrews 2:6: "But one in a certain place testified, saying, What is man, that thou art mindful of him? or the son of man, that thou visitest him?" Hebrews 2:9: "But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels for the suffering of death, crowned with glory and honour; that he by the grace of God should taste death for every man. [for the: or, by the]"
  - The coming in the clouds is his (Sight 66); the name he comes by is a man's.
