SCOPE: the verses each numbered premise carries under PRESSED WITH: (its pressed witnesses, md_notes_rulings 392; any other verse a premise names is listed beside it as informational, not a witness) of the list that closes [DR2] (pressed against the defender) and of the CHALLENGER'S UNSUPPORTED-L4 PREMISES list that closes [CR3] (pressed against the challenger), and whether the answering side's round 2 or link round names each verse anywhere (the defender's [CR2], [CR3] or [CR4], the challenger's [DR1], [DR2] or [DR3], each after the round that pressed the verse). The line beginning "cited in" under each side lists every verse the opposing side cited before that link round that no later round of the side that holds the link names (for the defender [CR2], [CR3] or [CR4], for the challenger [DR1], [DR2] or [DR3], later than the round that first cites the verse); the line is informational and is not a list of witnesses (md_notes_rulings 392): a verse on it is a pressed witness only when a premise of the list against that side carries it under PRESSED WITH:, and leaving a verse of the line unanswered never counts. Not read by this ledger: ranges longer than 12 verses, references written with v., slash forms such as 24:16/43, and chapter-only references. A reference written without its book names a verse only when the book is named earlier in the same sentence ("(1:24)" alone names none). A reference to a verse past the end of its chapter in the KJV is dropped; the cited-in line lists only references written with their book.
