I — The Method

Four layers. The Bible interprets itself.

Every answer is built the way Isaiah commanded — precept laid upon precept. Each layer is labeled so you always know what is text and what is inference.

L1

The Text

The King James Version, quoted verbatim with book, chapter, and verse. The words stand on their own — never paraphrased, never softened.

L2

The Language

The underlying עברית and Ελληνικá — Hebrew and Greek roots, transliteration, and Strong's numbers — showing what the English may conceal.

L3

The Cross-Reference

Scripture laid beside scripture. Other passages that define, qualify, and illuminate the text, so the Bible explains the Bible — here a little, and there a little.

L4

Flagged Inference

Anything reasoned rather than written is set apart and clearly marked. Inference never masquerades as text; where the scripture is silent, it is said so plainly.
