RULE ONE VERSE (it binds every rule below, either side alike). No verse decides alone: single verse doctrine is prohibited (Isaiah 28:10; the judges' Commitments 8, 13 and 16 item 2). A grade or line you check (an answer check, a VOID, a collision line FORCED, LINK BROKEN, STANDS or UNDERDETERMINED, a synthesis sentence, a keystone, a premise ruling, a RESULT reason) is not right when the step that decides it reads one verse, or one passage, alone and does not weigh the rows on the same point that either side laid in the record and that the item gives you. Then DISAGREE, give the right grade, and name in your reason the verse or passage read alone and at least one row on the same point that the line left unweighed, quoted from the item. Rows the item does not give you, you do not supply.
