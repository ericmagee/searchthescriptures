# OPENING: DEFENDER

**Topic:** Did Christ have a biological father?

**Rule of engagement.** Isaiah 28:10: "For precept [must be] upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon line; here a little, [and] there a little: [must be: or, hath been]". My case is a chain of rows read together. Each link below is set out by its layers, one layer to a line.

---

## I. THE ACCOUNT, IN MATTHEW'S ORDER

I set out the sequence first, so that no reader brings in an adultery reading. The rows name a husband and his wife from the start.

**1. Husband and wife already in the espousal.**
- [L1] Matthew 1:16: "And Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ."
- [L1] Matthew 1:19: "Then Joseph her husband, being a just [man], and not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away privily."
- [L1] Matthew 1:20: "But while he thought on these things, behold, the angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a dream, saying, Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost. [conceived: Gr. begotten]"
- [L3] Luke 2:5: "with Mary his espoused wife".
- [L4] The narrator, the angel and Luke all name the two as husband and wife. They do so before the taking of Matthew 1:24. The espousal is therefore a marriage in the text's own words.

**2. They had relations as husband and wife within the espousal.**
- [L1] Genesis 4:1: "And Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived, and bare Cain, and said, I have gotten a man from the LORD. [Cain: that is, Gotten, or, Acquired]"
- [L3] 1 Samuel 1:19: "And they rose up in the morning early, and worshipped before the LORD, and returned, and came to their house to Ramah: and Elkanah knew Hannah his wife; and the LORD remembered her."
- [L3] Ruth 4:13: "So Boaz took Ruth, and she was his wife: and when he went in unto her, the LORD gave her conception, and she bare a son."
- [L4] In these rows the way a wife conceives is her husband knowing her. Where the LORD gives the conception (Ruth 4:13) or remembers her (1 Samuel 1:19), the husband stands in the same row. Matthew 1:19-20 name Joseph her husband and Mary his wife. Joining these rows, the conception came by Joseph knowing Mary his wife.

**3. The pregnancy was found before the household coming together.**
- [L1] Matthew 1:18: "Now the birth of Jesus Christ was on this wise: When as his mother Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost."
- [L2] "came together" is synerchomai (G4905). Its KJV renderings are "accompany, assemble (with), come (together), come (company, go) with, resort."
- [L2] "take unto thee" (1:20) and "took unto him" (1:24) are paralambanō (G3880): "receive, take (unto, with)."
- [L3] Matthew 1:24: "Then Joseph being raised from sleep did as the angel of the Lord had bidden him, and took unto him his wife:"
- [L4] Matthew shows what "came together" means by what follows: the taking into the house (1:24). The union of husband and wife has its own word, "knew" (1:25; Genesis 4:1). So 1:18 dates the finding before the household union, not before the knowing.

**4. Joseph's purpose: to spare his wife public shame.**
- [L1] Matthew 1:19 (quoted above).
- [L2] "make her a publick example" is deigmatizō (G1165): "to exhibit:--make a shew."
- [L2] "privily" is lathra (G2977): "privately:--privily, secretly."
- [L3] Mark 7:8: "For laying aside the commandment of God, ye hold the tradition of men, [as] the washing of pots and cups: and many other such like things ye do."
- [L3] Mark 7:7: "Howbeit in vain do they worship me, teaching [for] doctrines the commandments of men."
- [L4] The rows call her his wife (1:19, 1:20). A wife with child by her own husband breaks no commandment of God. The exposure Joseph would spare her (deigmatizō, "to exhibit") is the scorn of men's tradition, which Mark 7:7-8 set against the commandment.

**5. The angel bids the public taking, and Joseph obeys.**
- [L1] Matthew 1:20: "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife".
- [L1] Matthew 1:24: "and took unto him his wife".
- [L2] Both rows use paralambanō (G3880).
- [L4] The angel's command and Joseph's act share one verb. The open taking replaces "put her away privily."

**6. After the taking, no relations until the birth.**
- [L1] Matthew 1:25: "And knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son: and he called his name JESUS."
- [L2] "knew" is ginōskō (G1097), the KJV word of Genesis 4:1 for the union.
- [L3] Matthew 1:24 stands just before it: "took unto him his wife:"
- [L4] The withholding runs from the taking (1:24) to the birth (1:25). The finding with child came before the taking (1:18). So the begetting stands before the withheld span, within the espousal.

## II. WHAT "BIOLOGICAL" MEANS: SEED OUT OF A MAN'S LOINS

**To Jacob.**
- [L1] Genesis 35:11: "And God said unto him, I [am] God Almighty: be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee, and kings shall come out of thy loins;"
- [L3] Genesis 35:12: "And the land which I gave Abraham and Isaac, to thee I will give it, and to thy seed after thee will I give the land."

**To David.**
- [L1] 2 Samuel 7:12: "And when thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom."
- [L3] 1 Kings 8:19: "Nevertheless thou shalt not build the house; but thy son that shall come forth out of thy loins, he shall build the house unto my name."
- [L3] 2 Chronicles 6:9: "Notwithstanding thou shalt not build the house; but thy son which shall come forth out of thy loins, he shall build the house for my name."
- [L3] Psalm 132:11: "The LORD hath sworn [in] truth unto David; he will not turn from it; Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne. [body: Heb. belly]"

**Applied to Christ.**
- [L3] Acts 2:30: "Therefore being a prophet, and knowing that God had sworn with an oath to him, that of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne;"
- [L3] Romans 1:3: "Concerning his Son Jesus Christ our Lord, which was made of the seed of David according to the flesh;"
- [L3] 2 Timothy 2:8: "Remember that Jesus Christ of the seed of David was raised from the dead according to my gospel:"

**The words.**
- [L2] "seed" at Genesis 35:12 and 2 Samuel 7:12 is zera' (H2233): "seed; figuratively, fruit, plant, sowing-time, posterity; [idiom] carnally, child..."
- [L2] "seed" at Romans 1:3 is sperma (G4690): "something sown, i.e. seed (including the male 'sperm'); by implication, offspring."
- [L2] "loins" at Acts 2:30 is osphys (G3751): "the loin (externally), i.e. the hip; internally (by extension) procreative power."

**[L4]** Four steps join these rows:
1. The oath's seed proceeds "out of thy bowels" (2 Samuel 7:12).
2. That seed is the son "out of thy loins" (1 Kings 8:19).
3. Acts 2:30 applies "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh" to Christ, and osphys carries "procreative power."
4. Romans 1:3 calls him "of the seed (sperma) of David according to the flesh."

So the promise is fulfilled in seed out of a man's loins. That is what "biological" means in this debate.

## III. HE WAS FULLY HUMAN, AND SEED BRINGS FORTH AFTER HIS KIND

**(1) Christ was fully human.**
- [L1] John 1:14: "And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth."
- [L3] Hebrews 2:14: "Forasmuch then as the children are partakers of flesh and blood, he also himself likewise took part of the same; that through death he might destroy him that had the power of death, that is, the devil;"
- [L3] Hebrews 2:16: "For verily he took not on [him the nature of] angels; but he took on [him] the seed of Abraham. [took not...: Gr. taketh not hold of angels, but of the seed of Abraham he taketh hold]"
- [L3] Hebrews 2:17: "Wherefore in all things it behoved him to be made like unto [his] brethren, that he might be a merciful and faithful high priest in things [pertaining] to God, to make reconciliation for the sins of the people."
- [L3] Matthew 8:20: "And Jesus saith unto him, The foxes have holes, and the birds of the air [have] nests; but the Son of man hath not where to lay [his] head."
- [L3] Mark 10:45: "For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many."
- [L4] "Likewise" (2:14) and "in all things... like unto [his] brethren" (2:17) set his flesh with theirs. His brethren's flesh comes by a father's seed (Genesis 4:1). So his flesh came as theirs came.

**(2) Every seed after his kind.**
- [L1] Genesis 1:11: "And God said, Let the earth bring forth grass, the herb yielding seed, [and] the fruit tree yielding fruit after his kind, whose seed [is] in itself, upon the earth: and it was so. [grass: Heb. tender grass]"
- [L3] 1 Corinthians 15:38-39: "But God giveth it a body as it hath pleased him, and to every seed his own body. All flesh [is] not the same flesh: but [there is] one [kind of] flesh of men, another flesh of beasts, another of fishes, [and] another of birds."
- [L4] Each seed has "his own body" (15:38), and there is "one [kind of] flesh of men" (15:39). The flesh of a man is therefore of a man's seed.

**(3) The law forbids mixing seed.**
- [L1] Leviticus 19:19: "Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind: thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed: neither shall a garment mingled of linen and woollen come upon thee."
- [L2] "diverse kind" and "mingled" are kil'ayim (H3610): "two heterogeneities; divers seeds (-e kinds), mingled (seed)."
- [L2] "gender" is raba (H7250): "to squat or lie out flat, i.e. (specifically) in copulation; let gender."
- [L3] Deuteronomy 22:9: "Thou shalt not sow thy vineyard with divers seeds: lest the fruit of thy seed which thou hast sown, and the fruit of thy vineyard, be defiled."
- [L3] John 3:6: "That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit."
- [L4] John 3:6 keeps Spirit and flesh as two kinds, each bringing forth its own. Leviticus 19:19 forbids gendering two kinds. So a reading that makes the Holy Ghost the begetter of flesh has God doing what his own statute forbids. The kinds stay whole: flesh of flesh, as Genesis 1:11 orders.

**(4) "Is of the Holy Ghost" says where the child is from, in a row that names the husband.**
- [L1] Matthew 1:20: "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost. [conceived: Gr. begotten]"
- [L2] "of" is ek (G1537): "a primary preposition denoting origin (the point whence action or motion proceeds), from, out..."
- [L2] "conceived" (margin "begotten") is gennaō (G1080): "to procreate (properly, of the father, but by extension of the mother); figuratively, to regenerate."
- [L3] John 3:5: "Jesus answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and [of] the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God."
- [L3] Matthew 1:18: "the birth of Jesus Christ".
- [L4] John 3:5-6 use the same three words, gennaō + ek + Spirit, for the new birth, and what is "of the Spirit" is spirit. Matthew 1:18 names the child "Christ" in the same row. So "of the Holy Ghost" marks the holy origin and the anointing of the child.

  Gennaō is "properly, of the father." In the very row that carries it, the angel names "Joseph, thou son of David" and "Mary thy wife." It fits the chain: the father of the begetting is the husband the row names.

**(5) Luke 1:35 read with Luke 1:32.**
- [L1] Luke 1:32: "He shall be great, and shall be called the Son of the Highest: and the Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David:"
- [L1] Luke 1:35: "And the angel answered and said unto her, The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God."
- [L3] Acts 1:8: "But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth. [power...: or, the power of the Holy Ghost coming upon you]"
- [L3] Luke 9:34: "While he thus spake, there came a cloud, and overshadowed them: and they feared as they entered into the cloud."
- [L3] 1 Chronicles 28:6: "And he said unto me, Solomon thy son, he shall build my house and my courts: for I have chosen him [to be] my son, and I will be his father."
- [L4] The title and "the throne of his father David" are given at 1:32, before Mary asks at 1:34. The "therefore" of 1:35 reaches back to that throne promise.
  - The same "come upon" is the apostles' empowering (Acts 1:8).
  - The same "overshadow" is the presence cloud (Luke 9:34).
  - In one verse, 1 Chronicles 28:6 gives the title "my son" to Solomon, whom David begat.

  So "called the Son of God" is the covenant title of David's seed, given alongside a begetting father.

## IV. THE FATHER IS NAMED

- [L1] Luke 2:48: "And when they saw him, they were amazed: and his mother said unto him, Son, why hast thou thus dealt with us? behold, thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing."
- [L2] "father" is patēr (G3962): "a 'father' (literally or figuratively, near or more remote)."
- [L3] Luke 2:33: "And Joseph and his mother marvelled at those things which were spoken of him."
- [L3] Luke 2:41: "Now his parents went to Jerusalem every year at the feast of the passover."
- [L3] John 6:42: "And they said, Is not this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? how is it then that he saith, I came down from heaven?"
- [L3] John 1:45: "Philip findeth Nathanael, and saith unto him, We have found him, of whom Moses in the law, and the prophets, did write, Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph."
- [L3] Luke 2:4: "And Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judaea, unto the city of David, which is called Bethlehem; (because he was of the house and lineage of David:)"
- [L4] The father is named in four voices:
  - Mary, who heard the angel herself: "thy father and I."
  - The narrator: "Joseph and his mother," "his parents."
  - The town: "the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know."
  - A disciple: "the son of Joseph."

  Joseph is "of the house and lineage of David" (Luke 2:4). That is the line through which the seed of David runs (Romans 1:3).

## V. THE RECKONING: A QUESTION FOR THE CHALLENGER

- [L1] Hebrews 7:14: "For [it is] evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda; of which tribe Moses spake nothing concerning priesthood."
- [L3] Numbers 1:18: "And they assembled all the congregation together on the first [day] of the second month, and they declared their pedigrees after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, by their polls."
- [L2] "by the house of their fathers" is bayith (H1004), "a house... especially family," with 'ab (H1), "father, in a literal and immediate, or figurative and remote application."
- [L3] Hebrews 7:10: "For he was yet in the loins of his father, when Melchisedec met him."
- [L4] Under the law a man's tribe is declared "by the house of their fathers." Hebrews 7:10 places descent "in the loins of his father." So "sprang out of Juda" is evident through a father of Judah.

**The challenger is asked:** According to Hebrews 7:14, how is it evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda, given the law of Numbers 1:2 and 1:18?

## DECLARED CHAIN

1. **D1: The oath to David is fulfilled in seed out of a man's loins, according to the flesh.**
   [L1] 2 Samuel 7:12 "thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels"; [L2] zera' H2233 "seed... posterity"; sperma G4690 "seed (including the male 'sperm')"; osphys G3751 "procreative power"; [L3] Acts 2:30 "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ"; Romans 1:3 "made of the seed of David according to the flesh."

2. **D2: A wife conceives by her husband knowing her, and where the LORD gives the conception, the husband stands in the row.**
   [L1] Genesis 4:1 "Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived"; [L3] 1 Samuel 1:19 "Elkanah knew Hannah his wife; and the LORD remembered her"; Ruth 4:13 "when he went in unto her, the LORD gave her conception"; [L2] gennaō G1080 "to procreate (properly, of the father...)."

3. **D3: Joseph and Mary were husband and wife in the espousal; the finding with child came before the household coming together and the taking; the not-knowing runs from the taking to the birth.**
   [L1] Matthew 1:18-20, 24-25 ("Joseph her husband"; "Mary thy wife"; "took unto him his wife"; "knew her not till"); [L2] synerchomai G4905 "come (together)"; paralambanō G3880 "take (unto)"; [L3] Genesis 4:1 "knew" as the union.

4. **D4: "Of the Holy Ghost" marks the child's holy origin and anointing, in a row that names "Joseph, thou son of David" and "Mary thy wife"; Spirit brings forth spirit, and flesh brings forth flesh after his kind.**
   [L1] Matthew 1:20; [L2] ek G1537 "denoting origin"; [L3] John 3:6 "That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit"; Leviticus 19:19 "mingled seed"; Luke 1:32 "the throne of his father David"; Acts 1:8 "the Holy Ghost is come upon you."

5. **D5: The father is named in Mary's mouth, the narrator's, the town's and a disciple's, and he is of the house of David, by whose father's house the tribe is reckoned.**
   [L1] Luke 2:48 "thy father and I"; [L2] patēr G3962 "a 'father' (literally...)"; [L3] John 6:42 "the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know"; Luke 2:4 "of the house and lineage of David"; Hebrews 7:14 with Numbers 1:18 "by the house of their fathers."

6. **D6 (conclusion): Yes. Christ had a biological father, Joseph, the son of David, who knew Mary his wife within the espousal. The child is of his loins, "made of the seed of David according to the flesh" (Romans 1:3).**
   [L4] on D1 (seed out of the loins, sperma/osphys) + D2 (the husband's knowing, Genesis 4:1) + D3 (the order of Matthew 1:18-25) + D4 (ek and John 3:6) + D5 (the named father, Luke 2:48).
