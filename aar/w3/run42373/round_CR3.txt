  - The coming together (synerchomai, 1:18) is told in what follows as the taking. The angel bids it, "take unto thee" (1:20), and Joseph does it, "took unto him his wife" (1:24). Both verbs are paralambanō, "receive near, associate with oneself".

  - The rows call the two husband and wife while espoused (1:19, 1:20). Luke calls her "his espoused wife, being great with child" (Luke 2:5).
  - The canon's way of conceiving is a husband knowing his wife (Genesis 4:1; 1 Samuel 1:19).
  - So Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal. Matthew 1:18 dates the finding before the household union, and the "till" of Matthew 1:25 bounds the withholding from the taking to the birth.
  - It fits the link.
