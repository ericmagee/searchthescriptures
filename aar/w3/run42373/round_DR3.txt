# CHALLENGER, LINK ROUND

Each premise is answered under its own label. Every verse the defender pressed against it is quoted on its own [L1] line and met on its meaning.

---

## LINK 1: SUPPORTED

The premise is read as what its rows carry: David's seed reaches the child at the woman's end of the line. Joseph stands as husband and as head of the house.

**Footing**
- [L1] Matthew 1:16: "And Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ."
- [L2] gennaō (G1080), "to procreate (properly, of the father, but by extension of the mother)", is the verb of every "begat" in this list. At Joseph the line closes on "the husband of Mary". The next clause turns to the relative hós (G3739), "who, which", in the singular form "of whom". That is the feminine singular, so its one antecedent is Mary.
- [L3] Luke 1:31: "thou shalt conceive in thy womb, and bring forth a son".
- [L3] Luke 1:42: "blessed [is] the fruit of thy womb."
- [L3] Genesis 3:15: "between thy seed and her seed".
- [L3] Luke 3:23: "being (as was supposed) the son of Joseph, which was [the son] of Heli".
- [L3] Luke 3:31: "[the son] of Nathan, which was [the son] of David".
- [L4] Matthew 1:16 gives Joseph's father as Jacob by "begat". Luke 3:23 gives "Heli", and the words "the son" are the KJV's own italics there. Luke 3:23 itself marks the sonship of Joseph as "supposed". So Luke's list is a second line, which reaches David by Nathan (3:31). Matthew 1:16 gives the child as born "of whom", the woman. Genesis 3:15 sets seed at the woman's side ("her seed"). Luke 1:5 reckons descent at a woman's side as well: "his wife [was] of the daughters of Aaron". It fits the link.

**Answers to the presses**

**Press 1. Luke 1:27**
- [L1] Luke 1:27: "To a virgin espoused to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin's name [was] Mary."
- [L2] oikos (G3624): "a dwelling ... by implication, a family".
- [L3] Matthew 1:20: "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife".
- [L3] Matthew 1:21: "thou shalt call his name JESUS".
- [L4] The house of David is told here of Joseph. Matthew 1:20-21 bids Joseph, "son of David", take the wife and give the name. By Joseph's taking and naming the child stands in David's house. By Matthew 1:16 and Luke 3:31 the seed is carried through the woman. The two ends sit side by side. It fits the link.

**Press 2. Luke 2:4**
- [L1] Luke 2:4: "(because he was of the house and lineage of David:)"
- [L2] patria (G3965): "paternal descent ... family".
- [L3] Luke 2:5: "To be taxed with Mary his espoused wife, being great with child."
- [L4] The "because" gives Joseph's own reason for going up, namely his paternal descent. Mary goes "with" him as "his espoused wife". The enrolment reckons the child into the house at the head of the house. That is the legal end (Matthew 1:20-21). It does not displace Luke 3:31 as the seed end. It fits the link.

**Press 3. Luke 1:36**
- [L1] Luke 1:36: "And, behold, thy cousin Elisabeth, she hath also conceived a son in her old age".
- [L2] syngenēs (G4773): "a relative (by blood)".
- [L3] Luke 1:5: "his wife [was] of the daughters of Aaron, and her name [was] Elisabeth."
- [L4] Luke 1:36 names a blood relative on Mary's own side. Luke 1:5 shows that this Gospel gives a woman's descent in its own words. Mary's descent from David is carried by Luke 3:23-31, not by this row. It fits the link.

**Press 4. Psalms 132:11**
- [L1] Psalms 132:11: "The LORD hath sworn [in] truth unto David; he will not turn from it; Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne. [body: Heb. belly]"
- [L2] beṭen (H990): "the belly, especially the womb; also the bosom or body of anything".
- [L3] Romans 1:3: "which was made of the seed of David according to the flesh".
- [L3] Galatians 4:4: "made of a woman, made under the law".
- [L4] The body sworn of is David's, and I hold it so. Romans 1:3 and Galatians 4:4 both use ginomai (G1096), "to become, be made". The child was "made of the seed of David" and "made of a woman". David's fruit therefore reaches him as flesh through a woman of David's line. Luke 1:42, "the fruit of thy womb", is the same fruit. It fits the link.

**Press 5. Acts 2:30**
- [L1] Acts 2:30: "that of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne".
- [L2] osphys (G3751): "the loin ... procreative power".
- [L3] Hebrews 7:9: "Levi also, who receiveth tithes, payed tithes in Abraham."
- [L3] Hebrews 7:10: "For he was yet in the loins of his father, when Melchisedec met him."
- [L4] Loins name the source across generations. Levi was "in Abraham" and "in the loins of his father" many steps down. So David's loins reach the child through the line Luke 3:31 gives, without the husband being the begetter. It fits the link.

## LINK 2: SUPPORTED

**Footing**
- [L1] Jeremiah 22:30: "Thus saith the LORD, Write ye this man childless, a man [that] shall not prosper in his days: for no man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne of David, and ruling any more in Judah."
- [L2] ʻărîyrîy (H6185): "bare, i.e. destitute (of children); childless". It is followed by kîy (H3588), "for", the word that gives the reason.
- [L3] Matthew 1:12: "Jechonias begat Salathiel; and Salathiel begat Zorobabel".
- [L3] Matthew 1:15: "Matthan begat Jacob".
- [L3] Matthew 1:16: "And Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary".
- [L4] The reason clause gives the sense of "childless": no seed of his sits on the throne. Matthew 1:12-16 runs that seed by "begat" down to Joseph. A child begotten of Joseph is a man "of his seed". A child not begotten of Joseph is not. It fits the link.

**Answers to the presses**

**Press 1. Jeremiah 22:24**
- [L1] Jeremiah 22:24: "[As] I live, saith the LORD, though Coniah the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah were the signet upon my right hand, yet would I pluck thee thence;"
- [L2] chôwthâm (H2368): "a signature-ring; seal, signet".
- [L3] Jeremiah 22:30: "no man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne of David".
- [L4] The sentence is sworn: "[As] I live". It plucks the same man from the signet place before verse 30 closes the throne to his seed. The signet is the thing removed, and the seed is barred from the seat. It fits the link.

**Press 2. Haggai 2:23**
- [L1] Haggai 2:23: "In that day, saith the LORD of hosts, will I take thee, O Zerubbabel, my servant, the son of Shealtiel, saith the LORD, and will make thee as a signet: for I have chosen thee, saith the LORD of hosts."
- [L2] bâchar (H977): "to try, i.e. (by implication) select".
- [L3] Haggai 2:21: "Speak to Zerubbabel, governor of Judah".
- [L3] Matthew 1:12: "Salathiel begat Zorobabel".
- [L4] What is chosen is Zerubbabel himself, "my servant", and the office named is "governor", not one sitting upon the throne. His line is still the line of Jechonias (Matthew 1:12). The choosing of a signet and the barring of seed from the seat are two different matters. It fits the link.

**Press 3. Jeremiah 18:8**
- [L1] Jeremiah 18:8: "If that nation, against whom I have pronounced, turn from their evil, I will repent of the evil that I thought to do unto them."
- [L2] shûwb (H7725): "to turn back".
- [L3] Jeremiah 22:24: "[As] I live, saith the LORD".
- [L4] The rule of 18:8 is stated for "that nation" with its own condition, the turning. The sentence on Coniah opens with the LORD's oath, "[As] I live", and closes with the word "any more". It fits the link.

**Press 4. 1 Chronicles 3:17**
- [L1] 1 Chronicles 3:17: "And the sons of Jeconiah; Assir, Salathiel his son,"
- [L2] ʻărîyrîy (H6185): "childless".
- [L3] Jeremiah 22:30: "for no man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne".
- [L4] "Childless" is explained by the "for" clause: no seed of his prospers on the throne. Sons named in 1 Chronicles 3:17 are his seed who do not sit there. They are the line that Matthew 1:12 carries to Joseph. It fits the link.

**Press 5. Luke 1:32**
- [L1] Luke 1:32: "the Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David".
- [L2] thronos (G2362): "a stately seat ('throne')".
- [L3] Luke 3:31: "[the son] of Nathan, which was [the son] of David".
- [L4] The giver is the Lord God, and the throne is "his father David's". The line Luke 3:31 gives runs by Nathan to David, apart from Jechonias. It fits the link.

## LINK 3: SUPPORTED

**Footing**
- [L1] Galatians 4:4: "God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law,"
- [L2] ginomai (G1096): "to become, be made". The same verb stands at Romans 1:3, "made of the seed of David according to the flesh". gynē (G1135): "a woman".
- [L3] Luke 1:35: "that holy thing which shall be born of thee".
- [L3] Hebrews 10:5: "a body hast thou prepared me" [hast...: or, thou hast fitted me].
- [L3] Genesis 2:23: "This [is] now bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh".
- [L3] 1 Corinthians 15:39: "[there is] one [kind of] flesh of men".
- [L4] The child was "made of a woman" and "born of thee". The woman's own flesh is "flesh of my flesh" (Genesis 2:23), the one flesh of men (1 Corinthians 15:39). The kind is whole by her flesh. The body is "prepared" (Hebrews 10:5). It fits the link.

**Answers to the presses**

**Press 1. 1 Corinthians 15:38**
- [L1] 1 Corinthians 15:38: "But God giveth it a body as it hath pleased him, and to every seed his own body."
- [L2] sperma (G4690): "seed (including the male 'sperm'); by implication, offspring".
- [L3] Hebrews 10:5: "a body hast thou prepared me".
- [L4] The subject of the verse is "God giveth ... as it hath pleased him". The giving is God's pleasure. Hebrews 10:5 names the child's body as God's preparing. "His own body" is the body proper to the kind, and the kind is one flesh of men. It fits the link.

**Press 2. Romans 1:3**
- [L1] Romans 1:3: "which was made of the seed of David according to the flesh".
- [L2] ginomai (G1096): "to become, be made".
- [L3] Galatians 4:4: "made of a woman".
- [L4] I hold that the flesh is of David's seed. The verb is "made", the same verb as in "made of a woman". The seed is therefore made flesh through the woman. It fits the link.

**Press 3. John 3:6**
- [L1] John 3:6: "That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit."
- [L2] sarx (G4561): "flesh". gennaō (G1080): "to procreate; bear".
- [L3] John 3:5: "Except a man be born of water and [of] the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God."
- [L3] Luke 1:35: "The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee".
- [L3] Leviticus 19:19: "thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed".
- [L4] The press means that two kinds in one begetting is forbidden mingling. John 3:5-6 speaks of the new birth of men. The statute forbids mingled seed. Luke 1:35 names a coming upon and an overshadowing, which is power and not sown seed. So no seed of a second kind is joined, and the flesh is of one kind. It fits the link.

**Press 4. Genesis 2:22**
- [L1] Genesis 2:22: "And the rib, which the LORD God had taken from man, made he a woman, and brought her unto the man." [made: Heb. builded]
- [L2] bânâh (H1129): "to build".
- [L3] Genesis 2:23: "flesh of my flesh: she shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man."
- [L4] The row grants that the flesh God built was "flesh of my flesh". The woman's flesh is therefore the man's kind. What is born of her is of that kind. It fits the link.

**Press 5. Hebrews 2:17**
- [L1] Hebrews 2:17: "Wherefore in all things it behoved him to be made like unto [his] brethren, that he might be a merciful and faithful high priest in things [pertaining] to God".
- [L2] homoioō (G3666): "to assimilate; to become similar".
- [L3] Hebrews 2:14: "the children are partakers of flesh and blood, he also himself likewise took part of the same".
- [L4] The likeness is in flesh and blood, "took part of the same", and its stated aim is the high priest's mercy. Galatians 4:4 gives the manner, "made of a woman". It fits the link.

## LINK 4: SUPPORTED

**Footing**
- [L1] Matthew 1:19: "Then Joseph her husband, being a just [man], and not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away privily."
- [L2] apolyō (G630): "to free fully ... (specially) divorce". dikaios (G1342): "equitable (in character or act)".
- [L3] Matthew 1:18: "she was found with child of the Holy Ghost".
- [L3] Matthew 1:20: "fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost. [conceived: Gr. begotten]"
- [L2] gar (G1063): "assigning a reason".
- [L4] Matthew 1:19 attaches both "just" and "not willing to make her a publick example" to one act, "was minded to put her away", and that act is apolyō (divorce). Matthew 1:18 says she was "found with child". The angel then answers with a reason: "for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost". The reason given for taking her is the origin of the child. It fits the link.

**Answers to the presses**

**Press 1. Matthew 1:19**
- [L1] Matthew 1:19: "being a just [man], and not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away privily."
- [L2] paradeigmatizō (G3856): "to expose to infamy". lathra (G2977): "privately".
- [L3] Matthew 1:20: "thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife".
- [L4] The press means that putting her away privily is a shield, which a husband who was the father would give as well. The act in the verse is apolyō, "put away". It stands under "being a just [man]". A husband who is the begetter has no ground to put away his wife. The shielding is in the word "privily", which governs the way of the putting away and not the putting away itself. It fits the link.

**Press 2. Matthew 1:20**
- [L1] Matthew 1:20: "fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost."
- [L2] phobeō (G5399): "to be alarmed". gar (G1063): "assigning a reason".
- [L3] Matthew 1:18: "she was found with child of the Holy Ghost."
- [L4] The "fear" is of taking her. The reason attached to it by "for" is what is "conceived in her", begotten "of the Holy Ghost". The fear is answered by naming the origin of the child. It fits the link.

**Press 3. Mark 7:7**
- [L1] Mark 7:7: "Howbeit in vain do they worship me, teaching [for] doctrines the commandments of men."
- [L2] entalma (G1778): "an injunction, i.e. religious precept".
- [L3] Mark 7:8: "For laying aside the commandment of God, ye hold the tradition of men".
- [L4] The press means that Joseph feared the scorn of men's precepts. Mark 7:7-8 calls those precepts what sets aside "the commandment of God". Matthew 1:19 calls Joseph "a just [man]", and the law's divorce is the commandment of God (Deuteronomy 24:1, "then let him write her a bill of divorcement"). His justice stands on the commandment, not on men's tradition. It fits the link.

**Press 4. Colossians 2:8**
- [L1] Colossians 2:8: "after the tradition of men, after the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ."
- [L2] paradosis (G3862): "transmission, i.e. (concretely) a precept".
- [L3] Mark 7:8: "ye hold the tradition of men".
- [L4] The tradition of men is set against Christ and the commandment. Matthew 1:19 calls Joseph "just" and bids no tradition. His resolve is the resolve of a man who has found a wife with child and does not count the child his. It fits the link.

**Press 5. Matthew 1:24**
- [L1] Matthew 1:24: "Then Joseph being raised from sleep did as the angel of the Lord had bidden him, and took unto him his wife:"
- [L2] paralambanō (G3880): "to receive near, i.e. associate with oneself".
- [L3] Matthew 1:20: "for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost."
- [L4] I hold that the taking follows 1:19. It follows "as the angel ... had bidden him", that is, after the reason of 1:20 was given. What turned him was the origin of the child, not a recognised fatherhood. It fits the link.

## LINK 5: SUPPORTED

**Footing**
- [L1] Matthew 1:20: "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife".
- [L1] Matthew 1:21: "And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS".
- [L2] kaleō (G2564): "to 'call'".
- [L3] Matthew 1:24: "and took unto him his wife".
- [L3] Matthew 1:25: "and he called his name JESUS."
- [L3] Genesis 48:5: "Ephraim and Manasseh, which were born unto thee ... [are] mine; as Reuben and Simeon, they shall be mine."
- [L4] The angel bids two acts, the taking and the naming, and Joseph does both (1:24-25). In Genesis 48:5 Jacob counts as his own sons, "as Reuben and Simeon", the sons born unto Joseph. A man's word reckons to his house sons he did not himself beget. It fits the link.

**Answers to the presses**

**Press 1. Genesis 48:5**
- [L1] Genesis 48:5: "And now thy two sons, Ephraim and Manasseh, which were born unto thee in the land of Egypt before I came unto thee into Egypt, [are] mine; as Reuben and Simeon, they shall be mine."
- [L2] yâlad (H3205): "to bear young; causatively, to beget ... to show lineage".
- [L3] Numbers 1:18: "they declared their pedigrees after their families, by the house of their fathers".
- [L4] The press means that the sons are Joseph's begotten, Jacob's seed. I grant that they were "born unto thee", to Joseph. They stand in Jacob's house by his word, "[are] mine; as Reuben and Simeon". The house of the fathers is reckoned by such a claim. It fits the link.

**Press 2. Hebrews 7:14**
- [L1] Hebrews 7:14: "For [it is] evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda".
- [L2] prodēlos (G4271): "plain before all men". anatellō (G393): "to cause to arise; spring up".
- [L3] Isaiah 11:1: "there shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Jesse, and a Branch shall grow out of his roots".
- [L4] It is plain that he sprang out of Juda because Matthew 1:16 gives the house by Joseph and Luke 3:31 gives the seed by the woman. "Sprang" is the word for a branch rising from a stem, as in Isaiah 11:1. It fits the link.

**Press 3. Numbers 1:2**
- [L1] Numbers 1:2: "after their families, by the house of their fathers, with the number of [their] names, every male by their polls;"
- [L2] ʼâb (H1): "father, in a literal and immediate, or figurative and remote application".
- [L3] Luke 3:23: "being (as was supposed) the son of Joseph".
- [L2] nomizō (G3543): "to do by law (usage)".
- [L4] The house is reckoned by the father. The defender's own sense of nomizō is "by law (usage)". Luke 3:23 therefore places Joseph as father by law and usage, "supposed". That is the reckoning by taking and naming. It fits the link.

**Press 4. Ezra 2:59**
- [L1] Ezra 2:59: "but they could not shew their father's house, and their seed, whether they [were] of Israel".
- [L2] zeraʻ (H2233): "seed ... posterity".
- [L3] Numbers 1:18: "by the house of their fathers".
- [L4] The row names "their father's house" and "their seed" as two things to be shown. The house is a reckoning that can be shown by registry, as Numbers 1:18 shows. It fits the link.

**Press 5. Hebrews 7:5**
- [L1] Hebrews 7:5: "though they come out of the loins of Abraham".
- [L2] osphys (G3751): "procreative power".
- [L3] Luke 3:31: "[the son] of Nathan, which was [the son] of David".
- [L4] The loins named are Abraham's, a far ancestor, not the nearest father. For the child the loins are David's, reaching him by the line of Luke 3:31. The house comes by Joseph's taking and naming (Matthew 1:20-25). It fits the link.

## LINK 6: SUPPORTED

**Footing**
- [L1] Luke 1:34: "Then said Mary unto the angel, How shall this be, seeing I know not a man?"
- [L1] Luke 1:35: "The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God."
- [L2] pōs (G4459): "in what way? ... how". anēr (G435): "a man (properly as an individual male)". dio (G1352): "through which thing, i.e. consequently".
- [L3] Matthew 1:20: "that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost. [conceived: Gr. begotten]"
- [L3] Matthew 1:18: "before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost."
- [L4] Mary asks the way (pōs), "seeing I know not a man". The answer names the coming upon and the overshadowing. It then goes on with "therefore", dio, "consequently", the birth of that holy thing. The clauses just before "therefore" are the coming and the overshadowing. Matthew 1:20 gives the same child as "begotten" "of the Holy Ghost". It fits the link.

**Answers to the presses**

**Press 1. Acts 1:8**
- [L1] Acts 1:8: "But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me".
- [L2] eperchomai (G1904): "to supervene ... (figuratively) influence". dynamis (G1411): "miraculous power".
- [L3] Luke 1:35: "The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee".
- [L4] The press means that the verb and the power are empowerment for witness. I grant the same verb and the same power. In each place the verse names what the power brings: "ye shall be witnesses" in Acts 1:8, and in Luke 1:35 "therefore ... that holy thing which shall be born of thee". The power in Luke 1:35 is joined to a birth. It fits the link.

**Press 2. Luke 9:34**
- [L1] Luke 9:34: "there came a cloud, and overshadowed them: and they feared as they entered into the cloud."
- [L2] episkiazō (G1982): "to cast a shade upon ... to invest with preternatural influence".
- [L3] Luke 1:35: "the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee".
- [L4] I grant that overshadowing is the presence. In Luke 1:35 the presence is joined to "the power of the Highest", and "therefore" follows. Presence with miraculous power is followed by the birth. It fits the link.

**Press 3. Luke 1:32**
- [L1] Luke 1:32: "He shall be great, and shall be called the Son of the Highest".
- [L2] kaleō (G2564): "to 'call'". dio (G1352): "consequently".
- [L3] Luke 1:35: "therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God."
- [L4] The title was given in verse 32 before the question. In verse 35 "therefore also" adds the reason for it, and the reason is the coming upon and the overshadowing that stand just before it. It fits the link.

**Press 4. Ruth 4:13**
- [L1] Ruth 4:13: "So Boaz took Ruth, and she was his wife: and when he went in unto her, the LORD gave her conception, and she bare a son."
- [L2] hêrôwn (H2032): "pregnancy; conception".
- [L3] Luke 1:34: "seeing I know not a man".
- [L4] The LORD's part stands beside the husband where the row names his going in. Luke 1:34 names "I know not a man", and Luke 1:35 names no going in but the Holy Ghost coming upon her. Where the husband's act is absent, the row gives the Holy Ghost's coming in its place. It fits the link.

**Press 5. Luke 1:15**
- [L1] Luke 1:15: "he shall be filled with the Holy Ghost, even from his mother's womb."
- [L2] pneuma (G4151): "the Holy Spirit". ek (G1537): "origin (the point whence action or motion proceeds)".
- [L3] Matthew 1:20: "that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost."
- [L4] Luke 1:15 gives a filling "from his mother's womb", something added to one already conceived. Matthew 1:20 gives the origin of what is conceived, "of the Holy Ghost", with ek marking the point whence. A filling and an origin are different statements. It fits the link.
