SAYS HOW: "shows God acting by presence and not by a man". Counter: "when they only name it" with the pointer sentence ("thus"), if "acting by presence" is held a meaning and not a restatement.

J13 ACCOUNTS (thin). TAKES: "That is my link: reckoned son, not begotten son." WITNESS WORDS: "(as was supposed) the son of Joseph". SAYS HOW: "reckoned son, not begotten son". Deciding sentence: "the TAKES must say, in its own written words, how that position agrees with what the witness says": "reckoned son" restates "as was supposed", and "not begotten son" is the agreement, resting on a word the witness states. Counter: the pointer sentence ("That is my link"), and the absence sentence, first half, if "not begotten" is read as resting on what the witness does not state.
