## 2. The rule applied

The live judges' rulebook is engine_manual version 86, section 8. It is not the Proven Structure draft, which is not live.

- **Unanswered witness (ruling 373):** a witness pressed against a defender premise is answered only when a later defender round (round 2, 3 or 4) "quotes it or names it by reference and shows from the text why it does not overturn the link. **Re-citing the link's own footing is not an answer.**"
- **An unanswered pressed witness (ruling 372):** "the premise is UNEXHIBITED and counts toward CONCEDED LINKS and the standings."
- **Narrowing:** "a block that drops a limb and keeps the link narrowed is not a concession."
- **Standings:** COLLAPSED when 2c > L, where L is the number of premises listed against the side and c is the number conceded or unexhibited. A tie is not COLLAPSED. PROP-DEPENDENT applies when c is 1 or more.

## 3. Findings, strongest first

### 3.1 Premise 1: Deuteronomy 20:7 and Matthew 1:24 were not answered

**The premise charged** (quoting the defender's D1): "These two lines (Matthew 1:20 with the *ek pneumatos* lexical ground, and Matthew 1:25 with *ginōskō*) exclude a human biological father."

**The press:** "came together" in Matthew 1:18 means the taking into the house. The verses pressed:
- Deuteronomy 20:7: "And what man [is there] that hath betrothed a wife, and hath not taken her? let him go and return unto his house, lest he die in the battle, and another man take her."
- Matthew 1:24: "Then Joseph being raised from sleep did as the angel of the Lord had bidden him, and took unto him his wife:"

**The defender's answers:**
- **Link round:** "These date the taking. Matthew 1:18 dates the finding before the coming together, and Luke 1:34 puts Mary's 'I know not a man' inside the espousal." Matthew 1:18 and Luke 1:34 are both footing of the narrowed link 1 in the same block. That is re-citing the link's own footing.
- **Close round:** Deuteronomy 20:7 is never named. Matthew 1:24 appears only as "Matthew 1:24 has Joseph already taking his wife, so the abstinence is within the marriage as well." That addresses the time after the taking, not the window before it, which is what was pressed.
- **The defender's own grant:** in the close round it conceded "The challenger notes that conception is still future (Luke 1:31). I grant it." That removes Luke 1:34 as an answer for the period before the taking.

**Grade the rule requires:** UNEXHIBITED.

### 3.2 Premise 3: Isaiah 7:16 was quoted but not answered

**The premise charged:** "A question about means answered by naming a different means excludes the ordinary one."

**The witness:** Isaiah 7:16: "For before the child shall know to refuse the evil, and choose the good, the land that thou abhorrest shall be forsaken of both her kings." It was pressed to bound the sign within the prophet's own day.

**The defender's answers:**
- **Link round:** it quotes the verse, then answers Isaiah 8:3 (a different child's name). It then re-cites "Isaiah 7:14 says 'the Lord himself shall give you a sign,' and Matthew 1:22 applies it to this birth." Isaiah 7:14 with Matthew 1:23 is the declared footing of D3. No reason is given why Isaiah 7:16 does not overturn the premise.
- **Close round:** Isaiah 7:16 is not named.

**Grade the rule requires:** UNEXHIBITED.

### 3.3 Premise 6, the keystone premise: Numbers 1:18 was answered by a misreading

**The premise charged:** "He was conceived in Mary by the Holy Ghost without a human male."

**The witness:** Numbers 1:18: "And they assembled all the congregation together on the first [day] of the second month, and they declared their pedigrees after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, by their polls."

**The defender's answer (link round):** "Numbers 1:18, 'by the house of their fathers,' and Ezra 2:62, 'These sought their register.' These govern the priestly register, not the means of this conception."

**The record:** Numbers 1:18 is the census of "all the congregation." Numbers 1:2 reads "Take ye the sum of all the congregation of the children of Israel, after their families, by the house of their fathers." Numbers 1:47 reads "But the Levites after the tribe of their fathers were not numbered among them." The register in Numbers 1:18 is not priestly. That claim carries no layer, and it shows nothing from the text. The close round does not return to Numbers 1:18.

**Grade the rule requires:** UNEXHIBITED.

### 3.4 Premise 2: Luke 2:27 and 2:41 were answered with a bare gloss (weaker)

**The premise charged:** "Together they show that Joseph is husband and legal guardian but not begetter."

**The witnesses:**
- Luke 2:27: "And he came by the Spirit into the temple: and when the parents brought in the child Jesus, to do for him after the custom of the law,"
- Luke 2:41: "Now his parents went to Jerusalem every year at the feast of the passover."

**The defender's answer (link round):** "These are narrator words in the household sense." No layer supports the gloss. The challenger had already said in round 2: "No lexical ground is exhibited for that gloss." The close round does not revisit either verse.

**Grade the rule supports:** UNEXHIBITED. This is weaker than 3.1 to 3.3, because a judge may accept the gloss as a reason.

### 3.5 Premise 4: the charged clause was surrendered, and the rule does not settle how to count it

**The premise charged:** "This fits the Holy Ghost forming the body in Mary and does not fit a human father."

**The defender's link round:**
- "NARROWED TO: 'made of a woman' and 'a body hast thou prepared me' fit the Holy Ghost forming the body in Mary. They do not by themselves exclude a father, and I rest the exclusion on Links 1 and 3."
- "I concede that 'of a woman' alone proves no absence of a father."
- "I concede that Genesis 3:15, 'her seed,' is not decisive alone, since women are given 'seed' too."

**The problem:** the narrowed link drops exactly the clause the premise charged ("does not fit a human father"). All three judges graded it as narrowing, which the rule spares. The rulebook does not say whether a narrowing that gives up the charged clause is a concession of that premise.

**Recommendation:** put this question to the advisor of record for a ruling.
