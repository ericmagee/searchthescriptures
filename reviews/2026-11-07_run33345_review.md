## Core problem 1. The flow is running out of memory

**What the record shows.**
- Four of the six debate runs started on 2026-11-07 ended "crashed": runs 33276, 33289, 33311 and 33322.
- n8n gives the same message for each: "Workflow did not finish, possible out-of-memory issue."
- Each stopped at a different late step:
  - after the alternate's send-back build;
  - at the Drive file;
  - at the enforcer log;
  - at the panel failure note.
- Each stored execution is about 11 MB, and a run lasts about 70 minutes.
- Run 33311 wrote its debate row (3-0) before it died. Run 33322 wrote a row with no outcome.

**Why it matters.** Two runs in three die after the debaters' work is paid for. Some leave a half-written record.

**Solutions, in the order to try.**
1. **Split the flow into stages that run as separate executions:** debate rounds; then panel, cure and send-back; then alternate; then save. Each stage passes only the debate row id and re-reads what it needs from the database. A finished stage then frees its memory.
2. **Stop carrying full request bodies from node to node.** Every judge, cure, send-back and gate body holds the whole transcript (60,000 to 110,000 tokens). Keep the body only in the call node that sends it, and pass ids elsewhere.
3. **Remove the Grok reasoning hand-back** (Problem 6). It adds Grok's encrypted reasoning (about 280,000 characters per reply) to later Grok bodies. Its expression also loads the output of 26 call nodes on every OpenRouter call.
4. **Measure before and after.** Record the stored execution size and the crash count over the next ten runs. Do not count one clean run as a fix.

**What the record shows.**

- It disagreed with the judges on:

**A consistency defect in one row.**
- Gemini's stored verdict carries tag HELD and RESULT UNDERDETERMINED.
- The same row's recompute audit records "RESULT DEFENDER WINS."
- The outcome was derived as UNDERDETERMINED from it.

**Solutions.**
1. **Re-run the result recompute after the send-back, not only after the cure,** and store the recomputed tag, standings and RESULT together. A row whose tag and RESULT disagree is flagged and held from the count.
2. **Make an enforcer DISAGREE on a collision line or a keystone line a counted item.** If the judge keeps the line after the send-back, its sentence must quote the debate words the enforcer named. If it does not, the line is UNCURED, as K-W is today.
3. **Report the enforcer tally on the row** (disagreements by rule, and how many each judge kept or changed). Then a run where the enforcer rejects all ten collision lines is visible at a glance.

## Core problem 3. A press whose meaning the verse does not state is treated as a witness

This is the problem the project model named on 2026-11-05. It recurs here at Deuteronomy 22:24 with Matthew 1:19.

**The verses.**
- Matthew 1:19: "Then Joseph her husband, being a just [man], and not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away privily."
- The defender's round-3 press on Deuteronomy 22:24 reads: "MEANS: the law sets the other man's act in the espousal as a stoning offence, which Joseph meets by not making her 'a publick example'."

**What the record shows.**
- Neither verse names another man in Mary's case. The press line carries no "PROVEN IN" mark.
- The challenger answered: "The 'other man' of Deuteronomy 22:24 is the defender's own inference laid onto Matthew 1:19."
- **The defender then dropped it in its close:** "I do not need Deuteronomy 22:24's 'other man'."
- **The judges split three ways:**
  - Sol applied Commitment 5 ("UNPROVEN entries ... are not witnesses") and marked the challenger's answer MEETS.
  - Grok marked it MISSES: "the answer denies the press and does not show why the stoning row fits a knowing by Joseph."
  - Gemini first marked it MEETS, then changed it to MISSES after the send-back.

So one press, unproven and later dropped by its own author, was graded three ways, and the send-back moved one judge toward treating it as a witness.

**Solutions.**
1. **Enforce Commitment 5 in code before the judges read the ledger.** A press with no PROVEN IN mark, or whose mark points at a round that does not hold the meaning, is printed to the judges as UNPROVEN, not a witness. An answer check on it cannot read MISSES.
2. **Withdraw a press from the ledger when its author drops it** ("I do not need ..."). Any grade resting on it is struck, as a withdrawn link is.
3. **Check the press's meaning against its own verse.** A meaning that names a person, act or motive its verse does not contain is marked INFERRED. The judges are told that an inferred meaning is answered by showing the inference does not follow, which is what the challenger did here.

## Core problem 4. Withdrawal checks fire on trivial grants and cost seats

**What the record shows.**
- The single judge's audit lists 18 K-W items. Each is a sentence beginning "I grant," such as "I grant it.", "I grant the voice." and "I grant governor of Judah in Haggai 2:21."
- Each one obliges every judge to answer STANDS WITHOUT IT, CONCEDED or NOT HELD with a quotation, and an unanswered item removes the seat.
- In this run, two of four seats were not counted for debate-rule items: ChatGPT with 4, Sol with 1.
- The outcome rested on two seats.

**Solutions.**
1. **Fire K-W only on a grant that names footing a declared link rests on:** a verse, a word, or a premise the side listed. "I grant it." with no object is resolved in code to the sentence it answers, or is not an item.
2. **Collapse grants that answer the same press into one item.**
3. **Report seats lost to debate-rule items as a per-run figure.** If one seat in four is routinely lost, the rule is setting the panel size, not the judges.
