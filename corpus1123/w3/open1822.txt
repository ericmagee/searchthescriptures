IN ROUND 1, OPEN WITH THE ACCOUNT (operator, 11-23), before the IN DEBATE order below. State the whole sequence plainly and early, so that no reader, judge or reviewer brings in an adultery reading. Quote each row whole:
1. Husband and wife already in the espousal: "Joseph the husband of Mary" (Matthew 1:16); "Joseph her husband" (Matthew 1:19); "Mary thy wife" (Matthew 1:20).
2. [L4] They had relations as husband and wife during the espousal. The rows name them husband and wife (Matthew 1:19, 1:20), and the canon's way of conceiving is a husband knowing his wife ("Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived", Genesis 4:1; "Elkanah knew Hannah his wife", 1 Samuel 1:19).
3. The pregnancy became visible before the ceremonial or household coming together: "before they came together, she was found with child" (Matthew 1:18). The coming together is their ceremonial or household union (rulings 359 and 500); Matthew narrates Joseph's own act in it as the taking, which the angel bids (Matthew 1:20) and Joseph does (Matthew 1:24).
4. Joseph, "not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away privily" (Matthew 1:19): a private separation, to shield her from public scandal. The scandal rests on the tradition of men, not on the commandment of God (Mark 7:8, [L3] application: it speaks of washings).
5. [L4, on the rows quoted] The angel told him not to fear going on with the public taking, the open course set against the "privily" of Matthew 1:19: "fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife" (Matthew 1:20); and he "took unto him his wife" (Matthew 1:24).
6. After the taking, no relations until the birth: "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son" (Matthew 1:25, read with Matthew 1:24).
The layers for each step are held in Sight 266 (THREE EVENTS, NOT ONE).
