OLD:
"Before they came together" is synerchomai (G4905): "to convene, depart in company with, associate with, or (specially), cohabit (conjugally)".
NEW:
"Before they came together" is synerchomai (G4905). The KJV renders it "accompany, assemble (with), come (together), come (company, go) with, resort"; its sense at Matthew 1:18 is set by the narrative (Matthew 1:20, 1:24) and by rulings 359 and 500.
