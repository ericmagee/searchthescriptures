## 2. Why the defender never concedes: the causes, each with a solution

### P1. The defender is told to answer every premise SUPPORTED
- **Where:** Init with Guards, WIN THE PANEL, defender frame. "Foot every link on at least two exhibited layers ... so no link can be broken or counted against you. In the link round answer every listed premise LINK n: SUPPORTED with that footing."
- **Effect:** CONCEDED is never offered to the defender as a right answer. The general text says "A link honestly conceded as floating is DISCIPLINE, not defeat", but the defender-specific sentence overrides it with an instruction to answer SUPPORTED every time.
- **Solution:** replace the sentence with: "In the link round answer each premise as the text stands: SUPPORTED with its footing where the text carries it, NARROWED TO the limb the text carries, or CONCEDED where it does not. A conceded link the text does not carry is discipline; defending it is the fault the judges count."

### P2. Rule DD-2 calls an in-round concession a defense failure
- **Where:** D1 Build and D2 Build. "No concession, re-grade, or framework adjustment is ever made on the opponent's clock," and "A defense that concedes in-round to a context-stripped term is a defense-discipline slip."
- **Effect:** in rounds 1 and 2 the defender is forbidden to concede and is told a concession is a slip. The wrong claim carries into the link round already built up (round 2 added the Nathan route).
- **Solution:** strike the no-concession clause and the "defense-discipline slip" sentence. Keep only the answer rule from fix set 8: a correction is answered on the chain with book, chapter and verse. Add: "Where the correction shows the text does not carry your claim, say so and narrow or concede it."

### P3. The challenger can only break a link with a verse that states its contrary
- **Where:** WIN THE PANEL, challenger frame. "A defender link is broken only when you set beside it a quoted [L1] (or [L2]) text that states the contrary about the same matter."
- **Effect:** this is the stating-verse test aimed at the challenger. No verse says "Mary was not of David's house". So a claim the text never makes cannot be broken. The burden sits on the side that says the text is silent, not on the side that makes the claim. It contradicts DG-61 and rulings 396 to 398 (the meaning must be proven by the side that claims it).
- **Solution:** replace with: "A defender link is broken when its claim is not carried by the layers it exhibits: a claim of fact or meaning the quoted rows do not state, and no footed chain reaches, is unproven. Show the rows that bear on it (here: Luke 1:27, Luke 2:4 give David's house to Joseph; Luke 1:5, 1:36 give Mary's kin as Aaron's) and the gap."

### P4. NARROWED TO shields a link that still rests on the dropped limb
- **Where:** D3 Build (link round forms) and the judges' rule "a block that drops a limb and keeps the link in a narrowed form is not a concession: grade the narrowed link."
- **Effect:** the defender dropped "Luke 3 runs through Mary" and Luke 1:27 as grounds, then kept "the seed of David reaches Jesus through that body". The narrowed link still asserts what only the dropped limbs could carry. No check, and no judge, tested whether the narrowed claim survives without them.
- **Solution:**
  - **The rule:** a narrowed link is graded on the footing left after the narrowing. If its remaining claim still needs the dropped limb, it is CONCEDED.
  - **The form:** the NARROWED TO line names what it drops and states the kept claim with its remaining footing.
  - **A TRIAL check (K-NW):** it marks a narrowed link whose kept sentence carries a claim term that the dropped limb carried. Here the term is Davidic seed through the mother.

### P5. The two-layer bar counts layers, not what they carry
- **Where:** the footing rule (two lower layers exhibited), as the judges apply it.
- **Effect:** Romans 1:3 ("seed of David according to the flesh") and Galatians 4:4 ("made of a woman") are two layers. Neither states that the woman is of David's seed. The [L4] joins them by supplying that fact. The judges counted the footing as met.
- **Solution:**
  - **Footing lines state what they carry:** each footing line gives a one-sentence MEANS, as presses do under the proven-structure form.
  - **The judge's test:** does any layer, or the stated chain, carry each fact the [L4] needs? A fact the chain supplies but no layer carries is unexhibited.
  - **This is not the retired single-verse demand:** no verse has to state the conclusion. The test is that the chain may not add a fact absent from all its rows. That is ruling 397 ("if a script is claimed to mean something.. that needs to proven").

### P6. The outcome math pays for never conceding
- **Where:** the clause definitions. A withdrawn link counts toward w, and w of 1 or more gives PARTIAL-1; a withdrawn keystone gives BROKEN-2. A narrowed link counts toward nothing.
- **Effect:** a model that reads the rulebook learns that conceding moves the verdict toward the challenger and narrowing costs nothing. Every incentive points to narrowing in words and keeping the claim.
- **Solution:** with P4, a narrowed link that still needs its dropped limb counts as CONCEDED toward CONCEDED LINKS and the standings. Keep w for links withdrawn outright, so an honest concession and a disguised one score alike, and only the honest one avoids the judges' finding.

### P7. The answer check confirms words, not the meaning claimed
- **Where:** the judges' ANSWER CHECK lines (from the proven-structure change).
- **Effect:** "ANSWER CHECK: Luke 3:31 -- [DR3] -- 'Luke's line runs by Nathan' -- HOLDS" checks that Luke 3:31 contains Nathan and David. It does not check what the defender used it for, which is that Luke's line is Mary's. The text names Joseph at the head of that line (Luke 3:23).
- **Solution:** the answer check quotes the answer's MEANS sentence and confirms that the answer's own layers carry it. An answer whose meaning goes beyond its rows does not HOLD.
