# OPENING: Jesus had no biological father

**Position.** I defend the standard reading: Jesus had no human biological father. His conception in Mary was by the Holy Ghost, and Joseph was husband and legal guardian, not begetter.

**Term.** "Biological father" means the male parent who begets, the one the KJV marks with "begat." The Greek is *gennaō* (to beget, when said of a father; to bear or be born, when said of a mother). The question is whether the text assigns that act to a man.

---

## I. Matthew 1: the begetting chain breaks at Joseph

**1. The repeated "begat" stops.** [L1] Matthew 1:2-16 runs "Abraham begat Isaac; and Isaac begat Jacob…" through to verse 16: "And Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ." [L2] Every "begat" is *egennēsen* (he begat), active voice, with a male subject. At verse 16 the pattern changes. The text says *ex hēs egennēthē Iēsous* (out of whom was born Jesus). *Hēs* (of whom) is feminine singular, so its antecedent can only be Mary, not Joseph. *Egennēthē* is passive (was born). Joseph is called *ton andra Marias* (the husband of Mary), and no active "begat" is ascribed to him. [L4] The genealogy has "begat" in every prior generation and withholds it here. Both the verb change (L2) and the feminine relative (L2) stand in the verse (L1). So the text deliberately declines to make Joseph the begetter.

**2. The source of the conception is stated.** [L1] Matthew 1:18: "When as his mother Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost." [L2] *Prin ē sunelthein autous* (before they came together) uses *sunerchomai* (to come together), here marital union. *Heurethē en gastri echousa* means "was found having in womb." *Ek pneumatos hagiou* means "out of Spirit holy," and *ek* (out of) marks source or origin. [L1] Matthew 1:20: "that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost." [L2] *To gennēthen en autē* (that which has been begotten or conceived in her) is the same *gennaō*, and *ek pneumatos estin hagiou* gives its source as the Holy Ghost. [L4] The word that elsewhere names a father's begetting is used here, and its source is *ek pneumatos* (out of Spirit). That leaves no place for a human male source.

**3. Joseph's own reaction fits no other reading.** [L1] Matthew 1:19: "Then Joseph her husband, being a just man, and not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away privily." [L2] *Apolusai* (to dismiss, to release) is the word for divorce. [L1] Matthew 1:20 then has the angel say "fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife." [L4] Joseph knew he was not the father (Matt 1:19), and he accepted the pregnancy only after the angel's word (Matt 1:20). The reassurance would be needless if he were the father.

**4. No union before the birth.** [L1] Matthew 1:25: "And knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son." [L2] *Ouk eginōsken* (he was not knowing) uses *ginōskō* (to know), the verb for marital intimacy. [L3] Genesis 4:1, "Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived," uses *yada'* (to know) in the same sense. This verse speaks only of the time up to the birth, and that is all my case needs. [L4] Both the verb's idiom (L2, Gen 4:1) and Matthew's statement (L1) place the conception wholly outside any union with Joseph.

## II. Luke 1: Mary's own statement and the angel's answer

CHAIN SCOPE (Isaiah 28:10): no single verse is required to state a link or its contrary. The witnesses a side exhibits, read together line upon line, are the text's own statement, and "no single verse states it" is never a ground for STANDS or UNDERDETERMINED. A link marked STANDS, or a collision graded UNDERDETERMINED, against a witness the other side exhibited names in the same line the round that answered that witness. A witness left unanswered is a BROKEN ground. That a single verse does not state a link or its contrary is never a ground for STANDS or UNDERDETERMINED, for either side. A witness contradicts a link only when its own words state the contrary; a verb that leaves the point unnarrated does not, for either side. This tests a pressed witness, not a chained premise, which is judged by its footing (DG-61). When the witnesses pressed against a link are the pressing side's own chain read together, the collision weighs chain against chain by footing, never verse by verse; that no one of them states the contrary in its own words is the stating-verse test DG-61 retires, at a judge's seat as at a debater's, for either side. Grade a link BROKEN when the other side exhibited [L1]/[L2]/[L3] witnesses that together contradict it and the broken side's later rounds left any of those witnesses UNANSWERED (COUNTING RULE). Grade UNDERDETERMINED only when the side's later rounds answered every such witness and the witnesses, read together, still leave the point open. k is the number of lines graded DEFENDER LINK BROKEN. A side with any link graded BROKEN here cannot be SELF-SUPPORTED.

DEBATE RULE VIOLATION (Isaiah 28:10: "here a little, [and] there a little"). The rule on a BROKEN ground binds you as it binds the debaters. On every COLLISION POINTS line you grade DEFENDER LINK BROKEN or CHALLENGER LINK BROKEN, exhibit on that line itself at least two lower layers of DIFFERENT kinds, each tagged: [L1] with the KJV words quoted; [L2] with the Hebrew or Greek word in its own script, with its transliteration marks, or with its Strong's number; [L3] with the cross-reference named. Two quotations at one layer are one layer. A BROKEN line without them is a DEBATE RULE VIOLATION. The system checks every BROKEN line after you answer; a violating line is returned to you to cure, by exhibiting the two layers, or to answer in writing why it stands. An answer that does not exhibit them leaves the violation standing, and your judgment is then not counted. The same holds when your CONCEDED LINKS or CHALLENGER CONCEDED LINKS count is 0 while you grade a link of that side BROKEN for a witness left unanswered. Both sides alike.

DEFENDER CHAIN MARK: <FOOTED|STRAINED|SLACK> -- <one clause naming the load-bearing link that decides the mark>.
CHALLENGER CHAIN MARK: <FOOTED|STRAINED|SLACK> -- <one clause naming the load-bearing link that decides the mark>.
These two lines come last, after OUT-OF-ROUND CONCESSIONS, the defender's first. One definition marks each side's own constructed chain, the defender's declared chain (D1 to the conclusion link) and the challenger's own alternative reading: FOOTED = every load-bearing link of the side's chain is footed on an exhibited L1-L3 layer; its reading stands as a built structure. STRAINED = the chain is built but one or more load-bearing links float: a link floats only when the block that states it exhibits fewer than two prior layers; the mark names the layers exhibited and the layer lacking; it stands only under charity. SLACK = the side advanced no coherent chain of its own: objections without a constructed reading, or a construction that never cohered. Each mark describes ONLY how well that side built its own chain. It is descriptive, not a verdict. It MUST NOT change DEFENSE STANDING, CHALLENGE STANDING, CONCEDED LINKS, CHALLENGER CONCEDED LINKS, the CLAUSE line, KEYSTONE or the top-line verdict. A FOOTED chain is fully compatible with its side losing the debate; a SLACK chain is fully compatible with its side winning it.
