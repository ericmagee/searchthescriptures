DEFINITION THE JUDGE WORKS UNDER:
CHAIN (Isaiah 28:10: "For precept [must be] upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon line; here a little, [and] there a little"): an argument built from many verses. Each LINK is one step, standing on its verses and on the steps before it. A link is judged by its support, never by whether any verse, or the chain as a whole, states it word for word; and a verse whose words leave the question open decides nothing by itself.
A verse whose own words state the contrary is weighed with the chains. It does not decide alone.
In the KJV, capitals mark who is meant: father and Father, god and God and GOD, lord and Lord and LORD are different words. A judge may not treat them as one word or as balancing each other.
