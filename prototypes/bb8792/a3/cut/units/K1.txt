HELD. The text states that before Mary and Joseph came together she was found with child of the Holy Ghost ([L1] Matthew 1:18, 20), with the active begetting verb withheld from Joseph in the record of the generations ([L2] egennēthē in Matthew 1:16). While the challenger demonstrated that Davidic lineage is reckoned according to the flesh ([L1] Romans 1:3; [L3] Acts 2:30), neither text states that Joseph begat the child ([L4]). Because the defender answered the witnesses pressed against the chain and neither withdrew nor suffered refutation of the declared links, the defense holds ([L4]).
