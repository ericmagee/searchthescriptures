The weakest defender link is the Davidic flesh-route through Mary (the part of link 6 resting on Luke 3:23-31). The defender conceded it is an inference ("grammar permits... but does not require it") and that the text never states Mary's lineage. The only Davidic house the KJV names is Joseph's ("of the house of David," Luke 1:27; "of the house and lineage of David," Luke 2:4). The [L4] is not floating, since it exhibits [L1] Luke 3:23, 31 and Matthew 1:16 plus [L3] Ezra 2:61 and 1 Chronicles 2:21-22, but it is the thinnest. It leaves 2 Samuel 7:12 ("out of thy bowels") and Acts 2:30 ("the fruit of his loins") answered only by the "loins are generational" reading, though the defender does not stake the keystone on this link and Matthew 1:16, 18-20 and Luke 1:34-35 still carry the chain.
