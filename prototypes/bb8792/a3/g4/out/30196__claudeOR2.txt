The weakest defender link is link 6, the claim that Luke 3 gives Mary's line through Heli. The inference is footed, not floating, since its block exhibits [L1] Luke 3:23, Ezra 2:61 and 1 Chronicles 2:21-22 plus the [L2] bare genitive *tou* (of the), but it only shows that the reading is permitted. Luke 3:23 says Joseph "was the son of Heli", no text names Mary's father or her descent, and the defender admits it is "an inference and is not stated". That leaves the "according to the flesh" rows (Romans 1:3; Acts 2:30) without a named route to David, which is the one place the challenger's seed-out-of-the-body rows go largely unanswered.
