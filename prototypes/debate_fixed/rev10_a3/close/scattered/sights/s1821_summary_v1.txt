THE CHAIN (Isaiah 28:10, DG-61): the LORD chose Israel ("Israel mine elect," Isaiah 45:4; Deuteronomy 7:6); within Israel the election is a remnant by grace, "they are not all Israel, which are of Israel" (Romans 9:6), "I have reserved to myself seven thousand men" (11:4), "a remnant according to the election of grace ... the election hath obtained it, and the rest were blinded" (11:5, 7); and the LORD gives the number of the sealed, "an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel" (Revelation 7:4), twelve thousand of each tribe named (7:5-8). SIX SHARED WORDS join the sealed to the elect: (1) WITH HIM: "a Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him (met' autou) an hundred forty and four thousand" (Revelation 14:1); "they that are with him (met' autou) are called, and chosen (eklektoi, G1588), and faithful" (17:14). (2) WITHOUT FAULT: "they are without fault (amōmoi, G299) before the throne of God" (14:5); "chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame (amōmous, G299)" (Ephesians 1:4); the word stands in 7 rows of the canon. (3) SEALED: the sealed of the tribes (sphragizō, G4972; Revelation 7:3-8); the chosen of Ephesians 1:4 "sealed with that holy Spirit of promise" (1:13, the same verb). (4) FIRSTFRUITS: "the firstfruits (aparchē, G536) unto God and to the Lamb" (Revelation 14:4); "if the firstfruit be holy, the lump is also holy" in Paul's election chapter (Romans 11:16); "Israel was holiness unto the LORD, and the firstfruits of his increase" (Jeremiah 2:3); "a kind of firstfruits," written "to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad" (James 1:1, 18); the word stands in 8 rows. (5) SERVANTS AND ELECT: "the servants (doulous) of our God" sealed in their foreheads (Revelation 7:3); "mine elect shall inherit it, and my servants shall dwell there" (Isaiah 65:9); "my chosen ... his servants" (65:15). (6) THE FOUR WINDS: "four angels ... holding the four winds (tessaras anemous) of the earth" till the servants are sealed (Revelation 7:1-3); "his angels ... shall gather together his elect from the four winds (tessarōn anemōn)" (Matthew 24:31; Mark 13:27). THE PICTURE: the 144,000 sealed of all the tribes of the children of Israel are the elect, the remnant by the election of grace, chosen before the foundation of the world, the firstfruits with the Lamb, called and chosen and faithful (L1-L2-L3). The number is declared, not withheld (Revelation 7:4, "I heard the number"; DG-12). The great multitude "which no man could number" (7:9) is the harvest after the firstfruits, one people (Sights 352, 294). Who and where they are today is the Shepherd's to name (DG-20).
