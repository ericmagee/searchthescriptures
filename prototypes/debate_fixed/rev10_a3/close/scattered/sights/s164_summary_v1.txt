Rom 11:13-14: Paul speaks 'to you Gentiles' (v.13) and hopes to 'provoke to emulation [them which are] my flesh, and might save some of them' (v.14). On the verse's own surface 'my flesh' names the PROVOKED party, Paul's kinsmen (Rom 9:3 'my brethren, my kinsmen according to the flesh'), a party DISTINCT from the addressed Gentiles (L1; C5 instrumental address). 'My flesh' is kinsman language (L1-L2). The provocation is the jealousy chain of Deuteronomy 32:21 quoted at Romans 10:19 and set up at Romans 11:11 ("salvation is come unto the Gentiles, for to provoke them to jealousy"): the Gentiles Paul addresses are scattered Israel, Lo-ammi, and they provoke his kinsmen (DG-1; Conclusions #58, #73). Never deploy this sight as 'Paul calls the Gentiles my flesh': the verse distinguishes the provoking addressees from the provoked kinsmen, both of Israel. Who and where they are today is the Shepherd's to name (DG-20).
