L1-L2: In Rom 9:24 Paul says the vessels of mercy are called "not of the Jews only, but also of the Gentiles." He IMMEDIATELY quotes Hosea's Lo-ammi reversal (Rom 9:25-26 = Hosea 2:23 / 1:10), not Isaiah 56. His choice of Hosea is his own internal signal: "the Gentiles" here = Lo-ammi scattered northern Israelites living among the nations.

L3: Rom 9:27-29 returns immediately to "children of Israel" and Isaiah's remnant. So the "Gentile" vessels of mercy sit inside an entirely Israel-framed argument, bookended on both sides by Israel language.

Conclusion: Paul defines his own "Gentiles" by the proof text he chooses: Hosea's Lo-ammi. The note holds that definitional move rather than importing the later generic sense of "Gentile." [framework: Hellenistai/scattered-Israel distinction in view]
