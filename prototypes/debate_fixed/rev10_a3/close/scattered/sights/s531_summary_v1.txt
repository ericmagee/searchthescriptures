Paul writes to the Galatians: "if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise." The verb is present-tense indicative (este, "ye ARE"): it states a present standing as fact [floor-verified V-PAI-2P, fold 30, 2026-07-16]. The tense itself does not adjudicate whether that standing pre-existed and is activated or is conferred; the ACTIVATION reading (standing activated, not manufactured) is the HELD reading, carried at L3: supplied by the conditional structure (ei...ara) and the letter context, fold-held since 2026-06-09, floor-supported by fold 30. The same letter closes at 6:16 with "the Israel of God": the congregation Paul writes to, "the churches of Galatia" (1:2), the body of the church (Colossians 1:18; Ephesians 1:22-23; DG-1). Sperma holds its biological register throughout Galatians 3 (used for Christ at v.16, for Abraham's descendants at vv.7,19; N-NSN singular at v.29 against plural umeis; fold 30). THE HELD LINE: the Lo-ammi reading is HELD at L3; the grammar and letter context are consistent-with and supportive-of it, and the referent is named at the Romans 9:25-26 seats (Sights 35/552/566; Sight 659): the Galatians Paul writes to are scattered Israel, Abraham's seed counted by promise (DG-1; Conclusion #2). Serve the verb's claim as a present-standing fact; serve the activation reading at L3 held. Who and where they are today is the Shepherd's to name (DG-20).
