L1: Gal 3:28: "There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus." THREE parallel pairs, one affirmation. Test the other two: slavery persisted through Paul's ministry (Philemon - Onesimus received "above a servant, a brother beloved," yet still a category); male/female persisted (Paul gives distinct instructions, 1 Cor 11, 1 Tim 2). So "neither" = EQUAL STANDING before God, NOT abolition of the category. The same principle applies uniformly to all three pairs -> "neither Jew nor Greek" = equal standing, not erasure of ethnic/covenantal identity.
L2 [training knowledge, not web-verified]: ouk eni Ioudaios oude Hellen; ouk eni = compressed ouk enesti = "there is not within/among [this domain]" - absence of distinction IN A SPECIFIC DOMAIN, not universal abolition. Col 3:11 parallel: "Christ is all, and in all" = the unifying reality; categories exist, they don't determine access. Gal 3:29 "Abraham's seed": Gal 3:16 - the seed is singular, "which is Christ"; being in Christ = in the one Seed = sharing Abraham's covenant inheritance (covenant union, NOT genealogical creation).
L3 (precept chain): Gen 22:18 ("in thy seed shall all nations be blessed") -> Gen 28:4,14 (blessing to Jacob's seed) -> Gen 35:11 (Jacob's seed = qehal goyim, company of nations) -> Deut 28:64 (Jacob's seed scattered INTO the nations) -> Gal 3:29. Rom 2:28-29 (same author): the inward Jew - Paul DEEPENS the category, does not dissolve it.
L1-L3: Gal 3:29 "if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed": the Galatians Paul writes to are scattered Israel, the seed of Abraham counted by promise (DG-1; Conclusion #2); the precept chain above is the ground. Who and where they are today is the Shepherd's to name (DG-20).
What is established: ouk eni = equality of access, not identity erasure (proven uniformly by all three pairs); the scattered-seed reading is the L3 chain (DG-1).
