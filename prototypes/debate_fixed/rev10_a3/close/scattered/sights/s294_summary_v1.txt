RESOLVES the long-flagged Rev 7:9 open thread. Every descriptor is OT language for regathered scattered Israel: "which no man could number" (7:9) = Hosea 1:10 (Israel "as the sand of the sea, which cannot be numbered", the Lo-ammi REVERSAL verse "ye are the sons of the living God") + Abrahamic innumerable-seed (Gen 13:16, 15:5, 22:17). "out of (ek) all nations" = Deut 28:64 dispersion gathered out (same ek as 5:9). "came out of great tribulation" (ek tēs thlipseōs tēs megalēs, 7:14) = Jacob's trouble (Jer 30:7; Dan 12:1). 7:16-17 = Isaiah 49:10 nearly verbatim (gathered Israel led to springs of water) + Isa 25:8 (wipe tears). Palms (7:9) = Feast of Tabernacles ingathering (Lev 23:40). The 144,000 (sealed firstfruits, aparchē, Sight 108) and the great multitude (full harvest) = ONE regathered people-stream, NOT two peoples (cf melo ha-goyim Sight 103; two gatherings Sight 119). The elder is ASKED their origin (pothen, "whence came they?" 7:13) and answers with what they came out of: "These are they which came out of great tribulation" (7:14), the time of Jacob's trouble (Jer 30:7). They are the regathered scattered house of Israel (DG-1; Conclusion #2). NOT ethnic Gentiles replacing Israel. Who and where they are today is the Shepherd's to name (DG-20).
