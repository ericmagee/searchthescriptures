L1 THE STITCH (new find): Hos 1:10 has two clauses: (a) number of the children of Israel as the sand of the sea; (b) not-my-people → sons of the living God. Paul quotes clause (b) in Rom 9:26 attributed to Osee, then v.27 opens with Isaiah carrying clause (a) vocabulary (Isa 10:22, same words): Paul split Hosea 1:10 down its middle and cited its halves through two prophets, AND labels the second himself: Esaias ALSO crieth CONCERNING ISRAEL. Two linked citation formulas (As he saith ALSO in Osee / Esaias ALSO): if the Hosea half were ethnic Gentiles and the Isaiah half Israel, the also-chain across one split verse is incoherent; natural reading: both citations run on one subject, named inside Hos 1:10 itself: the children of Israel. L1 ADJACENCY: Paul quotes 1:10; the next verse (1:11) is the two-house gathering, Judah AND Israel together, one head, and Paul's sentence reproduces the structure: not of the Jews only but ALSO ex ethnōn: two groups, called together, one Lord. Ex ethnōn = out of the nations (location/state), precisely where the Lo-ammi house was SOWN (Hos 2:23 sow-her-in-the-earth, Sight 552 diaspora verb). L3 PARALLEL: 1 Pet 2:9-10 applies both reversed names to the diaspora (1:1) then distinguishes the audience FROM the Gentiles (2:12): apostolic application without re-referencing. COUNTERS FULL STRENGTH: Rom 9:30 ethnē sweep parses both ways: framework reading keeps one referent (scattered house attained by faith; Israel-after-the-law stumbled, v.31); standard reading possible but costs re-referencing a prophecy whose subject is named in the quoted verse's own first clause and makes the concerning-Israel stitch awkward. Rev 22:18 sits over re-referencing. || L2 (verified re-walk 2026-06-20): Rom 9:24 alla kai ex ethnon: ek = "out of," marking origin/location (the state where Lo-ammi was sown); citation spine is the kai-chain (kai ex ethnon v.24 / kai in Osee v.25 / Esaias also v.27), supporting one referent across the split Hosea verse. || FLOOR ROSTER (row-verified 2026-07-16, original_words Rom 9:24, 12 words): ou monon (G3756 PRT-N + G3440 ADV, 'not only') ex ioudaiōn (G1537 PREP + G2453 A-GPM, 'of the Jews') alla kai (G235 CONJ + G2532 CONJ, 'but also') ex ethnōn (G1537 PREP + G1484 N-GPN, 'of the Gentiles/nations'); the verb is ekalesen (G2564 V-AAI-3S, 'he called') with one object hēmas ('us'). The not-only/but-also structure with twin ex-phrases is ON ROWS: one called company drawn out of two locations/states. Deploy at this sight's held layer: the two-ex structure is L1-L2, and both groups are of Israel, named in the first clause of the verse Paul quotes, "the children of Israel" (Hosea 1:10; DG-1; Conclusion #2). Who and where they are today is the Shepherd's to name (DG-20).
