Ephesians 2:11-12 names the recipients' CONDITION, not an origin: "in time past Gentiles in the flesh," "without Christ," "aliens from the commonwealth of Israel," "strangers from the covenants of promise," "having no hope," "without God in the world." This is the condition of cast-off Israel living as not-a-people among the idol-nations (Hosea 1:9; "far off" = Isaiah 57:19 at Ephesians 2:17; 1 Corinthians 12:2 "carried away unto dumb idols"). Gentile here is a condition of an Israelite (BB-7870); the recipients are the scattered house (DG-1, resolved for the biblical record, BB-7858) [L3, held]. The heathen and the stranger keep their own words (Sight 298 categories 2-3). No blood verdict in either direction (DG-60); who and where the scattered are today is the Shepherd's (DG-20). In debate this is our held ground from the opening: it refutes "these ethnē are ethnic outsiders" (the reflex). No concede vocabulary in-round (DD-2).
