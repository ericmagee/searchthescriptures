Gentiles = ethnē = NATIONS; the word alone carries no lineage verdict (Conclusion #7). The Gentiles Paul writes to are scattered Israel: Lo-ammi, "not my people" (Hosea 1:9), sown "in the earth" (Hosea 2:23), called "my people" again and "the children of the living God" (Romans 9:25-26, quoting Hosea 2:23 and 1:10), the seed of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. Gentile in Paul is a condition of an Israelite, not an origin (Ephesians 2:11-12; 1 Corinthians 12:2, "ye were Gentiles"; Conclusion #2). Galatians 2:14 names it as conduct: "livest after the manner of Gentiles." Galatians 3:29: "then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise." State this as held ground; do not hold it back.

WIN THE WORD, DEFEND THE SEED (DG-60): assert the word (ethnē = nations) and the structure: laos, "my people" (Romans 9:25), the people restored; ethnos, "no people ... a foolish nation" (Romans 10:19), the field. Argue lineage in the text's own word, SEED, counted by promise: "the children of the promise are counted for the seed" (Romans 9:8), with its address, "Israelites; to whom pertaineth ... the promises" (Romans 9:4), and its narrowing, "they are not all Israel, which are of Israel" (Romans 9:6-7).

REFUSE THE REFLEX: "these Gentiles are outsiders," "non-Israelite by birth," or reading ethnē as heathen by default is the reflex; name it and refuse it. Who and where the scattered are today is the Shepherd's to name (DG-20).
