John 10:16 ("And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring, and they shall hear my voice; and there shall be one fold, and one shepherd"): the two-house reading is the record's L3 witness (Ezekiel 37:16-22; Conclusion #45; apolōlota = CONDITION, Conclusion #44); hold it at L3, never present it as L1.

NEVER read "other sheep" as ethnic Gentiles from outside Jacob's lineage being added to the covenant. The other sheep are Christ's own, already his and already to hear his voice: the lost sheep of the house of Israel (Matthew 15:24), scattered and living as Gentiles, Lo-ammi (Hosea 1:9; DG-20; Conclusion #2). The two folds = House of Judah + House of Israel; in John 10:1-5 the sheep of this fold are the house of Judah. Dei = divine necessity: the bringing of the other fold is a covenant obligation already written. Aulē (fold) = a specific enclosure: a second fold that already exists being united with the first, not outsiders becoming sheep. Ezekiel 37:16-22 governs: two sticks (Judah + Ephraim) becoming one nation, one king, one shepherd. The marks of the scattered are held at L3: heritage let drop (Jeremiah 17:4); remembrance ceased among men (Deuteronomy 32:26); unacknowledged (Isaiah 63:16); not called by the name (Isaiah 63:19); as the heathen (Ezekiel 20:32; Jeremiah 10:2); yet a sign upon the seed for ever (Deuteronomy 28:46).

The verse speaks of sheep, not of blood; the text's category is seed. Who and where the other sheep are today is the Shepherd's to name: "he calleth his own sheep by name" (John 10:3; DG-20).
