Matthew 28:19 "teach all nations" (panta ta ethnē): the WORD ethnē = NATIONS; it carries no lineage verdict (Conclusion #7, KJV floor). Guard against reopening this as universal ethnic-outsider inclusion by lexical default: the word is "nations," not "non-Israelite." God promised Israel himself "a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee" (Genesis 35:11, goy and goyim, the same word).

The commission reaching "all nations" converges with the Deuteronomy 28:64 scatter as an L3 cross-reference. STATUS: HELD (word at L1-L2, scope-convergence at L3). The nations the commission reaches are where Israel was scattered (Deuteronomy 28:64, "scatter thee among all people") and sown (Hosea 2:23, "I will sow her unto me in the earth"); who and where the scattered are today is the Shepherd's to name (DG-20).

DISCIPLINE: do not name who and where the scattered are today (DG-20); do not default the word to ethnic-outsider (DG-1).
