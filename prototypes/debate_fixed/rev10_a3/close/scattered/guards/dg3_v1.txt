Romans 11 olive TREE identity = Israel, both houses (Jeremiah 11:16-17, "the house of Israel and ... the house of Judah"; Conclusion #65): SOLID. The tree is not re-defined as a Gentile-church body: the root is Israel's ("thou bearest not the root, but the root thee," v.18), the address is instrumental ("my flesh," v.14), jealousy presupposes a prior claim (v.11), and v.24 calls it "their own olive tree."

THE GRAFT RESTORES. The wild branches are Lo-ammi Israel returning to their own covenant, carried by the jealousy chain (Deuteronomy 32:21 "not a people," quoted at Romans 10:19; Romans 9:24-26, quoting Hosea 2:23 and 1:10): standing suspended, never terminated. The text does its own counting: "the children of the promise are counted for the seed" (Romans 9:8). THE STRANGER IS NOT SHOWN TO BE AN OUTSIDER: the stranger who joins himself to the LORD (Isaiah 56:3, 6) is not named by origin, and the text puts the stranger-words on Israel itself ("ye were strangers in the land of Egypt," Exodus 22:21; "an alien unto my mother's children," Psalm 69:8; "his seed should sojourn in a strange land," Acts 7:6; the patriarchs "strangers and pilgrims," Hebrews 11:13). Do not set the joining stranger against the grafted branch as two peoples. Paul names the branches' horticultural status (agrielaios), and Ephesians 2:3 shows physis can name an acquired condition. CITATION CAUTION: do not rest the graft on Romans 11:24's possessive; the jealousy chain is the strong ground. Hold: TREE = Israel (solid); GRAFT = the returning house, in the covenant word; the text's category is seed.
