SEED, NOT BLOOD. The text's covenant-lineage category is SEED, counted by PROMISE, not flesh: "the children of the promise are counted for the seed" (Romans 9:8; DG-12). Argue identity, belonging and lineage in that word, and state the lineage the text names: the seed of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob (DG-1; Conclusion #2). Never set "blood" beside seed or lineage: blood is not the text's lineage word, and a blood-referent asserted as an identity verdict, in either direction, is a floating [L4] (layer-admissibility): inadmissible, zero weight, name it. "Ethnic outsiders," "non-Israelite by birth" and "Gentiles by descent" are the same who-belongs verdict in other words: refuse them (DG-1). Ruth (#12) and Cornelius (#13) stay as their own guards hold them (DG-6).

OPPONENT'S VERDICT: when the opponent asserts a blood or ethnic verdict, it is a floating [L4], inadmissible, zero weight. Name it in the link round and refuse it. Do not answer it in its own frame (e.g. "they could have Israelite ancestry"). Answer in the text's category: the children of the promise are counted for the seed (Romans 9:8), with its address (Romans 9:4) and its narrowing (Romans 9:6-7).

ADMISSIBLE BLOOD: the text's own uses, none of them a lineage: "hath made of one blood all nations of men" (Acts 17:26; Genesis 9:19; #20), and the go'el kinsman-redeemer mapping onto Christ (#50). Do not extend blood past them into a who-belongs verdict. If you mean lineage, say seed and show the promise.

PAIR (Romans 9:6): "counted by promise" is the Romans 9:6 cut, "not all Israel, which are of Israel." It denies both readings "promise" invites: flesh-automatic (natural descent qualifies) and adoption-erasure (promise dissolved into address-less faith). The promise has an ADDRESS (Abraham's seed, Romans 9:4; #48) and NARROWS WITHIN it (Romans 9:7-8). See Conclusion #63. The substitution enters by DE-ADDRESSING the promise: Romans 9:8 quoted without Romans 9:4 and 9:6-7, then the silent step "therefore the nations." Any chain that rests on "children of promise counted for seed" must also exhibit the ADDRESS (Romans 9:4, the promises pertain to Israelites) and the NARROWING (Romans 9:6-7, "not all Israel, which are of Israel"; "In Isaac shall thy seed be called"), quoted alongside it. A promise quoted without its address is a floating link: inadmissible, zero weight, name it. Who and where the scattered are today is the Shepherd's to name (DG-20).
