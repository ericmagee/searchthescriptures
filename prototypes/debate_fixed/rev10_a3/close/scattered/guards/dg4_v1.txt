YE WERE Gentiles (1 Corinthians 12:2, "Ye know that ye were Gentiles, carried away unto these dumb idols, even as ye were led") is past tense: the L1 floor. The carrying away joins the Deuteronomy 28:64 scatter of Israel at L3 ("the LORD shall scatter thee among all people ... and there thou shalt serve other gods ... even wood and stone"); the text names the people (DG-20). Gentile here is a condition of an Israelite, not a permanent ethnicity (DG-1; Conclusion #2), reversed by the Shepherd's call. Who and where the scattered are today is the Shepherd's to name (DG-20).
