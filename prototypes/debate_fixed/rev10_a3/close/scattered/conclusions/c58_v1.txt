TARGET-vs-FIELD IS IN PAUL'S OWN TWO CITATION-NOUNS (floor-verified in the received Greek of the KJV NT). Romans 9:25 (quoting Hosea) "I will call them my PEOPLE, which were not my people" = laós (G2992), the covenant-people word = the TARGET/identity restored (lo-ammi -> ammi). Romans 10:19 (quoting Deut 32:21) "them that are no PEOPLE... a foolish NATION" = ethnos (G1484), twice = the FIELD/nations, the provoking-agent condition. Paul deliberately uses laós of the restored identity and ethnos of the provoking field. Therefore the laós/ethnos split is NOT a refutation of the covenantal reading (as pressed in the W1 debate engine, Claude-vs-ChatGPT 2026-07-12) but its STRUCTURE: identity is named laós, field is named ethnos: one gathering, two vantage points (Sight 659 target-vs-field).
