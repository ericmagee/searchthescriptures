In Romans 11:13 "I am the apostle of the Gentiles," ethnos means NATIONS, not ethnic outsiders, and Scripture stamps that word on Israel's own seed (Genesis 35:11 "a company of nations"; Genesis 48:19 melo ha-goyim, Ephraim's seed "a multitude of nations"). The stranger-words are not racial terms either: the text puts zar and nokri on an Israelite ("I am become a stranger unto my brethren, and an alien unto my mother's children," Psalm 69:8; DG-3). The Gentiles Paul addresses are scattered Israel (DG-1; Conclusion #2). v.13 addresses "you Gentiles"; v.14 names the ones provoked, "them which are my flesh" (the jealousy chain, Conclusion #65). The wild branches (Romans 11:17, 24) are Lo-ammi northern Israel returning to their own covenant, carried by the jealousy chain (Deuteronomy 32:21 lo-am quoted at Romans 10:19; Romans 9:24-26, quoting Hosea 2:23 and 1:10; Conclusion #65), not by v.24's possessive: "their own olive tree" (idian) is said of the natural branches, and the wild branch is graffed "into a good olive tree" (DG-3, citation caution). The v.24 descriptor "wild by nature" stays L4-consistent, not L1-asserted (Sight 511).
