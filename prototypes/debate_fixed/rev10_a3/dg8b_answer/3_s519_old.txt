(b) narrating past the rows: "no man" or "without sex" as stated fact, any detail of act, time or place past what the rows require, or "of the Holy Ghost" read as paternity (DG-18) or cut from its row (DG-59). The seed rows above require seed out of a man's body, and the canon's stated means of begetting is knowing ("Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived," Gen 4:1); Joseph and Mary were husband and wife in law from the espousal ("Joseph her husband," Matt 1:19; "Mary thy wife," Matt 1:20; "his neighbour's wife," Deut 22:24), and "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son" (Matt 1:25) bounds the abstinence to the pregnancy. So the record holds, at L4 footed on those rows, that Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal (Sight 32).
