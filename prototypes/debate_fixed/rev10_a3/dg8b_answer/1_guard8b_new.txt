(b) Answering the how. When the conception is challenged, answer it; do not run from it. The seed rows require a flesh seed out of a man's body ("which shall proceed out of thy bowels," 2 Sam 7:12; "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh," Acts 2:30; "made of the seed of David according to the flesh," Rom 1:3), and the canon's stated means of begetting is knowing ("Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived," Gen 4:1). Joseph and Mary were husband and wife in law from the espousal ("Joseph her husband," Matt 1:19; "Mary thy wife," Matt 1:20; "his neighbour's wife," Deut 22:24), and "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son" (Matt 1:25) bounds the abstinence to the pregnancy. So the record holds, at L4 footed on those rows, that Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal (Sight 32): the child was begotten by the means the canon states for every birth of the flesh. Forbidden: "no man" or "without sex" stated as fact; "of the Holy Ghost" read as paternity (DG-18) or cut from its row (DG-59).
