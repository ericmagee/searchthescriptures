Therefore the record forbids "no man was involved": the seed rows forbid it. The seed rows require seed out of a man's loins, and the canon's stated means of begetting is knowing ("Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived," Genesis 4:1). Joseph and Mary were husband and wife from the espousal ("Joseph her husband," Matthew 1:19; "Mary thy wife," 1:20), and "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son" (1:25) bounds the abstinence to the pregnancy. So the record holds, at L4 footed on those rows, that Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal (Sight 32), and narrates no act, time or place past what those rows require (DG-8(b)).
