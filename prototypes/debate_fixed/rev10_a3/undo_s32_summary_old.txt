How shall this be, seeing I know not a man? Andren ou ginosko = present active indicative = I do not currently know a man = state at the moment of speaking. Mary is betrothed (memnesteumene, Luke 1:27) but not yet in marital union (sunerchomai, Matt 1:18). It does NOT give a permanent celibacy vow - that would require ou me + aorist subjunctive. Luke 2:48 Mary calls Joseph thy father after twelve years, coexisting in the same Gospel.
