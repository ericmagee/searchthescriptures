RESOLVED 2026-06-22 (precept upon precept, Isa 28:10): the Davidic seed is honored THROUGH the named line — Joseph. A man WAS involved; the record never suggests Mary had a baby without a man.

FATHER — JOSEPH, named: "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing" (Luke 2:48); "Is not this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know?" (John 6:42); "Joseph, thou son of David" (Matt 1:20); Joseph names the child (Matt 1:21,25).

SEED — required and honored through that line, in the words of the text: "which shall proceed out of thy bowels" (2 Sam 7:12); "Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne" (Ps 132:11); "made of the seed of David according to the flesh" (Rom 1:3); "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ" (Acts 2:30); "it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda" (Heb 7:14). A real seed proceeding out of the body, EVIDENT, cannot stand with no man. Legal adoption produces no seed (Sight 2).

SPIRIT — not the biological begetter: "that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost" (Matt 1:20; cf. 1:18) = covenant-origin marker, not paternity (DG-18; Sight 10).

MATT 1:16 PASSIVE — "of whom was born Jesus" — is non-narration, not a counter-witness; do not enthrone the one passive verb against the chorus (journal 373).

FORBIDDEN both directions: (a) suggesting Mary had a baby without a man — "no human father," "without Joseph," the Holy Ghost as the conception, or routing the flesh through Mary alone (her Davidic descent not established, Sight 6); (b) narrating past the rows: "no man" or "without sex" as stated fact, any detail of act, time or place past what the rows require, or "of the Holy Ghost" read as paternity (DG-18) or cut from its row (DG-59). The seed rows above require seed out of a man's body, and the canon's stated means of begetting is knowing ("Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived," Gen 4:1); Joseph and Mary were husband and wife in law from the espousal ("Joseph her husband," Matt 1:19; "Mary thy wife," Matt 1:20; "his neighbour's wife," Deut 22:24), and "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son" (Matt 1:25) bounds the abstinence to the pregnancy. So the record holds, at L4 footed on those rows, that Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal (Sight 32). Christ had a biological father (DG-8).

Governing: DG-8.
