FLOOR STATEMENT AND THE RECORD'S RULED SENTENCE: the seed is David's according to the flesh, out of loins and bowels; the father is named in four registers; reckoning is by the father's house and the register was struck for the wife's; "Son of God" is a covenant and resurrection title; the angel's verbs are empowerment and presence; the curse is uncited, answered, bounded, and not escaped by Luke 3; Joseph's row is intent; "made of a woman" is mortality. Therefore the record forbids "no man was involved": the seed rows forbid it. The seed rows require seed out of a man's loins, and the canon's stated means of begetting is knowing ("Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived," Genesis 4:1). Joseph and Mary were husband and wife from the espousal ("Joseph her husband," Matthew 1:19; "Mary thy wife," 1:20), and "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son" (1:25) bounds the abstinence to the pregnancy. So the record holds, at L4 footed on those rows, that Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal (Sight 32), and narrates no act, time or place past what those rows require (DG-8(b)). The dogma's four pillars measure as: two rows read at the word level (Matthew 1:19, intent, not knowledge; Luke 1:34, present indicative), one title read against its own canon, and one trap the canon has already sprung.
