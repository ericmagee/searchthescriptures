RESOLVED 2026-06-22 (precept upon precept / Isaiah 28:10): the Davidic seed is honored THROUGH the named line — Joseph. A man WAS involved. Nothing here may suggest Mary had a baby without a man. Hold every plain witness; never enthrone one passive verb against the chorus.

FATHER — JOSEPH, the named biological Davidic father. Mary own mouth: "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing" (Luke 2:48). The public: "Is not this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know?" (John 6:42; cf. John 1:45; Luke 4:22). The angel: "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife" (Matt 1:20). Joseph names the child (Matt 1:21,25). The father is NAMED, and a man was involved.

SEED — REQUIRED and HONORED THROUGH THAT LINE, in the words of the text: "I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels" (2 Sam 7:12); "Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne" (Ps 132:11); "made of the seed of David according to the flesh" (Rom 1:3); "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne" (Acts 2:30); "Jesus Christ of the seed of David" (2 Tim 2:8); "it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda" (Heb 7:14); "The sceptre shall not depart from Judah" (Gen 49:10). A real seed proceeding out of the body, according to the flesh, EVIDENT — cannot stand with no man. Legal adoption does not produce seed (Sight 2).

SPIRIT — NOT the biological begetter: "that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost" (Matt 1:20; cf. 1:18) is the covenant-origin / anointing marker, not paternity (DG-18; Sight 10).

THE MATT 1:16 PASSIVE — "of whom was born Jesus" — is non-narration, NOT a counter-witness. It does not say "no man"; it simply does not narrate the begetting. Precept upon precept (Isa 28:10): the rest of the witnesses fill what that one verse leaves quiet — the seed is honored through the named line. Enthroning that lone passive as doubt about whether a man was involved is the reflex (journal 373).

FORBIDDEN — both directions:
(a) Suggesting Mary had a baby WITHOUT A MAN: "no human father," "no biological father," "without Joseph," virgin-birth-as-no-human-father, the Holy Ghost as the biological conception, or routing the flesh through Mary alone (her Davidic descent is not established, Sight 6).
(b) Narrating past the rows. The seed rows require a flesh seed out of a man's body ("which shall proceed out of thy bowels," 2 Sam 7:12; "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh," Acts 2:30; "made of the seed of David according to the flesh," Rom 1:3), and the canon's stated means of begetting is knowing ("Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived," Gen 4:1). Joseph and Mary were husband and wife in law from the espousal ("Joseph her husband," Matt 1:19; "Mary thy wife," Matt 1:20; "his neighbour's wife," Deut 22:24), and "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son" (Matt 1:25) bounds the abstinence to the pregnancy. So the record holds, at L4 footed on those rows, that Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal (Sight 32). Forbidden: "no man" or "without sex" stated as fact; any detail of act, time or place past what those rows require; "of the Holy Ghost" read as paternity (DG-18) or cut from its row (DG-59).
Also forbidden: resolving the genealogy divergences by any construct the text does not state (DG-56).
