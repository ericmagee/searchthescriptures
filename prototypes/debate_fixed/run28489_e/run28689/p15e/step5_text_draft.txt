THE DEBATE FIX: what the judges are told, and how verdicts are counted

Measured first: since 17 October, 31 debates on whether Christ had a biological father; 8 ended UNDERDETERMINED, 5 of the last 9.

Item 1. Judges may not keep a point standing because no single verse states the opposite.
 Measure: in all 8 UNDERDETERMINED debates, a counted judge kept points standing or open on the ground that "no verse states" the other side's claim, in both directions, 33 lines in all.
 New wording for the judges: "A link marked STANDS, or a collision graded UNDERDETERMINED, against a witness the other side exhibited names in the same line the round that answered that witness. A witness left unanswered is a BROKEN ground. That a single verse does not state a link or its contrary is never a ground for STANDS or UNDERDETERMINED, for either side."
 A new check sends any such line without an answering round back to the judge to regrade.
 Yes or no.

Item 2. A point a side concedes in its own closing round counts as withdrawn.
 Measure: in today's live debate two judges counted the defender's conceded point as standing; the system flagged it but changed nothing.
 Change: the conceded point counts as withdrawn and the judge's verdict is recomputed from its own counts.
 Effect on today's debate, from the stored judgments: the outcome becomes DEFENDER PREVAILS, WEAKENED. The defender in that debate argued that Christ had no human father, the reading the record forbids.
 Yes or no.

Item 3. When the correction step removes a judge's BROKEN line, the judge regrades it with its reason.
 Measure: in 7 judgments the judge wrote that the challenger wins; the correction step removed those lines and the verdict was counted as held. In one debate all three judges had written that the challenger wins.
 New wording for the correction step: "A BROKEN line you retract is regraded in the same reply: UNDERDETERMINED only with the round that answered each witness named; a witness left unanswered keeps the line BROKEN in the corrected form."
 Yes or no.

Item 4. The rule that turns a held verdict into UNDERDETERMINED is not changed in this round.
 Measure: every counted UNDERDETERMINED judgment held the defender's chain while finding the challenger's chain fully supported; the judges' rule then writes UNDERDETERMINED.
 Reason: turning those cases into DEFENDER WINS would award the reading the record forbids. Items 1 to 3 are tested first on the stored debates; if any still ends UNDERDETERMINED, this item returns to you with those debates.
 Yes or no.

Item 5. The judges are not given the drift guards.
 Measure: the judges are given no guard text today; the single-verse rule reaches them only as the chain rule in their instructions.
 Reason: the judges grade the debate, not the doctrine (the earlier ruling that guards are for the debaters).
 Yes or no.

 (a) The chain description that marks a link "floating" uses the same test as the rest of the instructions: "a link floats only when the block that states it exhibits fewer than two prior layers; the mark names the layers exhibited and the layer lacking."
 (b) An error of mine in this morning's rule: "a verse held to contradict it is quoted in the words that state the contrary" becomes "the witnesses held to contradict it are quoted, and they contradict it read together, line upon line".
