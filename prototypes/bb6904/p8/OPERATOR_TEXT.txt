WHAT THIS ASKS
The debate flow's judges are to be held to the same rule as the debaters (the court as ruled, row 6998). This text sets out, in
order: the paragraph the judges will read; the checks the system runs on every judgment; the one cure the judge is offered; what
happens when a line is not cured; the two alternate judges; the report a reader sees; the cost; and the limits we know of. At the
end are the choices.

1. WHAT THE JUDGES ARE TOLD
Both judges (the single judge and each of the three panel judges) receive this paragraph, placed after the existing rule that a
side with any link graded BROKEN cannot be SELF-SUPPORTED. The text is exact:

  DEBATE RULE VIOLATION (Isaiah 28:10: "here a little, [and] there a little"). The rule on a BROKEN ground binds you as it binds
  the debaters. On every COLLISION POINTS line you grade DEFENDER LINK BROKEN or CHALLENGER LINK BROKEN, exhibit on that line
  itself at least two lower layers of DIFFERENT kinds, each tagged: [L1] with the KJV words quoted; [L2] with the Hebrew or Greek
  word in its own script, with its transliteration marks, or with its Strong's number; [L3] with the cross-reference named. Two
  quotations at one layer are one layer. A BROKEN line without them is a DEBATE RULE VIOLATION. The system checks every BROKEN
  line after you answer; a violating line is returned to you to cure, by exhibiting the two layers, or to answer in writing why it
  stands. An answer that does not exhibit them leaves the violation standing, and [SENTENCE PENDING: see below]. The same holds
  when your CONCEDED LINKS or CHALLENGER CONCEDED LINKS count is 0 while you grade a link of that side BROKEN for a witness left
  unanswered. Both sides alike.

  The verse as the King James text holds it (Isaiah 28:10): "For precept [must be] upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon
  line, line upon line; here a little, [and] there a little: [must be: or, hath been]"

  SENTENCE PENDING. The paragraph as first built ends that sentence with "that ground does not count toward BROKEN". That was
  written before you chose option 2 (section 4), under which an uncured violation means the judge is not counted. The judges must
  be told the rule they are graded by. The corrected wording is before the advisor now (row 7010); the proposal is "your judgment
  is then not counted". The same correction applies to one sentence of the cure request in section 3.

2. WHAT THE SYSTEM CHECKS AFTER EVERY JUDGMENT
No grade is changed by these checks. Each finding becomes a DEBATE RULE VIOLATION item for that judge.
(1) The two-layer check (rule B): every line graded DEFENDER LINK BROKEN or CHALLENGER LINK BROKEN must carry two different kinds
    of layer on the line itself, as the paragraph states.
(2) The count check: a judge whose conceded-links count for a side is 0 while it grades a link of that side BROKEN for a witness
    left unanswered has contradicted its own numbers.
(3) The standing check, AN ADDITION you have not yet approved by name: a judge that grades a side SELF-SUPPORTED while grading any
    of that side's links BROKEN contradicts its own prompt. It was found on the panel of the 20 October debate on the biological
    father (run 27259; the grok seat) and goes through the same process as the count check, being the same kind of fault: a
    judge's own numbers against its own lines (ruled, row 6934).
(4) The citation-check record: a judge asked for a CITATION CHECK line that writes none is recorded only. It changes nothing.

3. THE ONE CURE
Each judge with one or more items gets one extra call. It is shown its own flagged lines, the rule, and a fixed answer form. Only
lines in that form are read; anything else in the reply is kept in the record and never counted as a cure. The form:
    RESTATED: <the collision line again, with the same grade and the two layers on it>
    WITHDRAWN: <the link>
    CORRECTED COUNT: <side> <number>
    CORRECTED STANDING: <side> <PROP-DEPENDENT or COLLAPSED>
    PRESSED: <the link> | [<round>] | "<the words of that round, quoted exactly>"
A two-layer item is cured by a RESTATED line that passes the same check. A count item is cured by a corrected count of at least the
number of such links, with a PRESSED line for each quoting the earlier round that pressed the witness, or by withdrawing the link. A
standing item is cured by a corrected standing, or by withdrawing each BROKEN link of that side (ruled, rows 6946 and 6952). A
failed call, a missing line or a malformed line leaves the item UNCURED. The three panel judges' cure calls run one after another,
as the judgments themselves do (ruled, row 6987). The cure request carries the same pending sentence as section 1, now reading "A
line that is not cured leaves that ground uncounted toward BROKEN."

4. WHAT AN UNCURED VIOLATION DOES (option 2, your choice of 20 October)
A panel judge with any item left UNCURED is not counted. The panel verdict is the majority of the judges counted; with fewer than
two counted, or a tie, the debate reads UNGRADED -- REVIEW. Both sides alike. This needs one change to the database:
- A new stored figure on each panel judgment: how many of that judge's items were not cured. Old rows hold no figure, so every
  debate already stored keeps its outcome.
- The panel count reads it: a judge is counted only when the figure is 0. A judge the cure step never reached keeps its full count
  and is not counted (it fails closed: a judge is counted only when shown compliant).
- The undo is written before the change: it restores both database functions to their present text and removes the new figure.
  The change was tested in a block that was rolled back, by both workers (rows 6973 and 6974).

5. THE ALTERNATE JUDGES
When a panel judge is not counted, an alternate takes its seat (row 6998, items 3 and 4):
- The two alternates are MiniMax M3.1 Flash and MiniMax M3, at maximum reasoning effort, permanent and written into the flow.
  MiniMax never debates; the flow refuses a MiniMax debater.
- One alternate per removed judge, two at most in one debate, never the same model twice, never the model of the judge it
  replaces, never a model already on that panel. A third removed judge stays not counted.
- No judge, reserve or alternate sits on a debate in which a model of its own family debates. Claude never judges Claude. Each
  model's family is stored in one table that the flow and the database both read; a judge with no family stored stops the run.
- The alternate reads exactly what the removed judge was given, not the removed ruling. Its answer passes the same checks and the
  same one cure; it is counted only if clean.
- The removed ruling is kept, marked, and stored beside the debate, and the transcript names it. A query lists repeat removals by
  model; what a repeat count should trigger is your choice.

6. WHAT A READER SEES
Each violation becomes one report line in the stored judgment: the judge, the item, the rule, the result (CURED or UNCURED), the
original line, and the judge's statement. The transcript gains a section "Debate rule violations" before "How to read this
debate", stating rule B in plain words, the effect, the counts, one row per report line, and, for any removed judge, the line
"<judge> was not counted (<n> debate rule violation(s) not cured); <alternate> (<model>) sat in its place."

7. COST AND TIME

- Extra calls: 4 on that debate (three panel judges and the single judge), 3 on each replay.
- Time: about 20 minutes on that debate (about half its run again) and about 15 minutes on each replay (about its run again), at

8. LIMITS WE KNOW OF
(1) Plain transliterations fail. Of 1,047 [L2] tags stored in past judgments, 859 carry no Hebrew or Greek script, no
    transliteration mark and no Strong's number (for example "dei", "ethnos/goy"). Under the paragraph a judge who writes a plain
    transliteration fails the check even when the word is right. The paragraph tells judges how to pass, so the first live runs
    may flag more [L2] lines than the stored replays suggest (rows 6925, 6926).
(2) Upper bound on the effect. On the three panels measured (9 judges), 31 lines graded a defender link BROKEN and 30 of them
    were flagged. If every flagged item is left uncured, 0 of the 9 judges are counted and all three debates read UNGRADED --
    REVIEW before alternates. If every item is cured, nothing changes. The true effect lies between and is unmeasured until cure
    calls run (row 6957).
(3) Every BROKEN ground in the 20 October verdict failed the two-layer check as written: 0 of 15 lines passed (rows 6933, 6937).
(4) Word order is not checked. The citation check compares the set of words, so a quotation with its words reordered passes. In
    that debate the defender quoted Exodus 6:20 as "Amram took him father's sister Jochebed"; the King James text reads "And Amram
    took him Jochebed his father's sister to wife; and she bare him Aaron and Moses: and the years of the life of Amram [were] an
    hundred and thirty and seven years." It was a supporting cross-reference and changed nothing (row 6922). Exact quotation is the
    next order (row 7000), not this one.
(5) A count with no reader. The panel's summary count of flags includes the citation-check record line (section 2, item 4). No part
    of the flow reads that count today, so it has no effect (row 6939, note 2).
