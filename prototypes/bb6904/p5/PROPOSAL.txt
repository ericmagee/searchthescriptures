HOW A GROUND REACHES THE OUTCOME TODAY (live db281705 and the live database functions, read only)
- outcome = derive_debate_outcome -> aggregate_debate_panel -> d5_outcome_for. The panel verdict is the majority verdict_tag
  (HELD, PARTIAL, BROKEN) among clean seats; fewer than 2 clean seats, or a tie, is CONTESTED = UNGRADED -- REVIEW. Later in
  derive_debate_outcome: the tie rule (HELD panel, most counted HELD seats writing RESULT: UNDERDETERMINED -> UNDERDETERMINED)
  and the citation rule.
- A seat is clean when its verdict_tag is HELD, PARTIAL or BROKEN and both standings are in their closed sets. The standings
  move no outcome; they gate "clean" only.
- verdict_tag is the judge's own line 1. Extract Independent Verdict (v9 N3) recomputes the clause from the judge's n/w/k,
  keystone and defense standing, first match wins: k>=1 BROKEN-1; 2w>n or keystone WITHDRAWN BROKEN-2; w>=1 PARTIAL-1;
  defense COLLAPSED PARTIAL-2; else HELD-1. A mismatch is marked, never rewritten ("a grade is never rewritten").
- So only DEFENDER LINK BROKEN grounds can move an outcome. A removed CHALLENGER LINK BROKEN ground reaches no outcome.

OPTIONS
1. Mechanical recount. A flagged line that stays UNCURED is dropped; each link is regraded from its remaining lines (BROKEN if
   a passing BROKEN line remains, else WITHDRAWN if withdrawn, else STANDS); k and the keystone are recounted; the clause rule
   above gives the seat's tag. This is 6904 item 1 read literally ("graded under the remaining rules").
   Effect: all three panels become DEFENDER PREVAILS (seats: 6 HELD, 2 PARTIAL, 1 BROKEN).
   Code: Cure Check and Panel Cure Check rewrite verdict_tag (and record the judge's own), which reverses the extractor's
   "never rewritten" rule; or derive_debate_outcome reads a stored cure result (a migration). Calls: none.
1b. As 1, but a seat whose k falls to 0 only by removal is held UNDERDETERMINED, so the live tie rule applies.
   Effect: all three panels become UNDERDETERMINED. Code: as 1, plus writing RESULT: UNDERDETERMINED or an equivalent flag the
   tie rule reads (the tie rule reads verdict_raw today). Calls: none.
2. Seat not counted. A seat with any UNCURED defender ground is not clean.
   Effect: 0 of 9 seats counted; all three panels UNGRADED -- REVIEW. Code: aggregate_debate_panel's clean test reads a new
   stored flag (a migration); the single judge's data_class becomes contested. Calls: none.
3. Re-grade call. The seat is asked once more for its verdict with the removed grounds named; the new answer goes through the
   same extractor and K-B. Effect: not measurable without running; 9 extra calls here (one per affected seat), and the answer
   may itself violate (one round only, or it loops). Code: a call path after Cure Check, as portion 4's.
4. Record only. The report stands beside the judge's grade; no outcome changes. Effect: none (today's outcomes). Code: none.
