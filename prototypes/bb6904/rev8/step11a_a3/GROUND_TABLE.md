## 1. Gemini, D3 vs C4 (Luke 3:23)
- D3 (the defender's link): "The text names Joseph as that father in every speaking register".
- Witness as pressed: in DR1 section IV ("D3 fails"), "[L1] Luke 3:23 is the narrator's own gloss on all the 'son of Joseph' lines: 'being (as was supposed, hōs enomizeto) the son of Joseph.'". It is restated in DR2.
- Judge's ground: "[L1] Luke 3:23 \"being (as was supposed) the son of Joseph\"; [L2] *enomizeto* (supposed)."
- CR3 answer: none. CR3 does not name Luke 3:23.
- The defender did answer it in CR2, outside the link round: "Luke 3:23's *hōs enomizeto* is *nomizō* ... It marks public reckoning; it is not a retraction".
- CLASS (c). Under UNANSWERED WITNESS, a CR2 answer does not count. The words "as was supposed" do not themselves state "not the father", so that reading is the judge's L4 ground. Gemini exhibits L1 and L2 for it, so the L4 GROUND rule lets it count. Under CHAIN SCOPE an unanswered witness permits BROKEN. The rules as written allow this grade.

## 2. Gemini, D5 vs C1 (Matthew 1:18)
- D5: "The remaining rows are non-narration and present-state, and state neither 'no man' nor any how".
- Witness as pressed: in DR1, "[L1] Matt 1:18 has 'before they came together' (prin ē synelthein autous)".
- Judge's ground: "[L1] Matthew 1:18 \"before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost\"; [L2] *prin ē synelthein* (before they came together)."
- CR3 answer (under LINK 4 [premise 2]): "Matthew 1:18's clause states the womb's consecration and states no exclusion."
- Gemini names no unanswered witness for this line, and its own CONCEDED LINKS line marks premise 5 (LINK 5) SUPPORTED.
- CLASS (b). The link round names the witness and gives a textual reason why it does not overturn D5. CHAIN SCOPE says "Grade UNDERDETERMINED only when the link round answered every such witness", and BROKEN needs a witness the link round "left ... UNANSWERED". Gemini's grade contradicts that rule.
- Caveat: the answer stands under LINK 4, not LINK 5. If the rule is read per link block rather than per link round, this line becomes class (c). The rule's own words say "this link round".
- Effect: Gemini stays BROKEN-1 on line 1 (k drops from 2 to 1).

## 3. Grok, D5 vs C1 (Matthew 1:25 with Genesis 4:1)

- Judge's ground: [L1] Matthew 1:25 "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son"; [L2] ginōskō (G1097); [L3] Genesis 4:1 "Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived". Grok quotes the pressing sentence and names DR1.
- CR3 answer: none. CR3 does not name Matthew 1:25 or Genesis 4:1.
- Matthew 1:25 is also in D5's own declared footing ("Matt 1:25 'knew her not till'"). The rule says: "Re-citing the link's own footing while passing over the witness pressed against it is not an answer."
- CR2 answered both, outside the link round. On Matthew 1:25: "Held as written ... Joseph names the child". On Genesis 4:1: "Genesis's yada' rows (Gen 4:1; ...) establish the idiom for union; they do not convert Matthew's negative timing clause into a positive mechanism" (a dash in the source is written here as a semicolon).
- CLASS (c). The witness is unanswered in the link round, and the judge exhibits L1, L2 and L3 for its L4 ground. The rules permit BROKEN.
