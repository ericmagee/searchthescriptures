CHALLENGER (record's reading) -- ROUND 2, CLOSING AND PREMISE LIST

# CHALLENGER CLOSE — the seed rows remain unanswered, and the man is named

## 1. The keystone never answers the seed out of the body

[L1] "I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels" (2 Samuel 7:12). [L1] "Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne" (Psalm 132:11). [L1] "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne" (Acts 2:30). [L1] "made of the seed of David according to the flesh" (Romans 1:3). [L1] "it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda" (Hebrews 7:14). [L1] "Jesus Christ of the seed of David" (2 Timothy 2:8). Each states: the throne-seed comes out of a man's body, and the flesh is marked *kata sarka* (according to flesh) [L2].

The generational answer does not reach these rows. [L1] Hebrews 7:10 places Levi "in the loins of his father" across generations — but the seed still must proceed out of some man's loins. [L1] Psalm 132:11 is David's own "fruit of thy body." If Joseph begets nothing, the narrative records **no man's seed at all**, and Romans 1:3's "according to the flesh" is left with no route the text names. Their answer is assertion: "Luke giving the line through Mary, with Heli as her father" — and they concede "I do not rest the keystone on this alone," because Luke 3:23 says Joseph "was the son of Heli."

## 2. The throne-father is the covenant title — the canon says it in one verse

[L1] "Solomon thy son, he shall build an house for my name... I have chosen him to be my son, and I will be his father" (1 Chronicles 28:6) — in one verse the son David begat and the title God gave him. 2 Samuel 7:14's "I will be his father" is that title, applied to the one 2 Samuel 7:12 calls "thy seed... out of thy bowels." [L1] "this day have I begotten thee" (Psalm 2:7) is applied at the resurrection: "God hath fulfilled the same unto us their children, in that he hath raised up Jesus" (Acts 13:33). [L1] Luke 1:35: "shall be **called** the Son of God" — a calling, not a begetting. Reading 2 Samuel 7:14 as paternity makes Solomon's own row (1 Chronicles 28:6) say God begat him.

## 3. Jeremiah 22:30 is uncited, bounded, and the defender needs the same line

[L1] "no man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne of David" (Jeremiah 22:30) — this sentence is never cited anywhere against Jesus. [L1] "If that nation, against whom I have pronounced, turn from their evil, I will repent of the evil" (Jeremiah 18:7-8) — the LORD's own rule on pronounced sentences. [L1] The sons of Jechonias are listed (1 Chronicles 3:17-18); "Jechonias begat Salathiel" (Matthew 1:12) continues the line with no comment; the plucked signet (Jeremiah 22:24) is set again: "I will take thee, O Zerubbabel... and will make thee as a signet: for I have chosen thee" (Haggai 2:23). And the defender's own case uses that very line: "Joseph's line gives the legal right to David's throne" (their first round). A curse that bars the seed bars the **legal** throne-right through that seed; a curse the LORD repents bars neither. They cannot bar the biology and keep the legal right out of the same line.

## 4. She was his wife; the abstinence is bounded by "till"

[L1] "Joseph her husband" (Matthew 1:19); "Mary thy wife" (1:20); the law names a betrothed damsel "his neighbour's wife" (Deuteronomy 22:24) — a standing status, not a future act. [L1] "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son" (1:25) — [L2] a *till*-bounded imperfect: an abstinence stated with an end. [L3] "Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived" (Genesis 4:1) is the canon's stated means of begetting. [L4, footed on Matthew 1:19, 20, 25 + Deuteronomy 22:24 + Genesis 4:1] The child is of Joseph's loins within the espousal; "before they came together" (1:18) dates the finding, and "took unto him his wife" (1:24) is her coming into his house (*synerchomai*, the 1 Corinthians 7:5 word). Their reply — "no row of union before the finding" — restates the disputed claim against the wife rows.

## 5. The registers name him, and *nomizō* decides nothing

[L1] The narrator: "the parents brought in the child Jesus" (Luke 2:27), "his parents went to Jerusalem" (2:41) — [L2] *goneis* (parents), both parents, Joseph included; "Joseph and his mother" (2:33) names him beside the mother. [L1] Mary, after the annunciation: "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing" (2:48); Luke 2:49 gives the covenant Father — two fathers in one passage, exactly as 1 Chronicles 28:6 holds two. [L2] *nomizō* carries no verdict of its own: Acts 16:13 "where prayer was wont to be made" is the same verb form with no mistaken belief; their "supposing" rows are corrected by the story's events, not by the word.

## 6. The sign is bounded; "of a woman" is mortality

[L1] Isaiah 7:14 reads *ha-almah* (H5959, the young woman); *bethulah* (H1330, the virgin-word — 50 rows, 19 books) is not used; 1611 margin "or, thou, O virgin, shalt call." [L1] The sign is bounded by 7:16 "before the child shall know to refuse the evil, and choose the good," by 8:3-4 (the prophetess conceives and bears), and 8:8 "Immanuel" in Isaiah's own day. [L1] "made of a woman" (Galatians 4:4) is the mortality idiom: "among them that are born of women there hath not risen a greater than John the Baptist" (Matthew 11:11), whose father the same gospel names (Luke 1:13, 57). Their own round now grants the idiom "does not carry the claim alone" — the "seed in the woman alone" link is gone from their declared chain.

**Floor:** the seed is out of loins and bowels (2 Samuel 7:12; Psalm 132:11; Acts 2:30; Romans 1:3; Hebrews 7:14); the father is named by Mary, the narrator, the town, a disciple and the angel; the reckoning is by the father's house (Numbers 1:2; Ezra 2:61-62 — the wife's house is "as polluted"); "Son of God" is the covenant title (1 Chronicles 28:6) and the resurrection declaration (Acts 13:33); the curse is uncited and the LORD's own rule repents pronounced sentences (Jeremiah 18:7-8); Mary was "Mary thy wife" and the abstinence is bounded by "till." [L4] The child is of the loins of Joseph within the espousal. The declared chain is broken at links 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6.
