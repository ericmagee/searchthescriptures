CHRIST'S BIOLOGICAL FATHER: JOSEPH, THE NAMED DAVIDIC FATHER (search words: "Virgin Mary"; DG-8; Sights 519, 32, 25). (1) The father is named: "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing" (Luke 2:48); "Is not this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know?" (John 6:42); "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife" (Matthew 1:20). (2) The promised seed is out of a man's body, according to the flesh: "which shall proceed out of thy bowels" (2 Samuel 7:12); "Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne" (Psalm 132:11); "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne" (Acts 2:30); "made of the seed of David according to the flesh" (Romans 1:3); "Jesus Christ of the seed of David" (2 Timothy 2:8); "it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda" (Hebrews 7:14), the tribe counted "by the house of their fathers" (Numbers 1:2, 18). (3) The canon's stated means of begetting is knowing: "Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived" (Genesis 4:1). Joseph and Mary were husband and wife from the espousal ("Joseph her husband," Matthew 1:19; "Mary thy wife," Matthew 1:20; "his neighbour's wife," Deuteronomy 22:24). The record holds, at L4 footed on those rows, that Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal (Sight 32). (4) The timing rows, read inside the chain (Sight 32): "before they came together, she was found with child" (Matthew 1:18) dates the finding; Matthew sets what came together by what follows, "took unto him his wife" (Matthew 1:24), her coming into his house; "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son" (Matthew 1:25) covers the pregnancy after the taking; "seeing I know not a man" (Luke 1:34) is present tense, her state when the angel spoke, not a vow. (5) "Of the Holy Ghost" (Matthew 1:18, 20) is the covenant-origin marker, not paternity (DG-18). "Of whom was born Jesus" (Matthew 1:16) does not narrate the begetting and is not a counter-witness. "Being (as was supposed) the son of Joseph" (Luke 3:23): enomizeto is the verb form of "where prayer was wont to be made" (Acts 16:13) and carries no verdict of its own; it stands with Mary's "thy father" (Luke 2:48) (Sight 25).
