UNSUPPORTED-L4 PREMISES

1. That the three timing rows carry a union before the birth. The footing is "wife," but Matthew 1:18 says "before they came together" and 1:25 says "knew her not till." Quote: "Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal"
2. That Matthew 1:20 names covenant origin and not a bodily conception, resting on John 1:13 about believers with no exhibited link to a womb. Quote: "The verse marks covenant origin"
3. That "came together" at 1:18 is decided by 1:24, reversing the narrative order. Quote: "Matthew sets what "came together" by what follows"
4. That loins-language is limited to the immediate male line, though Hebrews 7:10 reaches generations and Luke 1:42 names the womb's fruit. Quote: "it is the line of the men, not a metaphor for the mother"
5. That Galatians 4:4 is only a mortality idiom, ignoring "God sent forth his Son" and Genesis 3:15 "her seed." Quote: "Galatians 4:4 "made of a woman" is the mortality idiom"
6. That a curse the apostles do not cite lapses, an argument from silence against the text of Jeremiah 22:30. Quote: "A curse the apostles never cite cannot carry the keystone."
