UNSUPPORTED-L4 PREMISES

LINK 1: the step from the passive verb to the exclusion of a father floats — the exhibited footing is the active-to-passive shift and the feminine pronoun, and the counter rows Romans 1:3, Acts 2:30, 2 Samuel 7:12, Genesis 4:1 are never engaged. Defender's words: "The grammar assigns the birth to the mother alone. That is a deliberate signal, not an accident."

LINK 2: the claim that Mary's question itself excludes a man is supplied by the reader; the exhibited rows state a question and an answer about the Spirit's coming and a name, and none of them says no man is involved. Defender's words: "The question presupposes that the conception will not come from a man."

LINK 3: the asserted ground of Joseph's intent is not in the row — Matthew 1:19 names two grounds and no verb of knowledge, and Deuteronomy 22:23-24 and 24:1 are not named by Matthew. Defender's words: "Joseph's reason for mercy and privacy is that he knew the child was not his."

LINK 4: the exclusion of a human father from "made of a woman" and "her seed" floats on one layer — Job 14:1, Job 25:4, Matthew 11:11 (John, whose father is named, Luke 1:13) and Genesis 4:1 are the counter rows and are not answered by the exhibited footing. Defender's words: "Scripture assigns the human origin to the woman and the preparation of the body to God, not to a human father."

LINK 5: the claim that Mary's flesh carries the Davidic line rests on no verse that states Mary's descent; the exhibited rows are Luke 1:42 and Romans 1:3, and the stated kinswoman row is Luke 1:5, 36, "of the daughters of Aaron." Defender's words: "Mary's flesh then carries the line, which Luke 1:42 and Romans 1:3 fit."

LINK 6: the denial of any human paternity rests on Luke 1:35 alone and does not answer 1 Chronicles 28:6, 2 Samuel 7:14, Acts 13:33 and Romans 1:4, where the title and the begetting are placed elsewhere than at the conception. Defender's words: "This is a divine and unique origin. It is not a human paternity of seed, so the denial of a biological father stands."
