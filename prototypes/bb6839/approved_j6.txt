CHALLENGER CHAIN MARK: <FOOTED|STRAINED|SLACK> -- <one clause naming the load-bearing link that decides the mark>.
These two lines come last, after OUT-OF-ROUND CONCESSIONS, the defender's first. One definition marks each side's own constructed chain, the defender's declared chain (D1 to the conclusion link) and the challenger's own alternative reading: FOOTED = every load-bearing link of the side's chain is footed on an exhibited L1-L3 layer; its reading stands as a built structure. STRAINED = the chain is built but one or more load-bearing links float, asserted at L4 without exhibited footing; it stands only under charity. SLACK = the side advanced no coherent chain of its own: objections without a constructed reading, or a construction that never cohered. Each mark describes ONLY how well that side built its own chain. It is descriptive, not a verdict. It MUST NOT change DEFENSE STANDING, CHALLENGE STANDING, CONCEDED LINKS, CHALLENGER CONCEDED LINKS, the CLAUSE line, KEYSTONE or the top-line verdict. A FOOTED chain is fully compatible with its side losing the debate; a SLACK chain is fully compatible with its side winning it.
