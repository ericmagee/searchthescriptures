DEFENDER (standard reading) -- ROUND 3, LINK ROUND

**LINK 1: SUPPORTED**
- [L1] Matthew 1:2-16 uses "begat" for every generation, but verse 16 reads "Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus." The chain of "begat" stops at Joseph and does not reach the child.
- [L2] In "of whom" (ex hēs, out of whom) the relative pronoun is feminine singular, so the birth is referred to Mary alone. "Was born" (egennēthē, was brought forth) is passive and has no male subject. Matthew 1:20 says "that which is conceived [gennēthen, begotten or conceived] in her is of the Holy Ghost."
- [L1] Matthew 1:18 says "before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost." The conception is dated before any union.
- Chain: the pressed rows are speech about Joseph, not a statement that he begat the child.
  - Luke 2:48 "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing" and Luke 2:27 "the parents brought in the child Jesus" are household usage.
  - Luke 4:22 "Is not this Joseph's son?" and John 6:42 "the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know" are the crowd's words. John 1:45 "Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph" is a disciple's description.
  - Luke 3:23 supplies the narrator's own qualifier, "being (as was supposed) the son of Joseph." [L2] The verb is enomizeto (was reckoned by custom).
  - Matthew 1:20 "Joseph, thou son of David" names the man to whom the naming is assigned ("thou shalt call his name JESUS," 1:21). It does not name a begetter.
  - Acts 2:30 speaks of David's loins, and Link 3 answers it.

**LINK 3: SUPPORTED**
- [L1] Luke 1:34 "How shall this be, seeing I know not a man?" is answered in 1:35 "The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee... therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God." [L2] "Therefore" (dio kai, for which reason also) ties the title to the mode of conception.
- [L3] Genesis 3:15 says "her seed." Isaiah 7:14 says "a virgin shall conceive," which Matthew 1:22-23 applies to this birth.
- Pressed rows: 2 Samuel 7:12 "which shall proceed out of thy bowels," Genesis 35:11 "kings shall come out of thy loins," Psalm 132:11 "Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne," and Acts 2:30 "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh." These name David's or Jacob's body as the source of the line. None says a man begat the last generation.
- [L2] Galatians 4:4 says "made of a woman" (genomenon ek gynaikos, come to be out of a woman). Romans 1:3 says "made of the seed of David" (ek spermatos Dauid, out of David's seed). The verb and the preposition are the same, so David's flesh reached this child through the woman.

**LINK 4 [premise 3]: NARROWED TO:** the Jechonias limb as corroboration only.
- [L1] Matthew 1:11-12 places Jechonias in Joseph's line. [L1] Jeremiah 22:30 says "No man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne of David."
- What I keep: Joseph's line carries that bar, so the throne-right in Luke 1:32 cannot rest on Joseph's begetting. The curse does not stand alone as proof.
- Jeremiah 18:8 concerns a nation that turns from evil. Jeremiah 22 pronounces on a man, "write ye this man childless" (22:30).
- Haggai 2:23 and 1:1 set the signet on a "governor of Judah," never on a king. That matches the bar rather than reversing it.

**LINK 4 [premise 4]: NARROWED TO:** the conditional only: if no male begat the child, the Davidic flesh came through Mary.
- I drop any claim that a verse names Mary's descent. Luke 3:23-31 does not name her, and "Heli as Mary's father" is inference.
- Footing is [L2] Galatians 4:4, ek gynaikos (out of a woman), with Romans 1:3, ek spermatos Dauid (out of David's seed). The chain from these two is conditional and does not depend on Luke 3:23.
- Numbers 1:2, 1:18 and Ezra 2:59, 2:61-62 govern registration "by the house of their fathers," which is legal reckoning. Hebrews 7:14 "sprang out of Juda" (anatetalken, has risen up) states tribe, not a begetter.

**LINK 5: NARROWED TO:** the pairing, not a correction.
- [L1] Luke 2:48 "thy father" is followed at once by Luke 2:49 "wist ye not that I must be about my Father's business?" I drop any claim that 2:49 denies Joseph's role.
- What I keep: the text places two fathers in adjacent verses, and the child answers Mary's title with his own. [L3] Luke 1:35 told Mary "the Son of God," so the second "Father" is already defined for her.
- Luke 2:27 "the parents" and Acts 16:13 "where prayer was wont to be made" use the same family of verbs, nomizō (hold by custom). The verb marks custom or reckoning, not a verdict on begetting.

**LINK 6: SUPPORTED**
- [L1] Matthew 1:18 puts the conception "before they came together," and Matthew 1:25 says "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son." The abstinence runs through the birth, so Joseph could not have begotten the child.
- [L1] Joseph's role is the naming: "thou shalt call his name JESUS" (1:21), "and he called his name JESUS" (1:25). Matthew 1:19-20 gives him "husband" and "wife."
- Genesis 4:1 "Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived" gives the ordinary means. Luke 1:34-35 states the exception.
- Romans 1:3 and Hebrews 7:14 are answered under Links 3 and 4. [L2] Acts 2:30 is David's loins (osphyos, loins), and the flesh route is ek gynaikos (out of a woman).

CHALLENGER'S UNSUPPORTED-L4 PREMISES

1. The child's answer is stripped of contrast. Challenger: "speaks of business and denies nothing". PRESSED WITH: Luke 2:49 "wist ye not that I must be about my Father's business?"; Luke 2:48 "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing"; Luke 1:35 "that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God".
2. The Spirit-conception is reduced to a title. Challenger: "covenant origin, not paternity". PRESSED WITH: Matthew 1:20 "that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost"; Matthew 1:18 "before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost"; Luke 1:35 "The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee".
3. David's flesh is routed through Joseph by assertion. Challenger: "David's flesh seed reaches this child through a father of David's house, and the text names him." PRESSED WITH: Matthew 1:16 "Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus"; Galatians 4:4 "made of a woman"; Romans 1:3 "made of the seed of David according to the flesh".
4. The Jechonias bar is waved off for want of a citing row. Challenger: "The curse is never laid here." PRESSED WITH: Jeremiah 22:30 "No man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne of David"; Matthew 1:11-12 "Josias begat Jechonias... Jechonias begat Salathiel"; Haggai 1:1 "governor of Judah".
5. Acts 2:30's loins are made Joseph's. Challenger: "The seed is of his loins". PRESSED WITH: Acts 2:30 "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh"; Luke 1:32 "the throne of his father David"; Matthew 1:16 "of whom was born Jesus".
6. Mary's question is confined to a passing state. Challenger: "Mary's question is her state when the angel spoke". PRESSED WITH: Luke 1:34 "How shall this be, seeing I know not a man?"; Luke 1:35 "therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God"; Matthew 1:25 "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son".
