## Problems and solutions
P1. A concession is defined by footing, not by truth. D3 Build (link round): "LINK n: CONCEDED -- it floats free (rests on no exhibited prior-layer footing)". D4 Build (close) offers keep, narrow or concede with no test. A link that is wrong but quotes two layers is SUPPORTED by the form, so a defender has no rule under which it should concede it. Ruling 405 (Commitment 3: a link that cannot agree with the meaning of a verse pressed against it fails) binds the judges only.
S1. In D3 Build and D4 Build, make the concession test the meaning test of ruling 405: CONCEDED when the link floats free, or when a verse pressed against it, read with the meaning the challenger showed with its layers, cannot agree with the link and you cannot show from its layers a meaning that does.

P2. NARROWED TO is free and its kept words are not tested. The rule: "a block that drops a limb and keeps the link in a narrowed form is not a concession: grade the narrowed link." D5's kept limb, "the seed of David reaches Jesus through that body", is the limb it said it dropped.
S2. Judge rule: a narrowed link is graded by what its kept words still claim; where the kept words assert what the side gave up, the narrowing is void and the link is graded as it was first written. Give the judges the side's own grant sentences ("I do not rest", "I drop", "I concede", "the text does not say") beside each link, read by code.
