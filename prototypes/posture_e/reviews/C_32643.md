4. **Check glosses against the lexicon.** Compare each quoted gloss with `lexicon_words.anchor_gloss` for its Strong's number, and flag added words.
5. **Brief the Oracle on answering.** When pressed, it should answer with the fit (Matthew 1:20's "for") rather than the absence ("No verb of knowing"). The absence form is barred to the other side as well, and here it cost the Oracle two judges.
