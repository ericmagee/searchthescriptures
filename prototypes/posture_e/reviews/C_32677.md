## 4. Findings, most serious first

### Finding 1. The defender withdrew the Mary-flesh limb, then reasserted it in its close, and nothing marked it

- **The withdrawal, round 2:** "No row I cite says outright that Mary was of David's house. I withdraw my sentence that Romans 1:3 'places the Davidic descent through Mary.'"
- **The withdrawal, link round:** "LINK 5 [premise 5]: NARROWED TO: the limb that the 'evident' Juda descent of Hebrews 7:14 is reckoned in law through Joseph's house. I drop the claim that a row places Mary in the house of David."
- **The close, under D1:** "Paul puts 'made of the seed of David' beside 'made of a woman' without a begetter, so the descent in Romans 1:3 is carried through the woman. That fits D1."
- **The close, under D5:** "The 'flesh' descent from David comes through the woman, as Romans 1:3 and Galatians 4:4 put together say."
- The close returns the very claim withdrawn twice: that the flesh of David reaches the child through Mary.
  - Ruling 412, item 3: "a close sentence that reverses a link-round narrowing or grant is marked and disregarded."
  - No judge marked either sentence. The self-contradiction mark (B7) did not fire. The synthesis reports D5 as "NARROWED-BY-ITS-OWN-SIDE" and does not mention the return.
- The withdrawal cures then accepted D5 as standing without the withdrawn claim, on exactly the ground the close reasserted:
  - Grok: "STANDS WITHOUT IT because (L1) Galatians 4:4 'made of a woman' and (L1) Matthew 1:20 ... carry D5 without Mary's Davidic house."
  - ChatGPT: "STANDS WITHOUT IT because (L1) Galatians 4:4 says 'made of a woman' ..."
- The D5 conclusion still needs the flesh of David to reach the child: "made of the seed of David according to the flesh" (Romans 1:3). Once Mary's house is withdrawn and Joseph is excluded as begetter, no carrier is left in the defender's rows. The synthesis names this as the defender's most vulnerable point: it leaves "the physical requirement ... unattached to a named Davidic physical progenitor."
- **Ruling 424's test** asks whether the link's own conclusion needed what was withdrawn. This D5 is a ready test case: the defender's own round-1 D5 was built on that sentence.
