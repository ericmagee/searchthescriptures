## Proposed sight

**Title.** Flesh After His Kind: the Creator's Order Gives Human Flesh One Way into Being, Forbids the Mingled Seed, Made Christ "Like unto His Brethren" in All Things, and Carries Lineage and Heritage Through the Seed (Sperma) of the Father

**Primary passage.** Hebrews 2:14-18

**Layer status.** L1-L2-L3

**Summary.**

CLAIM (the operator's words): "conceived by the Holy Ghost" read as a begetting of the flesh "is not a natural selection or order of the Creator." "Human flesh has only 1 mechanism of existence according to scripture." "Lineage and heritage is carried through the seed (sperma) of the father."

L1. THE CREATOR'S ORDER: SEED AFTER HIS KIND.
- Genesis 1:12: "And the earth brought forth grass, [and] herb yielding seed after his kind, and the tree yielding fruit, whose seed [was] in itself, after his kind: and God saw that [it was] good."
- 1 Corinthians 15:37-40: "And that which thou sowest, thou sowest not that body that shall be, but bare grain, it may chance of wheat, or of some other [grain]: But God giveth it a body as it hath pleased him, and to every seed his own body. All flesh [is] not the same flesh: but [there is] one [kind of] flesh of men, another flesh of beasts, another of fishes, [and] another of birds. [There are] also celestial bodies, and bodies terrestrial: but the glory of the celestial [is] one, and the [glory] of the terrestrial [is] another."

L1. MIXED SEED IS FORBIDDEN.
- Leviticus 19:19: "Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind: thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed: neither shall a garment mingled of linen and woollen come upon thee."
- Deuteronomy 22:9: "Thou shalt not sow thy vineyard with divers seeds: lest the fruit of thy seed which thou hast sown, and the fruit of thy vineyard, be defiled."

L1. HE WAS MADE LIKE HIS BRETHREN, IN ALL THINGS.
- Hebrews 2:14: "Forasmuch then as the children are partakers of flesh and blood, he also himself likewise took part of the same."
- Hebrews 2:16: "For verily he took not on [him the nature of] angels; but he took on [him] the seed of Abraham."
- Hebrews 2:17-18: "Wherefore in all things it behoved him to be made like unto [his] brethren, that he might be a merciful and faithful high priest in things [pertaining] to God, to make reconciliation for the sins of the people. For in that he himself hath suffered being tempted, he is able to succour them that are tempted."
- John 1:1: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."
- John 1:14: "And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us."

L1-L2. LINEAGE AND HERITAGE THROUGH THE SEED (SPERMA) OF THE FATHER.
- Numbers 1:2: "after their families, by the house of their fathers, with the number of [their] names, every male by their polls."
- Numbers 1:18: "they declared their pedigrees after their families, by the house of their fathers."
- Romans 1:3: "which was made of the seed of David according to the flesh"; the Greek at "seed" is spermatos (G4690).
- Hebrews 7:10: "For he was yet in the loins of his father."
- Acts 2:30: "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne."
- 2 Samuel 7:12: "I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels."

L3. "OF THE HOLY GHOST" STAYS IN ITS ROW.
- Matthew 1:20: "that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost". This is the covenant-origin marker, not paternity (DG-18). It is held as written and never set against the rows above (DG-59).
- John 3:6: "That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit."

L3. "HER SEED" DOES NOT REMOVE THE FATHER.
- Genesis 3:15: "between thy seed and her seed."
- Genesis 4:25: "And Adam knew his wife again; and she bare a son, and called his name Seth: For God, [said she], hath appointed me another seed instead of Abel." Eve calls Seth her seed, and Adam is his father.

[L4], footed on the rows above. The Creator's order gives each seed its own body after his kind, and one kind of flesh of men. His law forbids the mingled seed. Christ took part of flesh and blood "likewise," "in all things ... like unto [his] brethren," and took on him "the seed of Abraham." The pedigree is declared by the house of the fathers, and the seed is in the father's loins. So a begetting of the flesh by the Holy Ghost, with the flesh routed "through the woman," has no row in the Creator's order. The flesh came as his brethren's came, of the seed of David according to the flesh.

IN DEBATE, CHALLENGE (the operator's words): "Explain how is it evident Christ sprang out of Judah given the Law in Numbers (Hebrews 7:14)"
- Hebrews 7:14: "For [it is] evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda; of which tribe Moses spake nothing concerning priesthood."
- With Numbers 1:2 and 1:18: "by the house of their fathers."
