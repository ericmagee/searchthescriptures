## Proposed replacement text (the changed sections only)

**1. Replace "L1. MIXED SEED IS FORBIDDEN" with:**

L1-L2. MIXED SEED IS FORBIDDEN, AND THE TEXT KEEPS FLESH AND SPIRIT AS TWO KINDS.
- **The law.**
  - Leviticus 19:19: "Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind: thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed: neither shall a garment mingled of linen and woollen come upon thee."
  - "Diverse kind" and "mingled" are kil'ayim (H3610), "two heterogeneities; divers seeds (-e kinds), mingled (seed)". "Gender" is raba (H7250), "to squat or lie out flat, i.e. (specifically) in copulation; let gender".
  - Deuteronomy 22:9: "Thou shalt not sow thy vineyard with divers seeds: lest the fruit of thy seed which thou hast sown, and the fruit of thy vineyard, be defiled."
- **The two kinds.**
  - John 3:6: "That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit."
  - 1 Corinthians 15:40: "[There are] also celestial bodies, and bodies terrestrial: but the glory of the celestial [is] one, and the [glory] of the terrestrial [is] another."

**2. Replace "L3. 'OF THE HOLY GHOST' STAYS IN ITS ROW" with:**

L1-L2-L3. MATTHEW 1:20 WHOLE, WITH ITS MARGIN: THE FATHER IS NAMED IN THE SAME ROW.
- Matthew 1:20: "But while he thought on these things, behold, the angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a dream, saying, Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost. [conceived: Gr. begotten]"
- **The margin's word.** It is gennaō (G1080), "to procreate (properly, of the father, but by extension of the mother); figuratively, to regenerate".
- **The same verb in the same frame.** 1 John 5:1 joins it to "of God", said of believers: "Whosoever believeth that Jesus is the Christ is born of God: and every one that loveth him that begat loveth him also that is begotten of him. [is born: Gr. has been born]". The verb stands three times in the verse (gegennētai, gennēsanta, gegennēmenon, G1080).
- **The reading.** "Of the Holy Ghost" is the covenant-origin marker, not paternity (DG-18). The row that carries it names the father of the house, "Joseph, thou son of David", and "Mary thy wife". The verse is held whole and never set against the rows above (DG-8, DG-59).
- John 3:6 stays here: "That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit."

**3. In "L1. HE WAS MADE LIKE HIS BRETHREN", quote Hebrews 2:15 and Hebrews 2:16 with its margin:**
- Hebrews 2:15: "And deliver them who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage."
- Hebrews 2:16: "For verily he took not on [him the nature of] angels; but he took on [him] the seed of Abraham. [took not...: Gr. taketh not hold of angels, but of the seed of Abraham he taketh hold]"
  - The verb is epilambanetai (G1949), "to seize (for help, injury, attainment, or any other purpose; literally or figuratively)".
  - "[him the nature of]" and "[him]" are supplied by the translators. Verse 16 is quoted as written; the claim about nature rests on 2:14 and 2:17.

**4. Add L2 under "LINEAGE AND HERITAGE THROUGH THE SEED (SPERMA) OF THE FATHER":**
- **Numbers 1:2 and 1:18,** "by the house of their fathers". The Hebrew is le-veit avotam: bayith (H1004), "a house (in the greatest variation of applications, especially family...)", and 'ab (H1), "father, in a literal and immediate, or figurative and remote application".
- **Hebrews 7:10,** "in the loins of his father". The Greek is osphys (G3751), "the loin (externally), i.e. the hip; internally (by extension) procreative power", and patēr (G3962), "a 'father' (literally or figuratively, near or more remote)".
- **2 Samuel 7:12,** "I will set up thy seed after thee". The Hebrew is zera' (H2233), "seed; figuratively, fruit, plant, sowing-time, posterity".
- **Romans 1:3** (spermatos, G4690) names the forefather, David, "according to the flesh".

**5. Replace [L4] points (2) and (3) with:**
- (2) His law forbids the gendering of two kinds (kil'ayim, "two heterogeneities"). The text keeps the flesh and the Spirit as two kinds of birth (John 3:6), and the terrestrial and the celestial as two kinds of body (1 Corinthians 15:40). So a begetting of the flesh by the Spirit would be the gendering of two kinds the law forbids.
- (3) Christ took part of flesh and blood "likewise" (Hebrews 2:14) and was made "in all things ... like unto [his] brethren" (Hebrews 2:17).
