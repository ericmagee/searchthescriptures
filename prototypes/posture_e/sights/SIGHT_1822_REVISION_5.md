## The change

REPLACE the live block, from "L1-L2. ROMANS 9:10 AND MATTHEW 1:20 ARE TWO FORMS, NOT ONE." through "Neither row cancels the other (DG-59).", with:

L1-L2-L3. "OF THE HOLY GHOST" (MATTHEW 1:18, 20): THE CHILD IN HER IS ANOINTED.

Matthew 1:18: "Now the birth of Jesus Christ was on this wise: When as his mother Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost."

"With child of" here is en gastri echousa ek (G1064, G1722, G2192, G1537). That is the same echo + ek as Romans 9:10, "had conceived (koiten echousa, G2845) by (ek, G1537) one". The opponent reads ek at 1:18 as the begetter. Three rows of reasoning answer him.

(1) THE WHOLE COUNT, AND THE HUSBAND IN THE EVENT. The reading that the Holy Ghost begat stands on one event (Matthew 1:18-20) against the whole count above:
- 60 canon rows;
- 33 that narrate a conception;
- every one of them naming the man in the row or its chain.
The event itself names the husband: "Joseph her husband" (1:19), "Mary thy wife" (1:20). "Before they came together" is synerchomai, the coming into one household, and the two were husband and wife from the espousal (Sight 32). A doctrine resting on one event against the whole count is single-verse doctrine.

(2) WHAT "OF THE HOLY GHOST" SAYS: THE CHILD IS THE ANOINTED.
- "Christ" is Christos (G5547), "anointed, i.e. the Messiah".
- Matthew 1:18 names the child in the same row: "the birth of Jesus Christ".
- He is the Anointed from his birth, before the Jordan:
  - Luke 2:11: "For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord."
  - Luke 2:26: "And it was revealed unto him by the Holy Ghost, that he should not see death, before he had seen the Lord's Christ."
  - Matthew 2:4: "And when he had gathered all the chief priests and scribes of the people together, he demanded of them where Christ should be born."
- At the Jordan the anointing is declared and named:
  - Luke 3:22: "And the Holy Ghost descended in a bodily shape like a dove upon him, and a voice came from heaven, which said, Thou art my beloved Son; in thee I am well pleased."
  - Acts 10:37-38: "That word, [I say], ye know, which was published throughout all Judaea, and began from Galilee, after the baptism which John preached; How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil; for God was with him."
  - Luke 4:18: "The Spirit of the Lord [is] upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised,"
- The same God anoints believers with the same Spirit, and the anointing abides in them. Every one of them has a father:
  - 2 Corinthians 1:21: "Now he which stablisheth us with you in Christ, and hath anointed us, [is] God;"
  - 2 Corinthians 1:22: "Who hath also sealed us, and given the earnest of the Spirit in our hearts."
  - 1 John 2:20: "But ye have an unction from the Holy One, and ye know all things."
  - 1 John 2:27: "But the anointing which ye have received of him abideth in you, and ye need not that any man teach you: but as the same anointing teacheth you of all things, and is truth, and is no lie, and even as it hath taught you, ye shall abide in him. [in him: or, in it]"
  - "Unction" and "anointing" are chrisma (G5545): "an unguent or smearing, i.e. (figuratively) the special endowment ('chrism') of the Holy Spirit". "Anointed" is chrio (G5548): "to smear or rub with oil, i.e. (by implication) to consecrate to an office or religious service".
  - The Spirit "in our hearts" and the anointing that "abideth in you" consecrate. They do not beget.
- The child is named as the one anointed: Acts 4:27, "For of a truth against thy holy child Jesus, whom thou hast anointed, both Herod, and Pontius Pilate, with the Gentiles, and the people of Israel, were gathered together,"
So "found with child of the Holy Ghost" and "that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost" say this: the child in her is the Anointed, anointed from the womb, with the anointing declared at the Jordan. Neither row names a begetter.

THE VERB AT 1:20, MET IN PLACE. "Conceived [Gr. begotten]" is gennao (G1080): "to procreate (properly, of the father, but by extension of the mother)". The verb says that a child was begotten in her. "Of the Holy Ghost" says what the child is: the Anointed. The row that carries the verb names the husband and the wife: "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife". The verb's proper subject, the father, is named in the same row. The Spirit is not that subject.

(3) THE HOLY GHOST ON A CHILD FROM THE WOMB DOES NOT REMOVE THE FATHER.
- Luke 1:15: "For he shall be great in the sight of the Lord, and shall drink neither wine nor strong drink; and he shall be filled with the Holy Ghost, even from his mother's womb."
- His father is named by the same angel: "thy wife Elisabeth shall bear thee a son" (Luke 1:13).
- This is a precept, not a word parallel (the ek of 1:15 goes with "womb"): the Holy Ghost on a child from the womb, and the father named.

(4) ROMANS 9:10 NAMES ITS BEGETTER BY KOITE.
- Romans 9:10: "And not only [this]; but when Rebecca also had conceived by one, [even] by our father Isaac;"
- koite (G2845): "a couch; by extension, cohabitation; by implication, the male sperm".
- Matthew 1:18-20 has no koite in its rows. What it names is the husband and wife in the event.
- Both rows stand whole, and neither cancels the other (DG-59). "Of the Holy Ghost" is the covenant-origin and anointing marker (Sight 10; DG-8).

SUPPORT ONLY: John 3:5, 3:6, 3:8 and John 1:13 give gennao + ek with the Spirit, or with God, for the new birth. They are not womb rows. John 3:6: "That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit."
