A witness contradicts a link only when its own words state the contrary; a verb that leaves the point unnarrated does not, for either side. This tests a pressed witness, not a chained premise, which is judged by its footing (DG-61). When the witnesses pressed against a link are the pressing side's own chain read together, the collision weighs chain against chain by footing, never verse by verse; that no one of them states the contrary in its own words is the stating-verse test DG-61 retires, at a judge's seat as at a debater's, for either side.
