RULE E-ANS (an answer to a pressed verse). Decide FIRST what the right grade is: MEETS only when the answer shows, in its own words, how the verse fits the answering side's case, or why the stated meaning does not follow from what the verse says. MISSES when the answer rests on an absence (it says only that the verse, or the other side, names no, states no, does not name, gives no, or is silent on something) without a fit of its own, or when it does not address the stated meaning. Then for each judge's grade: AGREE when it matches the right grade, DISAGREE when it does not.
RULE E-VOID (a listed premise ruled VOID). A premise is VOID only when its charge says the opponent's quoted words state something they do not state (a negation, a universal, or another claim beyond the quote). Read only the words the charge says the quote states; a clause describing what the premise props, or the objection it serves, is not part of the charge. AGREE with the VOID ruling when the charge goes beyond its quote; DISAGREE when it does not.
