## What the enforcer did
It confirmed link 3 in 2 of 6 sends (set C send 1, per side; execution 34535, combined), two lines each, always with the round 3 headline as the earlier sentence. In the other 4 sends it did not raise the pair. In both confirming replies these lines alone break link 3.

## Cause
The cause is in the debater's text, not in a fault of the prompt. The round 3 headline restates the link without "and by law"; the closing headline restates it with "and by law". Read word for word, the link was narrower in round 3 than in closing. The enforcer reads the round 3 headline as the whole of what was kept. My earlier reading (not a contradiction) rests on the named drop: round 3 names one limb as dropped (Hebrews 7:14) and legal fatherhood is not it.
The prompt does not settle which reading is right. Its sentence is: "A closing sentence that asserts again, as the debater's own, what it withdrew, dropped or gave up in an earlier round IS a contradiction." It does not say whether a part left out of a "NARROWED TO" restatement, without being named as dropped, counts as dropped. Because the text is silent, the call goes one way in 2 sends and the other in 4.
CORRECTION to my earlier files (setBC_reading_w3.md and the 33442 combined-call reading): I labeled these lines "doubtful, read by me as not a contradiction". The label doubtful stands. I should have recorded that the enforcer's reading is the literal one and that round 3 holds no word on legal fatherhood. This is not a plain enforcer error.

## Do the eight control replies bear on it
Only in part. None of the eight holds this form (a narrowed restatement that leaves a part unnamed, widened again in closing). Two are neighbours:
- 33299 A79 (confirmed): "Galatians 4:4 is withdrawn as load-bearing for Link 6", while the same round says the verse "stays in my chain as a verse consistent with the stated source". The enforcer set the stated remainder aside. That is a wider reading than on 33442, where no remainder was stated.
- 33299 A73 and A83 (confirmed, true): "Link 3 is narrowed to Isaiah 7:14 and Matthew 1:22-23. The Genesis 3:15 footing is withdrawn." Here the dropped part is named.
So the control shows the enforcer reads withdrawals broadly; it does not test the 33442 form.

## Countermeasures, sized
1. No change to the engine. The guard stays what the operator approved: I read every CONFIRMED closing line by hand at each run grade. Size 0.
2. One word in the closing enforcer's sentence: "what it withdrew, dropped or gave up" becomes "what it expressly withdrew, dropped or gave up". Measured +10 characters per call, +20 per debate (two calls); no code. Effect predicted, not observed. Risk: it could excuse a true line where the drop is by restatement and plainly meant. It would need a replay on 33442 and on the true lines of 33442 links 2 and 6, 34108 and 33299 before any write: relay calls, which need the operator.
3. No code-side countermeasure exists: the Record can check that quotations stand, not whether they conflict.

## Recommendation
Countermeasure 1. A text fix is not worth proposing now: the reading is defensible on the words, it appears in 2 of 6 sends on one debate, and the one-word fix has a risk I cannot size without calls.
What is open is not a design matter but the reach of the operator's contradiction rule: when a debater restates a link narrower in an earlier round without naming what was left out, and states it wider in closing, is that a contradiction? Under his words ("you can't withdraw after you made the contradiction ... you're caught") I cannot settle it from the text. If the coordinator thinks it worth his time, that single question is the ask; until then I grade such a line as doubtful and say so in the run grade.

Effect on this study: the defender's round 3 "NARROWED TO: Joseph is father only by supposition" drops "by law"; the closing "by supposition and by law only" reclaims it. The enforcer's CONFIRMED reading of link 3 is TRUE under the ruling. At run grades: when I hand-read closing lines, a part present earlier, absent from a NARROWED TO restatement, and present again in closing is a reclaimed drop (true contradiction), whether or not the enforcer confirmed it.

No build follows from the ruling by itself. Raising the enforcer's catch rate on this form would need prompt text; not measured, not built.
