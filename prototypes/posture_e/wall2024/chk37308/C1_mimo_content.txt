# CHALLENGER'S ROUND 1 — "Did Christ have a biological father?"

**THE READING I PRESS:** Yes. The record names the father — Joseph, of the house of David — inside the very verse the defence calls the source-verse; it states the seed out of a man's body, according to the flesh; the angel's two verbs are empowerment and presence at every row of them; and the reckoning that makes the sprouting out of Juda *evident* runs by the house of the fathers. I press D1, D2, D3, D4, and the keystone **D6**. I demand no verse to state my conclusion in its own words.

---

## PREMISE 1 — vs D1: "of the Holy Ghost" is the covenant-origin marker, not paternity

[L1] Matthew 1:20: "But while he thought on these things, behold, the angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a dream, saying, Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost. [conceived: Gr. begotten]"

[L2] ἐκ (ek, G1537, "out of, from") stands behind "of the Holy Ghost" in Matthew 1:18 and 1:20. The canon's own rows for ἐκ with God or the Spirit are covenant/spiritual origin set over against blood and flesh:

[L1] John 1:13: "Which were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God." [L3] 1 John 5:1: "Whosoever believeth that Jesus is the Christ is born of God:" [L3] 1 John 3:9: "Whosoever is born of God doth not commit sin;" [L3] 1 John 4:7: "every one that loveth is born of God, and knoweth God."

[L3] Romans 1:3: "Concerning his Son Jesus Christ our Lord, which was made of the seed of David according to the flesh;" — [L2] κατὰ σάρκα (kata sarka, "according to the flesh") is the marker the record adds when flesh-descent is meant; [L2] σπέρμα (sperma, G4690, "seed") stands in the same row.

**[L4]** — footing: [L1] John 1:13 + [L2] ἐκ (G1537); [L3] Romans 1:3. Chain: "of the Holy Ghost" (ἐκ πνεύματος ἁγίου) stands in the register of "born of God" (ἐκ θεοῦ), and John 1:13 sets that birth over against "blood," "the flesh," and "the will of man." Where the record means flesh-descent it says so with κατὰ σάρκα and σπέρμα (Romans 1:3); Matthew 1:20 uses neither. And the row the defence calls the source-verse names the father of the house in the same breath — "Joseph, thou son of David ... Mary thy wife." D1 reads a covenant-origin marker as paternity.

## PREMISE 2 — vs D3: the angel's two verbs are empowerment and presence; the naming is a covenant title

[L1] Luke 1:35: "And the angel answered and said unto her, The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God."

[L2] ἐπέρχομαι (eperchomai, G1904, "to come upon") stands in Luke 1:35. [L3] Acts 1:8: "But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me" — the same verb and construction, said of the apostles.

[L2] ἐπισκιάζω (episkiazō, G1982, "to overshadow") stands in Luke 1:35. [L3] Luke 9:34: "there came a cloud, and overshadowed them:" [L3] Acts 5:15: "the shadow of Peter passing by might overshadow some of them."

[L2] κληθήσεται (klēthēsetai, future passive of καλέω, kaleō, G2564, "shall be called") stands behind "shall be called the Son of God." [L3] 2 Samuel 7:14: "I will be his father, and he shall be my son" — said of Solomon, whose father is David. [L3] 1 Chronicles 28:6: "Solomon thy son ... I have chosen him [to be] my son, and I will be his father."

[L1] Luke 1:32: "He shall be great, and shall be called the Son of the Highest: and the Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David:"

**[L4]** — footing: [L1] Luke 1:35 + [L2] ἐπέρχομαι, ἐπισκιάζω + [L3] Acts 1:8, Luke 9:34, 2 Samuel 7:14. Chain: both verbs are empowerment and presence at every row of them — the apostles are "come upon" and are not begotten of it; the cloud and the shadow "overshadow" and beget nothing. The naming is a covenant designation of the register God gives Solomon while Solomon has a father in David. And the title and the father-throne stand at Luke 1:32, before Mary's question at 1:34. D3's "the Sonship-naming tied to them" reads the naming back into a paternity the row does not give.

## PREMISE 3 — vs D2: husband and wife from the espousal; the abstinence is bounded

[L1] Matthew 1:18: "When as his mother Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost."
[L1] Matthew 1:19: "Then Joseph her husband, being a just [man], and not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away privily."
[L1] Matthew 1:20: "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife"
[L1] Matthew 1:24: "Then Joseph being raised from sleep did as the angel of the Lord had bidden him, and took unto him his wife:"
[L1] Matthew 1:25: "And knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son: and he called his name JESUS."

[L2] γινώσκω (ginōskō, G1097, "to know," the conjugal idiom) stands behind "knew"; ἕως οὗ (heōs hou, "till") bounds that not-knowing at the bringing forth. The record's own stated means of begetting is knowing: [L3] Genesis 4:1: "And Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived, and bare Cain."

[L2] συνέρχομαι (synerchomai, G4905, "to come together") stands behind "before they came together"; Matthew sets what "came together" is by what follows — "took unto him his wife" (Matthew 1:24), her coming into his house. The union's word throughout the record is "knew."

[L2] γινώσκω (ginōskō) present active indicative stands in Luke 1:34 ("seeing I know not a man") — her state when the angel spoke.

**[L4]** — footing: [L1] Matthew 1:19, 1:20, 1:25 + [L2] γινώσκω, ἕως οὗ + [L3] Genesis 4:1. Chain: Joseph and Mary are husband and wife from the espousal — "Joseph her husband" (1:19), "Mary thy wife" (1:20). The union's word is "knew" (1:25), and the canon's stated means of begetting is the husband knowing his wife (Genesis 4:1). "Till she had brought forth" bounds the abstinence at the birth; read in that chain, the record holds that Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal, and the child is of his loins. D2's claim that union is placed wholly after the birth is not what the rows state: 1:18's "before they came together" dates the **finding**, and the coming-together Matthew names is the taking of the wife at 1:24.

## PREMISE 4 — vs D4 and keystone D6: the seed out of a man's body; the father named; the reckoning

[L1] Romans 1:3: "which was made of the seed of David according to the flesh;"
[L1] Acts 2:30: "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne;"
[L1] 2 Samuel 7:12: "I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels,"
[L1] Psalm 132:11: "Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne. [body: Heb. belly]"
[L1] Hebrews 7:14: "For [it is] evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda;"
[L1] Hebrews 7:10: "For he was yet in the loins of his father, when Melchisedec met him."
[L1] Hebrews 2:16: "For verily he took not on [him the nature of] angels; but he took on [him] the seed of Abraham. [took not...: Gr. taketh not hold of angels, but of the seed of Abraham he taketh hold]"

[L2] σπέρμα (sperma, G4690, "seed") and κατὰ σάρκα (kata sarka, "according to the flesh") stand in Romans 1:3; [L2] ὀσφύς (osphys, G3751, "loins") stands in Acts 2:30; [L2] בֶּטֶן (beten, H990, "belly") stands behind Psalm 132:11's margin "body: Heb. belly."

[L1] Luke 2:48: "behold, thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing."
[L3] John 6:42: "Is not this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know?"
[L3] Luke 4:22: "Is not this Joseph's son?"
[L3] John 1:45: "Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph."
[L3] Luke 2:4: "because he was of the house and lineage of David:"

**THE PRESS — the question the keystone must answer.** [L1] Hebrews 7:14 states it is *evident* that our Lord sprang out of Juda. The reckoning [L3] Numbers 1:2 and 1:18 state is by the father's house: "after their families, by the house of their fathers" (1:2); "they declared their pedigrees after their families, by the house of their fathers" (1:18). [L2] בַּיִת (bayith, H1004, "house, family") and אָב ('ab, H1, "father") stand in those rows.

**[L4]** — footing: [L1] Hebrews 7:14 + [L3] Numbers 1:2, 1:18 + [L2] bayith, 'ab. Chain: under the stated reckoning, sprouting out of a tribe runs by the father's house. For that sprouting to be *evident*, the father of the house must be named — and the record names him: "Joseph, thou son of David" (Matthew 1:20), "of the house and lineage of David" (Luke 2:4). Where the tie to Joseph were supposal only, the sprouting would stand on a supposal and not be evident. And the seed rows require seed out of a man's body: "which shall proceed out of thy bowels" (2 Samuel 7:12), "the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh" (Acts 2:30), "the fruit of thy body" (Psalm 132:11). Husband-and-wife reckoning produces no seed out of loins; the seed is out of Joseph's loins, as the record's own anatomy requires.

## PREMISE 5 — vs D4: the passive and "as was supposed" carry no denial

[L1] Matthew 1:16: "And Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ."
[L2] ἐγεννήθη (egennēthē, aorist **passive** of γεννάω, gennaō, G1080) stands behind "was born"; ἧς (hēs, feminine of ὅς, hos, G3739, "of whom — her") points to Mary.

[L1] Luke 3:23: "being (as was supposed) the son of Joseph, which was [the son] of Heli,"
[L2] νομίζω (nomizō, G3543, "to suppose, to reckon by custom") stands behind "was supposed"; [L3] Acts 16:13 carries the same verb form at "where prayer was wont to be made" — the word carries no verdict of its own.

[L3] Luke 2:27: "when the parents brought in the child Jesus," [L3] Luke 2:41: "his parents went to Jerusalem," [L3] Luke 2:48: "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing."

**[L4]** — footing: [L1] Matthew 1:16 + [L2] ἐγεννήθη passive, ἧς; [L3] Luke 2:48. Chain: the passive carries the **birth** from Mary and names no agent in that clause; a clause that does not narrate the begetting cannot carry a denial of one. The same verse names the tie that does the work — "the husband of Mary." And νομίζω is a verb of custom, used at Acts 16:13 of "wont to be made"; beside it stand Mary's "thy father" (Luke 2:48) and Luke's "his parents" (2:41). D4 reads non-narration and custom-language as a denial of fatherhood.

## CHARGE

**ASSUMES:** the Holy Ghost stands as the source of the child's begetting in the flesh — **ROWS GIVE:** "Joseph, thou son of David" and "Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost" (Matthew 1:20).

---

## UNSUPPORTED-L4 PREMISES

1. **D1** — defender's words verbatim: *"the record states the source of the begetting twice, and both times the source it states is the Holy Ghost."* Pressed meaning, with layers: [L1] John 1:13 "Which were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God"; [L3] 1 John 5:1 "born of God"; [L2] ἐκ (G1537). The ἐκ-with-God rows are covenant origin set over against blood and flesh; the flesh marker is κατὰ σάρκα (Romans 1:3), absent from Matthew 1:20.

2. **D2** — defender's words verbatim: *"every stated row of union between Joseph and Mary stands after the birth of this child."* Pressed meaning: [L1] Matthew 1:19 "Joseph her husband"; Matthew 1:20 "Mary thy wife"; Matthew 1:25 "knew her not till she had brought forth"; [L2] ἕως οὗ (heōs hou, "till"); [L3] Genesis 4:1 "Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived." The espousal already names husband and wife; the "till" bounds the abstinence.

3. **D3** — defender's words verbatim: *"with the Sonship-naming tied to them by 'therefore'."* Pressed meaning: [L1] Luke 1:32 "shall be called the Son of the Highest: and the Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David"; [L3] Acts 1:8 (the Holy Ghost "come upon" the apostles); Luke 9:34 and Acts 5:15 (ἐπισκιάζω at cloud and shadow); 2 Samuel 7:14 (the title given Solomon, whose father is David). The title and father-throne stand before Mary's question.

4. **D4** — defender's words verbatim: *"the begetting-word the record gives for Jesus in Matthew 1:16 is passive, out of her."* Pressed meaning: [L1] Matthew 1:16 "of whom was born Jesus" beside "the husband of Mary"; [L2] ἐγεννήθη (G1080, passive) carries the birth; [L3] Luke 2:48 "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing." A clause that does not narrate the begetting cannot deny it.

5. **D6 (keystone)** — defender's words verbatim: *"Therefore Christ had no biological father."* Pressed meaning: [L1] Romans 1:3 "made of the seed of David according to the flesh"; Hebrews 7:14 "it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda"; [L3] 2 Samuel 7:12 "which shall proceed out of thy bowels"; Acts 2:30 "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh"; Numbers 1:2, 1:18 "by the house of their fathers"; John 6:42 "the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know". The seed rows require seed out of a man's body, and the reckoning that makes the sprouting evident names the father's house.
