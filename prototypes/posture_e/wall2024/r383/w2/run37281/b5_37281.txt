**LINK 5 [premise 5]: SUPPORTED.**

- **[L1]** Luke 1:35: "that holy thing which shall be born of thee". Luke 1:42: "blessed [is] the fruit of thy womb".
- **[L2]** *Karpos* (G2590), "fruit", stands in Luke 1:42 ("fruit of thy womb") and in Acts 2:30 ("of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ"). *Osphys* (G3751) is "loins".
- **[L3]** Matthew 1:16: "Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus".
- **[L4]** The same word, fruit, is said of Mary's womb and of David's loins. The child is the fruit of her womb, "born of thee", and is also the promised fruit of David's loins. The flesh of David's line reaches him by the womb. This rests on Luke 1:42, Acts 2:30 and *karpos*, with Luke 1:35 as the stated source.

1. **Romans 1:3**, with *sperma* (G4690), "seed". The seed is David's. The verse does not name Joseph's loins.
