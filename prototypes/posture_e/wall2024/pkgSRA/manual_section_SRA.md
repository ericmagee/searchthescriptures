# pkgSRA: an answer given in the same round under another verse (rule text)

The answer check (E-ANS, DeepSeek) is served, for each pressed verse, THE SIDE'S LINES ELSEWHERE IN THE SAME ROUND: at most two lines (each cut at 400 characters, 900 in all) of the answering round that do not name the pressed verse, are not press or MEANS lines, the round header or a LINK heading, are not the answer's own line and were not already served as the side's other lines. A line is picked by the words of the stated meaning (five letters or more, outside a stop list) that the answer itself does not use, each weighted by one over the number of lines of that round holding it; a line is served when its weight is at least 0.5 and it holds one such word found in three lines or fewer. The rule text says an answer the side gave in the same round under another verse or link counts when in its own words it meets the stated meaning. When the added text would leave an item unchecked, the same-round text is removed first, before the side's other lines.
Limits: lines are picked by shared words, not by meaning, so a line that answers the press in other words is not served, and a served line may not answer it (the check judges). An answer given in a later round under another verse is not served.
