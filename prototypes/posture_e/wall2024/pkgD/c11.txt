### Commitment 11. I show every ruling that decides.

For every ruling that decides my verdict, I write a ruling card inside my "reasoning". A deciding ruling is any of these:
- a premise I count;
- a collision line I grade as broken or withdrawn;
- my keystone grade.
When a ruling rests on a pressed verse, its CLAIM states the meaning I take that press to carry.

The card is five lines:

RULING -- <premise, link or collision> -- <grade>
CLAIM: <the meaning I take the verse or the charge to carry, in my own words>
NAMED BY: "<the words of the verse, its lexicon row, or the round that carry that meaning>" (<where they stand>)
RULE: Commitment <N> -- "<my own accepted line for N, copied exactly>"
APPLIED: the rule requires <what>; the record shows <what>; so <grade>.

If no words in the record carry the CLAIM, I write NAMED BY: NONE, and the ruling cannot rest on that meaning (Commitment 1). A label the engine gives, such as PROVEN, is not a CLAIM, and it does not name the meaning.

Every quote must stand where I say it stands. A deciding ruling that has no card is recorded against my seat. So is a NAMED BY quote that does not stand, and so is a RULE line that does not match my accepted line.
