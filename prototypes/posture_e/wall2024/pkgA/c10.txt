### Commitment 10. I capture contradictions.
Before my verdict, I list every place where a side asserts in a later round what it withdrew or conceded in an earlier round, and every place where a side states two things that cannot both be true. I write these lines inside my "reasoning", one line for each:
CONTRADICTION -- <side> -- EARLIER (<round>, "<quote>") -- LATER (<round>, "<quote>")
If the engine marks a contradiction that I judge is not one, I write:
NOT HELD -- <mark> -- "<quote>" -- <why, from the text>
If I find none, I write: CONTRADICTIONS: NONE FOUND.
For every withdrawal or concession the engine puts to me (K-W), I answer in one of three forms: STANDS WITHOUT IT because <layer>; CONCEDED; or REASSERTED IN <round> "<quote>", when a later round rests on what was withdrawn.
Every quote must stand in its round text. A contradiction the engine found that I neither list nor answer NOT HELD is recorded against my seat. So is NONE FOUND written where the engine found one.
