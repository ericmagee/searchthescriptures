# pkgWVR: a withdrawn verse cited again as a footing in the close (rule text)

Close Enforcer Build names a code candidate when a side, in round 2 or 3, withdraws a verse or stops resting on it ("I withdraw <verse>", "I drop the <verse> ...", "I set aside <verse>", "I drop any reliance on <verse>", "I do not / no longer rest ... on <verse>"), and its close cites the same verse in a sentence that makes it a footing ("layer of", "stands on", "rests on", "footing of/for") with no set-aside words. Where the verse opens the line as a short lead-in ("Galatians 4:4 -- D6.") the next sentence counts with it. The candidate reads "named by the code (pkgWVR)" and the enforcer decides whether the close rests again on what the earlier line gave up. The link is taken from the sentence, else from the nearest heading of the close.
Not named: a partial withdrawal ("by themselves", "alone", "as sufficient", "on its own", "of itself", "only as"); "drop the claim/limb/reading that <verse> ..." (the verse stays); a close that only answers a press on the verse or uses another verb ("carries"); a pair already named. At most 3 per side.
Limits: a footing stated with another word ("holds", "supports", "carries") is not found. A lead-in on the closing sentence's own line does not open its pkgCRB block (pkgCRB rev 2 rule); the block then starts at the heading above and still holds the sentence.
