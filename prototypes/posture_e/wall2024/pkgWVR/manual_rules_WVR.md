# pkgWVR rev 3: a withdrawn verse cited again as a footing in the close (rule text)

Close Enforcer Build names a code candidate when a side, in round 2 or 3, withdraws a verse or stops resting on it ("I withdraw <verse>", "I drop the <verse> ...", "I set aside <verse>", "I drop any reliance on <verse>", "I do not / no longer rest ... on <verse>"), and its close cites the same verse as a footing of the side's own link. Each verse of a list ("Genesis 3:15 and Galatians 4:4") and each verse of a range ("Matthew 1:18-20") counts. "I withdraw it" or "I drop them" takes the verses named earlier in the same clause (after the last semicolon), else, when the sentence names none, those of the sentence before; a verse named only as the place of a clause, phrase, word, reading or part ("the clause ... in Luke 1:27") is not taken. A list verse followed by "stays", "stands", "remains", "holds" or "is kept" is not withdrawn. A footing is "layer of" or "footing of/for" with the verse in the same clause, or "stands on" or "rests on" with the verse after the verb and no "not", "instead of" or "rather than" between. A shortened book name in the close counts when it is a prefix of the full name ("Gal. 4:4", "Phil. 1:6"; "Jude" is not "Judges", "Philippians" is not "Philemon"). Where the verse opens the line as a short lead-in ("Galatians 4:4 -- D6.") the next sentence counts with it. The candidate reads "named by the code (pkgWVR)" and the enforcer decides whether the close rests again on what the earlier line gave up. The link is taken from the sentence, else from the nearest heading of the close ("D6", "LINK 6").
Not named: a withdrawal inside a quotation, or after "if", "unless", "whether", "suppose", or a request ("asks that I withdraw") in the same stretch without a comma (so "Whether or not it matters, I withdraw ..." and "Since he asks, I withdraw ..." are withdrawals); a partial withdrawal ("by themselves", "alone", "as sufficient", "on its own", "of itself", "only as") right after the verse; "drop the claim/limb/reading that <verse> ..."; a close clause that reports the opponent ("The challenger says ..."), has the opponent as subject ("The challenger rests on ..."; not across "that", so "The challenger has not answered that D6 rests on ..." is named), or names only the opponent's link ("C2 rests on ..."); a past-tense report ("rested on"); "John 3:6" against "1 John 3:6"; a pair already named. At most 3 per side.
Named on purpose: a verse withdrawn in round 2 and taken up again in round 3, then cited in the close (the reinstatement is itself the same class; the enforcer decides).
Limits: a footing stated with another word ("holds", "supports", "carries") or in a heading list ("D4: KEPT (Isaiah 7:14; Galatians 4:4)") is not found; a bare "1:20" in the close is not found; a close range that contains the verse ("Matthew 1:18-25" for 1:20) is not found; a withdrawal scoped to one link is named against another link (the enforcer decides); a withdrawn range over 21 verses is cut at 21; a lone inch mark (6") before the withdrawal reads as an open quotation.
