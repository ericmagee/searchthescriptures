# pkgWVR rev 6: a withdrawn verse cited again as a footing in the close (rule text)

Close Enforcer Build names a code candidate when a side, in round 2 or 3, withdraws a verse or stops resting on it, and its close cites the same verse as a footing of the side's own link. The candidate reads "named by the code (pkgWVR)" and the enforcer decides whether the close rests again on what the earlier line gave up. Where the finder cannot decide a form by its words, it names the pair and the enforcer judges the quoted sentence.

Withdrawn: "I withdraw / drop / set aside / retract <verses>", "I drop any reliance on <verses>", "I do not / no longer rest ... on <verses>", with one adverb allowed after "I" (now, hereby, therefore, thus, also, then, so); "<verses>: withdrawn" and "<verses> stay(s) / remain(s) / is / are withdrawn". Each verse of a list and of a range counts. "it" or "them" takes the verses of its own clause (after the last semicolon); when that clause is bare ("...; I withdraw it.", "...; therefore I withdraw it."), the clause before; when nothing stands before "I" in its own sentence, the sentence before, when at most six words and no quotation stand before "I" in its own sentence. A verse named only as the place of a clause, phrase, word, reading, sense, part, idiom or gloss ("the clause ... in Luke 1:27") is not taken by "it".
Not withdrawn: inside a quotation (a " after a digit is not a quotation mark); a clause opened by if, unless, provided, should, or once (not "once more/again"), with "And", "But" or "Even" allowed before it, and not "If anything"; the clause before a bare "it" clause when it is so opened; "if / unless / whether I withdraw"; "asks / wants / urges ... that I withdraw"; a condition right after the verse ("I withdraw Galatians 4:4 if he shows a row."); a partial withdrawal right after the verse ("alone", "by itself", "as sufficient", "on its own", "of itself", "only as"); "drop the claim/limb/reading that <verse>"; a list verse followed by "stays", "stands", "remains", "holds" or "is kept".
Footing in the close: "layer of" or "footing of/for" with the verse in the same clause, or "stands on" / "rests on" with the verse after the verb and no "not", "instead of" or "rather than" between. Where the verse opens the line as a short lead-in ("Galatians 4:4 -- D6.") the next sentence counts with it. Not named: a question; a clause that reports the opponent ("The challenger says ..."), has the opponent as subject ("The challenger rests on ..."; not across "that"), or names only the opponent's link; a past-tense report ("rested on").
Verses: a book name matches when one name is a prefix of the other (at least three letters) or by the short forms Jn, Mt, Mk, Lk, Phil (Philippians), Philem/Phlm (Philemon), Jud (Jude), Judg (Judges); a numbered book must carry the same number ("John 3:6" is not "1 John 3:6"); a bare "1:20" takes the book named before it on the line, else matches any book; a range in the close holds each of its verses; a list of bare verse numbers after a chapter:verse ("Matthew 1:18, 20", "Matthew 1:16, 18 and 20", "Matthew 1:18; 20", "Matthew 1:18 & 20") is read as verses of the same chapter, on both sides, but not when a book name follows the number ("Luke 9:34, 1 Samuel 16:13"). A bare number followed by a lowercase word ("Matthew 1:18, 20 years") is still read as a verse; the enforcer clears it. A withdrawal with no "I" or "we" before the verb ("I keep Matthew 1:18 and withdraw 20") is not read. The link is taken from the sentence, else from the nearest heading of the close ("D6", "LINK 6"). At most 3 per side; a pair already named is not named again.
Named on purpose: a verse withdrawn in round 2 and taken up again in round 3, then cited in the close (W2 accepted, Wall 5337).
Limits: a footing stated with another word ("holds", "supports", "carries") or in a heading list ("D4: KEPT (...)") is not found; a withdrawal scoped to one link is named against another link (the enforcer decides); a verse cited only in a close heading line is not named; "<verse> stands and the <other> reading is weak. I withdraw it." is named; a condition the finder does not know (for example "Until ...", "In case ...") is named and left to the enforcer.
Regression set: pkgWVR/regress/ (233 plants from every grade, rev 1 to rev 5; three answers set by agreement in overrides.json); every revision passes all of them before it is posted.

Note to the enforcer (rev 9): each named pair carries a note for that pair only: if the earlier line withdrew the verse itself or stopped resting on it (also when withdrawn "as" a witness, "as load-bearing" or "as a proof") and the close names it again as a layer or footing of the same link, the close rests on it even with less weight ("not alone", "a supporting layer", "not weight-bearing"); a lesser weight is still a rest. The note does not apply where only a clause, phrase, word, idiom or reading of the verse was given up and the verse kept, nor to any other pair (from the operator's ruling on run 35379, ASK 116; narrowed after the ASK 124 replay).
