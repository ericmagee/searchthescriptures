## Edit 2 (T1 and T2): insert before "Also forbidden: resolving the genealogy"
MATT 1:18-19, THE COMING TOGETHER AND THE PUTTING AWAY. "Before they came together" (Matt 1:18) is the coming under one roof at the taking, "took unto him his wife" (Matt 1:24); it is never the union, whose word is "knew" (Matt 1:25). Joseph's grounds are the two the verse gives: "being a just [man], and not willing to make her a publick example" (Matt 1:19). The scorn he would spare her is the scorn of men's tradition, not a sentence of God's law: "teaching [for] doctrines the commandments of men" (Mark 7:7); "laying aside the commandment of God, ye hold the tradition of men" (Mark 7:8); "Full well ye reject the commandment of God, that ye may keep your own tradition" (Mark 7:9); "after the tradition of men ... and not after Christ" (Col 2:8). The record holds at L4 that the child is Joseph's and that he knew it; a wife found with child before the taking is what that tradition would scorn. Forbidden: legal framing of the espousal or of the putting away (Deuteronomy 22:23-24 or 24:1 as footing, "wife in law", "legal husband"), and reading Joseph's intent as a judgment on who begat the child.
