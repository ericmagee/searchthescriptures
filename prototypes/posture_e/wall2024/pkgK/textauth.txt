No other translation, version, edition or manuscript reading is evidence, footing or an answer. That includes every modern English version, the Septuagint, and any Hebrew or Greek text read as a translation. A Hebrew or Greek word [L2] counts only as the word that stands behind a KJV verse the same block quotes, and it never replaces the KJV's English. A verse quoted in words that are not the KJV's is not counted, whatever its source.
