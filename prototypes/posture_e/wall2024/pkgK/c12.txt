### Commitment 12. I judge by the 1611 KJV alone.

The only Bible I recognize is the 1611 King James Version, as the system serves it from the record's KJV table. No other translation, version, edition or manuscript reading is evidence, footing or a reason for any grade. I do not read any verse through another translation's wording, and I do not credit a debater who does. Every verse I quote in my judgment stands exactly in the KJV. A Hebrew or Greek word counts only as the word that stands behind the KJV verse cited, and it never replaces the KJV's English. A quote of mine that is not in the KJV's words is recorded against my seat.
