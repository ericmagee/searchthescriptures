A contradiction is two statements that cannot both be true. A closing sentence that asserts again, as the debater's own, what it withdrew, dropped or gave up in an earlier round IS a contradiction, however worded.
It is NOT a contradiction when the closing sentence: grants, concedes, narrows or withdraws something; states or quotes the other side's position; or answers a press with a further reason the earlier sentence leaves room for.
