# pkgCRB: the closing enforcer reads a closing sentence with its block (rule text)

Close Enforcer Build shows, under each named candidate, the CLOSING BLOCK that holds its closing sentence: the close round from the heading above the sentence (a markdown heading, a bold line, "LINK n", "ANSWER:", or "Dn:"/"Cn:"/"Xn:") to the next heading, at most 1,500 characters before the sentence and 300 after it. When several candidates share one closing sentence, the block is shown once and the later candidates read "CLOSING BLOCK: as under X<n>." The instruction adds one sentence: a closing sentence that draws its point from a row ("So", "Therefore", "Thus") asserts what that row says, so if the earlier sentence withdrew, dropped or rested nothing on that row, the close asserts it again.
Limits: a closing sentence that cannot be found in the close (fewer than three words, or reworded) gets no block. A body line that itself begins "LINK n" starts a new block. NEW items the enforcer finds itself carry no block (it reads the whole rounds as before).
