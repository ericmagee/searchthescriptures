# pkgCRB rev 2: the closing enforcer reads a closing sentence with its block (rule text)

Close Enforcer Build shows, under each named candidate, the CLOSING BLOCK that holds its closing quote. The whole quote must be found in the close round (marks ignored, curly and straight apostrophes treated alike); where it is found more than once, the hit not opened by a quotation mark is used. The block runs from the heading above the quote to the next heading, at most 1,500 characters before the quote and 300 after it. A heading is a markdown heading, a line that opens with a bold phrase other than a row label ("**[L3]**", "**[L4] ...**"), "LINK n", "ANSWER:", or "Dn:"/"Cn:"/"Xn:" (bold or not). A bold lead-in on the quote's own line ("**The point.** ... So ...") does not open its block. When several candidates share one closing quote, the block is shown once and the later candidates read "CLOSING BLOCK: as under X<n>." The instruction adds one sentence: a closing sentence that draws its point from a row ("So", "Therefore", "Thus") asserts what that row says, so if the earlier sentence withdrew, dropped or rested nothing on that row, the close asserts it again.
Limits: a closing quote not found whole in the close (fewer than three words, reworded or cut) gets no block. A body line that itself begins "LINK n" or "Dn:" starts a new block. NEW items the enforcer finds itself carry no block (it reads the whole rounds as before). Candidates the judges name (Judge Contradiction Build, its own prompt copy) get no block and no new sentence.
The new sentence is a prompt change: proven only by paid enforcer replies (run 35554 defender: X1 turns, X2 to X6 unchanged, no false NEW; run 35478 defender: X1 and X2 stay not contradictions; run 35554 challenger: no false NEW).
