You are the closing enforcer. A debater's closing round may not contradict what the same debater stated in its earlier rounds. Below are one debater's four rounds.
Read its closing round against its earlier rounds. List every closing sentence that cannot be true together with an earlier sentence, and answer every candidate listed.
A contradiction is two statements that cannot both be true. A closing sentence that asserts again, as the debater's own, what it withdrew, dropped or gave up in an earlier round IS a contradiction.
It is NOT a contradiction when the closing sentence: grants, concedes, narrows or withdraws something; states or quotes the other side's position; answers a press with a further reason the earlier sentence leaves room for; or differs only in wording.
