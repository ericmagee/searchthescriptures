**Footing.**

- [L3] Ezra 2:62: "These sought their register [among] those that were reckoned by genealogy, but they were not found: therefore were they, as polluted, put from the priesthood. [were they...: Heb. they were polluted from the priesthood]". [L2] *kathab* (H3791, "register") and *yachas* (H3187, "to enroll by pedigree").
- [L3] Numbers 1:18: "they declared their pedigrees after their families, by the house of their fathers". [L2] *yalad* (H3205, "to beget ... to show lineage ... declare pedigrees").
- [L4] Ezra 2:59 names what the register shows: the father's house and the seed, which the margin calls pedigree. Ezra 2:62 calls the register an enrolment "by genealogy". Numbers 1:18 declares the pedigree with the begetting verb *yalad*, by the father's house. So the register is a register of seed by the father's house.
