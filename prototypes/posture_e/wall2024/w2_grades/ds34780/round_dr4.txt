## My position at the close

**Status of my chain.** C1 to C6 are kept, as written in my link round.

### The seed is David's, out of a man's body

- [L1] Romans 1:3: "which was made of the seed of David according to the flesh". [L2] *sperma* (G4690), "something sown, i.e. seed (including the male 'sperm')".
- [L3] 2 Samuel 7:12: "I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels". Acts 2:30: "of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne". [L2] *osphys* (G3751), "procreative power".
- [L4] The promise names the seed's way: out of the bowels, out of the loins, according to the flesh. The defender granted it: "the promise runs by descent from David's body" (G13).

### The line of fathers reaches Joseph, and Joseph is named father

- [L1] Luke 2:4: "because he was of the house and lineage of David".
- [L3] Matthew 1:2: "Abraham begat Isaac", with the begetting line granted to run to Joseph (G14). Luke 2:48: "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing". John 6:42: "Is not this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know?"
- [L4] Mary herself, the town and the narrator ("the parents," Luke 2:27; "his parents," Luke 2:41) name one man. The reckoning is "by the house of their fathers" (Numbers 1:18), and Hebrews 7:14, "evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda," is evident in exactly that reckoning.

### The union inside the espousal, and the timing rows read in order

- [L1] Matthew 1:19: "Joseph her husband". Matthew 1:20: "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost. [conceived: Gr. begotten]". Deuteronomy 22:24: "his neighbour's wife".
- [L3] Genesis 4:1: "Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived".
- [L4] They were husband and wife from the espousal, and the canon's stated means of begetting is knowing. On that footing, Joseph knew Mary his wife within the espousal, and the child is of his loins.

The rows hold that union in this order:

1. Matthew 1:18 states "before they came together, she was found with child". The defender granted that this coming together is the taking of Matthew 1:24, "took unto him his wife" (G4), and Deuteronomy 20:7 sets the taking apart from the betrothal (G43). So 1:18 dates the finding before the taking.
2. Matthew 1:25 states "knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son". The defender granted that it "bounds the abstinence after the taking" (G24).
3. Luke 1:34 states "How shall this be, seeing I know not a man?" It is her present state at the angel's word, and the conception of Luke 1:31 was future to that word (G25).

Read in that order, the espousal before the taking is the place the knowing stands, and the three timing rows stand as written.

### "Of the Holy Ghost" is origin; the verbs are presence

- [L1] Luke 1:35: "The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee". [L2] *eperchomai* (G1904), to come upon.
- [L3] Acts 1:8: "ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you". Luke 9:34: "there came a cloud, and overshadowed them". 1 John 5:1: "every one that loveth him that begat loveth him also that is begotten of him".
- [L4] The coming upon is power for witness, the overshadowing is the presence cloud, and the *ek* of Matthew 1:20 is the origin-preposition of the "born of God" rows (G6).

### "Son of God" is the covenant and resurrection title

- [L1] Luke 1:32: "He shall be great, and shall be called the Son of the Highest: and the Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David".
- [L3] 1 Chronicles 28:6: "Solomon thy son ... I have chosen him [to be] my son, and I will be his father". Psalms 2:7: "Thou [art] my Son; this day have I begotten thee", applied at the raising (Acts 13:33), at the priesthood (Hebrews 5:5), and beside "I will be to him a Father" (Hebrews 1:5). Romans 1:4: "declared [to be] the Son of God with power ... by the resurrection from the dead".
- [L4] The "therefore also" (*dio kai*) of Luke 1:35 connects back to Luke 1:32, where the title and the throne are given before Mary asks "How shall this be". The defender granted that order (G35), and granted that Solomon bore the title while being David's son (G45).

### He was made like his brethren

- [L1] Hebrews 2:14: "he also himself likewise took part of the same".
- [L3] Hebrews 2:16: "but he took on [him] the seed of Abraham. [took not...: Gr. taketh not hold of angels, but of the seed of Abraham he taketh hold]". Hebrews 2:17: "in all things it behoved him to be made like unto [his] brethren".
- [L4] He took part of flesh and blood "likewise". He came as his brethren came: of the seed of David according to the flesh, the son of Joseph, born of Mary his wife.

**My answer to the subject.** Yes. Christ had a biological father: Joseph, the named son of David, of whose loins came the promised seed according to the flesh. He is the Son of God by covenant, and declared so with power by the resurrection from the dead.
