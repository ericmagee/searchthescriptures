For each assumed fact it answers STATED (with the block's own quoted verse words), NOT STATED, or INFERRED ACT (never an ASSUMES, DG-61). Code is the second reader: every proper name of the fact must stand among the block's quoted verse words, read together (Commitment 3). The judges' grades are read after the panel and before the cure, so a grade changed at the cure is recorded as first given.
