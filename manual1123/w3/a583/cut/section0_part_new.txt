
## 0. How to read this manual
- The manual is held in parts: table `engine_manual_parts`, one row per part per version of that part. `SELECT engine_manual_toc()` lists the parts. `SELECT engine_manual_part('<part>')` gives one part; `SELECT * FROM engine_manual_full()` gives the whole text and its version. The manual's version is the highest part version. The revision history is `engine_manual_log`; its column `part` names the part a version changed. The parts are read in the order `engine_manual_toc()` gives. The table `engine_manual` holds the whole-text versions up to the cut and is not written again.
- This manual follows the body law of operational_doc §0 (body law): rules and the mechanics needed to follow them; no provenance in the body; one home per rule and per changeable value. Nothing in this manual is certified; the operator certifies.
- The live record corrects this manual. Where this manual and the live flow or table disagree, the live flow or table is right. Read the node code before acting on any sentence here.
- Source of truth is SQL. The Drive copy is a mirror written by the Daily Drive Sync (operational_doc §7, Daily Drive Sync); never edit it. To revise: call engine_manual_part_apply on the one part (engine_manual_apply refuses now that the manual is in parts) in the same batch as SET LOCAL truth.session_id and SET LOCAL truth.card = '<card label> <press time>Z <thread seat>' (ASK 477). First preview, with p_write false: it refuses unless the base is that part's latest version and has the given md5, and unless each pair's old text occurs exactly once when that pair is applied; it returns the new md5, length and per-pair counts. Then write with p_write true and that md5 and length: it inserts that part at the manual's highest version + 1 with its summary, and its `engine_manual_log` row dated by the Central date. Readers preview the same pairs and compare the md5; after the write, the readback is mrb.cjs (ASK 420(b)). EXECUTE is revoked from PUBLIC, anon and authenticated. Limit: an old text that overlaps itself is counted once.
- New text enters only through engine_manual_part_apply on an existing part. A part that has grown is divided in two with engine_manual_part_split, at a text that occurs once in it, with no change of text; no part and no size is refused.
- Every insert forces an audit_log row (engine_manual_guard, AFTER INSERT): version, md5, length, and since ASK 477 card=<truth.card or NONE>, session=<truth.session_id or NONE> and from=v<prior version> hash=<prior md5 12>. It is record only: a write without truth.card still succeeds and is marked card=NONE. The card field is the writer's claim, not proof of a press; the press is the card in the writer's thread.
- Retired flows (the W1 and Defends-vs-Claude families, DS-W1, the Kimi flows and the other archived debate flows): engine_manual v84. Their rows stand in `debate_results` as history and are read by their own variant (`debate_variant_canon`).
