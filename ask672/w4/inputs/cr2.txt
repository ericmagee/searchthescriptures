**Reply to D4 on born of flesh and Spirit**
[L1] "That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit." (John 3:6) [L2] G1080 (*gennao*, "born" in John 3:6): procreate, bear, beget, be born [L3] "But while he thought on these things, behold, the angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a dream, saying, Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost." (Matthew 1:20) [L4] John divides born of flesh and born of Spirit, and Matthew says that begotten in her is of the Holy Ghost, so Matthew places this begetting on the Spirit side of John's division [L1][L2][L3].

**Reply to D5 on knew her not**
[L1] "And knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son: and he called his name JESUS." **Reply on virgin with child applied to this birth**
[L1] "Behold, a virgin shall be with child, and shall bring forth a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, which being interpreted is, God with us." (Matthew 1:23) [L2] G3933 (*parthenos*, "virgin" in Matthew 1:23): virgin, maiden [L3] "To a virgin espoused to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin's name was Mary." (Luke 1:27) [L4] Matthew applies virgin shall be with child to this birth, and Luke calls Mary virgin espoused to Joseph, so the record joins virgin to with child in this account [L1][L2][L3].
