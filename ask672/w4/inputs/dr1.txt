### WHAT BIOLOGICAL MEANS: SEED OUT OF THE LOINS

[L2] G4690 (*sperma*, "seed" at Romans 1:3): seed, seeds, issue.
[L2] H2504 (*chalats*, "loins" at Genesis 35:11, 1 Kings 8:19, and 2 Chronicles 6:9): loins.
[L2] G3751 (*osphys*, "loins" at Acts 2:30): the loin (externally), i.e. the hip; internally (by extension) procreative power.

DECLARED CHAIN

1.
