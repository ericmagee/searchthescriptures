1. THE SPIRIT COMES UPON A MAN AND HE PROPHESIES OR SPEAKS.
- 1 Samuel 10:6 "the Spirit of the LORD will come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, and shalt be turned into another man." 10:10; 19:23.
- Numbers 11:25 "when the spirit rested upon them, they prophesied". 11:26, 11:29. Numbers 24:2 (Balaam).
- 2 Chronicles 15:1; 24:20 "the Spirit of God came upon Zechariah ... and said unto them, Thus saith God". 1 Chronicles 12:18. Ezekiel 11:5 "the Spirit of the LORD fell upon me, and said unto me, Speak".
- Joel 2:28 "I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy".

2. THE SPIRIT COMES UPON A MAN AND HE JUDGES, DELIVERS OR IS MADE STRONG.
- Judges 3:10 "the Spirit of the LORD came upon him, and he judged Israel, and went out to war". 6:34 (Gideon; margin "[came...: Heb. clothed]"). 11:29 (Jephthah). 14:6, 14:19, 15:14 (Samson). 1 Samuel 11:6 (Saul).

3. THE SPIRIT COMES WITH THE ANOINTING OF THE KING.
- 1 Samuel 16:13 "Then Samuel took the horn of oil, and anointed him in the midst of his brethren: and the Spirit of the LORD came upon David from that day forward."
- Isaiah 61:1 "The Spirit of the Lord GOD [is] upon me; because the LORD hath anointed me". Luke 4:18 gives the same with pneuma. Acts 10:38 "God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power".
- Isaiah 11:2 "the spirit of the LORD shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding". Isaiah 42:1 "I have put my spirit upon him: he shall bring forth judgment to the Gentiles."

4. THE SPIRIT FILLS A MAN WITH WISDOM FOR A WORK.
- Exodus 31:3 "I have filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship". 35:31; 28:3. Deuteronomy 34:9 (Joshua).

5. THE SPIRIT IS PUT UPON OR WITHIN THE PEOPLE AND THEIR SEED, BY COVENANT.
- Isaiah 59:21 "this [is] my covenant with them, saith the LORD; My spirit that [is] upon thee, and my words which I have put in thy mouth, shall not depart out of thy mouth, nor out of the mouth of thy seed".
- Isaiah 44:3 "I will pour my spirit upon thy seed, and my blessing upon thine offspring".
- Ezekiel 36:27 "I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes". 37:14; 39:29. Isaiah 63:11 "he that put his holy Spirit within him". Isaiah 32:15. Zechariah 12:10.

6. THE SAME IN LUKE 1 AND 2, AROUND LUKE 1:35.
- Luke 1:15, of John: "he shall be filled with the Holy Ghost, even from his mother's womb."
- Luke 1:35, to Mary: "The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee".
- Luke 1:41 "the babe leaped in her womb; and Elisabeth was filled with the Holy Ghost". 1:67 "Zacharias was filled with the Holy Ghost, and prophesied".
- Luke 2:25 "the Holy Ghost was upon him" (Simeon); 2:26.
- Acts 1:8 "ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses". Acts 2:4 "they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak".
- John has a named father (Luke 1:67 "his father Zacharias") and is filled with the Holy Ghost from the womb (Luke 1:15).
