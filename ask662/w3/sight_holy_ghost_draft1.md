The Holy Ghost Is the Spirit: Ruach and Pneuma Are Wind, Breath, Spirit; "Ghost" and "Spirit" Are One Greek Word; What the Spirit Does When It Comes Upon a Man

PROPOSED SUMMARY (the text that would be column summary):

The Spirit at the floor, rows first (kjv_word_map, lexicon_words, kjv_verses, pulled live 2026-11-25). Rows read are named in each part; the ruach and pneuma rows not named here were not read for this sight.
(1) THE WORD (L2). The Hebrew word is ruach (H7307). The lexicon gives it as "wind; by resemblance breath ... by resemblance spirit". The Greek word is pneuma (G4151): "a current of air, i.e. breath (blast) or a breeze; by analogy or figuratively, a spirit", rendered in the KJV "ghost, life, spirit(-ual, -ually), mind". "Holy" is qodesh (H6944) in Hebrew and hagios (G40) in Greek.
(2) COUNTS. ruach H7307, 378 rows: spirit 227, wind 81, breath 28, winds 11, and smaller counts of spirits, side, mind, blast, vain, courage, anger, air, quarters, tempest, windy, cool, whirlwind. pneuma G4151, 384 rows: spirit 254, ghost 91, spirits 31, and wind, life, spiritually, spiritual and a few others once each. "Ghost" never renders ruach.
(3) "HOLY GHOST" AND "HOLY SPIRIT" ARE ONE PHRASE (L1, L2). The canon reads "Holy Ghost" 90 times in 88 verses, every one in the New Testament, every one pneuma. The same two Greek words, hagios and pneuma, read "holy Spirit" at Luke 11:13 "how much more shall [your] heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him?", Ephesians 1:13 "that holy Spirit of promise", Ephesians 4:30 "grieve not the holy Spirit of God" and 1 Thessalonians 4:8 "his holy Spirit". The Old Testament does not read "Holy Ghost". It reads "holy spirit", ruach with qodesh, three times: Psalms 51:11 "take not thy holy spirit from me", Isaiah 63:10 "they rebelled, and vexed his holy Spirit", Isaiah 63:11 "where [is] he that put his holy Spirit within him?". "Ghost" is not a second thing beside "Spirit"; it is the same word in another English dress. John 19:30 "gave up the ghost" is the same pneuma.
(4) ONE WORD, WIND AND SPIRIT (L1, L2). John 3:8 holds pneuma twice in one sentence: "The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh, and whither it goeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit." (Sight 1171; Sight 1175 on John 6:63.) 2 Kings 19:7 "a blast" is ruach (Sight 1728). Genesis 1:2 "And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters" is ruach.
(5) THE KJV JOINS RUACH AND PNEUMA ITSELF (L1, L3). Isaiah 61:1 "The Spirit of the Lord GOD [is] upon me; because the LORD hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek" is ruach. Luke 4:18 gives it with pneuma: "The Spirit of the Lord [is] upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor". Joel 2:28 "I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy" is ruach. Acts 2:17 gives it with pneuma: "I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh: and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy". The Holy Ghost of the New Testament is the Spirit of the LORD of the Old.
(6) WHAT THE SPIRIT DOES WHEN IT COMES UPON, FILLS OR IS PUT UPON A PERSON (L1, L3; 61 Old Testament verses read, the query held in ask662/w3/spirit_comes_upon_rows_1908.md).
- He prophesies or speaks. 1 Samuel 10:6 "And the Spirit of the LORD will come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, and shalt be turned into another man." Numbers 11:25 "when the spirit rested upon them, they prophesied". 2 Chronicles 15:1; 24:20. Ezekiel 11:5 "And the Spirit of the LORD fell upon me, and said unto me, Speak". Numbers 11:29 "would God that all the LORD'S people were prophets, [and] that the LORD would put his spirit upon them!"
- He judges, delivers, is made strong. Judges 3:10 "And the Spirit of the LORD came upon him, and he judged Israel, and went out to war". Judges 6:34 "the Spirit of the LORD came upon Gideon", margin "[came...: Heb. clothed]". Judges 11:29; 14:6; 14:19; 15:14. 1 Samuel 11:6.
- He is filled with wisdom for a work. Exodus 31:3 "And I have filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship". Exodus 28:3; 35:31. Deuteronomy 34:9.
- The king is anointed and the Spirit comes. 1 Samuel 16:13 "Then Samuel took the horn of oil, and anointed him in the midst of his brethren: and the Spirit of the LORD came upon David from that day forward." Isaiah 11:2 "And the spirit of the LORD shall rest upon him". Isaiah 42:1 "I have put my spirit upon him: he shall bring forth judgment to the Gentiles." Isaiah 61:1 (part 5). Acts 10:38 "How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power".
- It is the covenant gift to the people and their seed. Isaiah 59:21 "As for me, this [is] my covenant with them, saith the LORD; My spirit that [is] upon thee, and my words which I have put in thy mouth, shall not depart out of thy mouth, nor out of the mouth of thy seed". Isaiah 44:3 "I will pour my spirit upon thy seed, and my blessing upon thine offspring". Ezekiel 36:27 "And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes". Ezekiel 37:14; 39:29.
In these rows no verse speaks of a begetting.
(7) THE SAME SPIRIT IN LUKE 1 AND 2 AND IN ACTS (L1). Luke 1:15, of John: "he shall be filled with the Holy Ghost, even from his mother's womb." His father is named: Luke 1:67 "And his father Zacharias was filled with the Holy Ghost, and prophesied". Luke 1:41 "the babe leaped in her womb; and Elisabeth was filled with the Holy Ghost".
