THE WORDS [L2].
- mashach (H4886), "to rub with oil, i.e. to anoint; by implication, to consecrate": 69 rows.
- mashiach (H4899), "anointed; usually a consecrated person (as a king, priest, or saint); specifically, the Messiah": 39 rows, rendered anointed 37 and Messiah 2.
- chrio (G5548), "to smear or rub with oil, i.e. (by implication) to consecrate to an office or religious service": 5 rows, all "anointed": Luke 4:18, Acts 4:27, Acts 10:38, 2 Corinthians 1:21, Hebrews 1:9.
- Christos (G5547), "anointed, i.e. the Messiah, an epithet of Jesus": 567 rows by exact tag (569 with compound tags), rendered Christ. The lexicon derives it from chrio.
- chrisma (G5545): 3 rows, "unction" and "anointing", at 1 John 2:20 and 2:27.
- Messias (G3323): 2 rows. John 1:41 "We have found the Messias, which is, being interpreted, the Christ. [the Christ: or, the Anointed]". The 1611 margin gives "the Anointed" for "the Christ".

ROWS READ.
A. Five verses hold an anoint word and ruach or pneuma together (join of kjv_word_map on both tags):
- 1 Samuel 16:13 "Then Samuel took the horn of oil, and anointed him in the midst of his brethren: and the Spirit of the LORD came upon David from that day forward."
- Isaiah 61:1 "The Spirit of the Lord GOD [is] upon me; because the LORD hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek"
- Luke 4:18 "The Spirit of the Lord [is] upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor"
- Acts 10:38 "How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil; for God was with him."
- Lamentations 4:20 "The breath of our nostrils, the anointed of the LORD, was taken in their pits, of whom we said, Under his shadow we shall live among the heathen." Breath is H7307, nostrils H639, anointed H4899.
B. The anointing and then the Spirit, in the next verses:
- Saul. 1 Samuel 10:1 "Then Samuel took a vial of oil, and poured [it] upon his head"; 10:6 "And the Spirit of the LORD will come upon thee"; 10:10 "and the Spirit of God came upon him, and he prophesied among them." And the Spirit leaves the rejected king: 1 Samuel 16:14 "But the Spirit of the LORD departed from Saul".
- David. 1 Samuel 16:1 "I will send thee to Jesse the Bethlehemite: for I have provided me a king among his sons."; 16:12 "Arise, anoint him: for this [is] he."; 16:13 above. His last words: 2 Samuel 23:1 "David the son of Jesse said, and the man [who was] raised up on high, the anointed of the God of Jacob"; 23:2 "The Spirit of the LORD spake by me, and his word [was] in my tongue."
- Elisha, whose rows are stated as they stand: the anointing is commanded and its doing is not told, and the spirit named is Elijah's. 1 Kings 19:16 "Elisha the son of Shaphat of Abelmeholah shalt thou anoint [to be] prophet in thy room."; 2 Kings 2:15 "The spirit of Elijah doth rest on Elisha." "Rest" there is H5117, the word of Isaiah 11:2 "shall rest upon him" and of Numbers 11:25 "when the spirit rested upon them".
C. The Branch of Jesse. Isaiah 11:1 "And there shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Jesse, and a Branch shall grow out of his roots:" 11:2 "And the spirit of the LORD shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the LORD;" The sentence runs to 11:4, "and with the breath of his lips shall he slay the wicked", where breath is ruach again. The word anoint is not in Isaiah 11:1-5.
D. The anointed king is called Son. Psalms 2:2 "against the LORD, and against his anointed"; 2:6 "Yet have I set my king upon my holy hill of Zion. [set: Heb. anointed]"; 2:7 "Thou [art] my Son; this day have I begotten thee." Acts 4:26 quotes it, "against the Lord, and against his Christ.", and 4:27 applies it: "For of a truth against thy holy child Jesus, whom thou hast anointed". Psalms 89:20 "I have found David my servant; with my holy oil have I anointed him:"; 89:36 "His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me."
E. He is called Christ at his birth. Luke 2:11 "For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord." Luke 2:26 "that he should not see death, before he had seen the Lord's Christ.", revealed "by the Holy Ghost", and 2:27 "when the parents brought in the child Jesus". Matthew 2:4 "he demanded of them where Christ should be born."
F. The anointing in the people. 2 Corinthians 1:21 "Now he which stablisheth us with you in Christ, and hath anointed us, [is] God;" 1:22 "Who hath also sealed us, and given the earnest of the Spirit in our hearts." 1 John 2:27 "the same anointing teacheth you of all things, and is truth"; John 14:26, of the Comforter, "he shall teach you all things".
G. An anointed one who is not of Israel. Isaiah 45:1 "Thus saith the LORD to his anointed, to Cyrus".

THE READING, EACH LINE MARKED.
R1 [L2]. "Christ" is the Greek word for "anointed", from the verb chrio; the 1611 margin at John 1:41 says so. Every "Jesus Christ" in the New Testament is "Jesus the Anointed".
R2 [L1]. Where Scripture says with what Jesus was anointed, it says "with the Holy Ghost and with power" (Acts 10:38), and he reads of himself "The Spirit of the Lord [is] upon me, because he hath anointed me" (Luke 4:18).
R3 [L1, L3]. The pattern of the anointed in the rows of B, for Saul and David: the man is named with his father (Saul the son of Kish at 1 Samuel 9:1-2, David the son of Jesse), he is anointed, and the Spirit of the LORD comes upon him. Elisha the son of Shaphat is set beside them only: his anointing is commanded, and what rests on him is called "The spirit of Elijah". 2 Samuel 23:1-2 holds all three of David in two verses.
R4 [L3]. Isaiah 11:1-2 gives the same of the Branch: out of Jesse, and the Spirit of the LORD resting on him. That this resting is his anointing is a join through Isaiah 61:1 and 1 Samuel 16:13, not a word of Isaiah 11.
R5 [L3]. The child is called Christ on the day he is born (Luke 2:11); Herod asks "where Christ should be born" (Matthew 2:4, after the birth at 2:1); and Matthew opens "Now the birth of Jesus Christ was on this wise" (1:18) in the verse that says "of the Holy Ghost".
R6 [L4, a reading]. Read with these rows, "that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost" tells Joseph that the child is the Anointed, the one on whom the Spirit of the LORD rests. This is the reading recorded at Wall 8003 (19:55 CST 2026-11-17: "'that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost.' translation: the child in her is anointed") and DG-8's "anointing marker"; the rows above are the passages it stands on.
R7 [L4]. Lamentations 4:20 names the anointed king with the words "The breath of our nostrils", ruach and mashiach in one phrase: the people speak of their anointed as their breath.
