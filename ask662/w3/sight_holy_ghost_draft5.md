(1) THE WORD [L2]. The Hebrew word is ruach (H7307). The lexicon gives it as "wind; by resemblance breath", and further on "by resemblance spirit". The Greek word is pneuma (G4151): "a current of air, i.e. breath (blast) or a breeze; by analogy or figuratively, a spirit", rendered in the KJV "ghost, life, spirit(-ual, -ually), mind". Beside ruach stands neshamah (H5397), "a puff, i.e. wind, angry or vital breath". "Holy" is qodesh (H6944) in Hebrew and hagios (G40) in Greek.
(2) COUNTS. ruach H7307, 378 rows: spirit 227, wind 81, breath 28, winds 11, and smaller counts of spirits, side, mind, blast, vain, courage, anger, air, quarters, tempest, windy, cool, whirlwind. pneuma G4151, 384 rows tagged in kjv_word_map: spirit 255, ghost 91, spirits 32, and wind, life, spiritually, spiritual and a few others once each. Matthew 27:50 "yielded up the ghost" is pneuma in original_words and is not among the 91. Every "ghost" that names the Spirit is pneuma; no ruach row is rendered "ghost".
(3) "HOLY GHOST" AND "HOLY SPIRIT" ARE ONE PHRASE [L1, L2]. The canon reads "Holy Ghost" 90 times in 88 verses, every one in the New Testament, every one pneuma. The same two Greek words, hagios and pneuma, read "holy Spirit" at Luke 11:13 "how much more shall [your] heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him?", Ephesians 1:13 "that holy Spirit of promise", Ephesians 4:30 "grieve not the holy Spirit of God" and 1 Thessalonians 4:8 "his holy Spirit". The Old Testament reads "holy spirit", ruach with qodesh, three times: Psalms 51:11 "take not thy holy spirit from me", Isaiah 63:10 "they rebelled, and vexed his holy Spirit", Isaiah 63:11 "where [is] he that put his holy Spirit within him?". One verse holds both English names for the one word, Acts 2:4: "And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance." At Matthew 12:31 "Holy" is a word the translators supplied: "but the blasphemy [against] the [Holy] Ghost shall not be forgiven unto men"; the Greek there has the Spirit, with no word for "Holy".
(4) THE BREATH OF LIFE IN MAN [L1, L2, L3].
- Genesis 2:7 "And the LORD God formed man [of] the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul." Breath is neshamah.
- Job sets ruach beside neshamah. Job 33:4 "The Spirit of God hath made me, and the breath of the Almighty hath given me life." Job 27:3 "All the while my breath [is] in me, and the spirit of God [is] in my nostrils; [the spirit...: that is, the breath which God gave him]". The 1611 margin at Job 27:3 names the spirit of God in a man's nostrils "the breath which God gave him". "Nostrils" (H639) is the word of Genesis 2:7. [L3]: Job 33:4 joins Genesis 2:7 by "breath" (neshamah, H5397, in both).
- The men who say this are men born. Job 32:2 "Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite". Job 3:3 "There is a man child conceived." Job 31:15 "Did not he that made me in the womb make him? and did not one fashion us in the womb?"
- Ezekiel 37:1-14: ruach stands ten times, rendered spirit, breath, wind and winds, with the margin at 37:9 "[wind: or, breath]". The flesh comes first with no life in it: 37:8 "the sinews and the flesh came up upon them, and the skin covered them above: but [there was] no breath in them." It lives when the ruach enters: 37:10 "the breath came into them, and they lived". "breathe upon these slain" (37:9) is the verb of Genesis 2:7 "breathed" (H5301). The breath of 37:5-10 is named at 37:14: "And shall put my spirit in you, and ye shall live". The vision is of a people: 37:11 "these bones are the whole house of Israel" (Sight 564).
- Sent, they are created; taken, they die. Psalms 104:30 "Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created". Psalms 104:29 "thou takest away their breath, they die, and return to their dust." Both are ruach. Ecclesiastes 12:7 "Then shall the dust return to the earth as it was: and the spirit shall return unto God who gave it."
- To give up the ghost is the breath going out. Luke 23:46 "Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit: and having said thus, he gave up the ghost." Genesis 25:8 "Then Abraham gave up the ghost, and died"; the lexicon gives the Hebrew verb as "to breathe out". "They die" at Psalms 104:29 is that same verb. At John 19:30 and Matthew 27:50 "the ghost" is pneuma itself. James 2:26 "For as the body without the spirit is dead", margin "[spirit: or, breath]".
(5) THE WIND [L1, L2, L3]. Genesis 1:2 "And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters" is ruach, its first row (Sight 897 on "moved"). Ruach on the waters: Genesis 8:1 "God made a wind to pass over the earth, and the waters asswaged"; Exodus 14:21 "the LORD caused the sea to go [back] by a strong east wind"; Exodus 15:8 "And with the blast of thy nostrils the waters were gathered together"; Exodus 15:10 "Thou didst blow with thy wind"; Psalms 147:18 "he causeth his wind to blow, [and] the waters flow." The 1611 margin at Genesis 3:8: "[cool: Heb. wind]". John 3:8 holds pneuma twice in one sentence, with the verb pneuma comes from: "The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh, and whither it goeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit." (Sight 1171; Sight 1175 on John 6:63.) Acts 2:2 "And suddenly there came a sound from heaven as of a rushing mighty wind"; that word for wind comes from the same verb as pneuma. [L2]: Ruach is one word for wind, breath and spirit; so is pneuma (John 3:8).
(6) THE NEW TESTAMENT NAMES THE SPIRIT OF THE LORD THE HOLY GHOST [L1, L3]. Isaiah 61:1 "The Spirit of the Lord GOD [is] upon me; because the LORD hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek" is ruach. Luke 4:18 gives it with pneuma: "The Spirit of the Lord [is] upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor". Joel 2:28 "I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy" is ruach. Acts 2:17 gives it with pneuma: "I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh: and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy". The text names that Spirit the Holy Ghost in each place. Acts 2:4 "And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost", and Peter of that same hour, Acts 2:16: "But this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel". Luke 4:1 "And Jesus being full of the Holy Ghost returned from Jordan, and was led by the Spirit into the wilderness", and after the reading of Isaiah, Luke 4:21: "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears." Of prophecy, 2 Peter 1:21: "holy men of God spake [as they were] moved by the Holy Ghost.", where "in old time" carries the margin "[in old time: or, at any time]"; David of himself, 2 Samuel 23:2: "The Spirit of the LORD spake by me, and his word [was] in my tongue." In one account Peter names the one lied to three ways: Acts 5:3 "to lie to the Holy Ghost"; Acts 5:4 "thou hast not lied unto men, but unto God."; Acts 5:9 "to tempt the Spirit of the Lord". [L3]: The Holy Ghost of the New Testament is the Spirit of the LORD of the Old.
(7) IT PROCEEDETH FROM THE FATHER AND IS HIS [L1]. John 15:26 "But when the Comforter is come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, [even] the Spirit of truth, which proceedeth from the Father, he shall testify of me:". The Son sends it from the Father; it proceeds from the Father (Sight 1198). Matthew 10:20 "For it is not ye that speak, but the Spirit of your Father which speaketh in you." Acts 2:33 "having received of the Father the promise of the Holy Ghost, he hath shed forth this, which ye now see and hear." Acts 2:38 "the gift of the Holy Ghost"; Acts 2:39 "For the promise is unto you, and to your children".
(8) IT KNOWS THE THINGS OF GOD, TEACHES, AND SPEAKS WHAT IT HEARS [L1, L3]. 1 Corinthians 2:10 "But God hath revealed [them] unto us by his Spirit: for the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God." 1 Corinthians 2:11 "For what man knoweth the things of a man, save the spirit of man which is in him? even so the things of God knoweth no man, but the Spirit of God." [L3]: where Isaiah 40:13 has "Who hath directed the Spirit of the LORD, or [being] his counsellor hath taught him?", Romans 11:34 has "For who hath known the mind of the Lord? or who hath been his counsellor?"

(10) THE POWER OF THE HIGHEST [L1, L2]. Luke 1:35 "And the angel answered and said unto her, The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God." Acts 1:8 "But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me", margin "[power...: or, the power of the Holy Ghost coming upon you]". The two verses share the Greek for "Holy", "Ghost", "come", "upon" and "power" [L2]. "Overshadow" (G1982, "to cast a shade upon") stands in five verses: a cloud in three (Matthew 17:5, Mark 9:7, Luke 9:34), Peter's shadow in one (Acts 5:15), the power of the Highest in Luke 1:35. In Matthew 17:5 the overshadowing cloud carries the voice "This is my beloved Son"; in Luke 1:35 the overshadowing is followed by "shall be called the Son of God". Micah 3:8 "But truly I am full of power by the spirit of the LORD". Zechariah 4:6 "Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts." Psalms 139:7 sets his spirit beside his presence in parallel lines: "Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee from thy presence?"
(11) WHAT THE SPIRIT DOES WHEN IT COMES UPON, FILLS OR IS PUT UPON A PERSON [L1, L3; 61 Old Testament verses read, the query held in ask662/w3/spirit_comes_upon_rows_1908.md].
- He prophesies or speaks. 1 Samuel 10:6 "And the Spirit of the LORD will come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, and shalt be turned into another man." Numbers 11:25 "when the spirit rested upon them, they prophesied". 2 Chronicles 15:1; 24:20. Ezekiel 11:5 "And the Spirit of the LORD fell upon me, and said unto me, Speak". Numbers 11:29 "would God that all the LORD'S people were prophets, [and] that the LORD would put his spirit upon them!"
- He judges, delivers, is made strong. Judges 3:10 "And the Spirit of the LORD came upon him, and he judged Israel, and went out to war". Judges 6:34 "the Spirit of the LORD came upon Gideon", margin "[came...: Heb. clothed]". Judges 11:29; 14:6; 14:19; 15:14. 1 Samuel 11:6.
- He is filled with wisdom for a work. Exodus 31:3 "And I have filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship". Exodus 28:3; 35:31. Deuteronomy 34:9.
- The king is anointed and the Spirit comes. 1 Samuel 16:13 "Then Samuel took the horn of oil, and anointed him in the midst of his brethren: and the Spirit of the LORD came upon David from that day forward." Isaiah 42:1 "I have put my spirit upon him: he shall bring forth judgment to the Gentiles." Isaiah 61:1 (part 6). Acts 10:38 "How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power".
- It is the covenant gift to the people and their seed. Isaiah 59:21 "As for me, this [is] my covenant with them, saith the LORD; My spirit that [is] upon thee, and my words which I have put in thy mouth, shall not depart out of thy mouth, nor out of the mouth of thy seed". Isaiah 44:3 "I will pour my spirit upon thy seed, and my blessing upon thine offspring". Ezekiel 36:27 "And I will put my spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes".
- In the New Testament it renews, bears fruit, and helps in prayer. Titus 3:5 "by the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost". Galatians 5:22 "But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith," beside 5:19 "the works of the flesh". Romans 8:16 "The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God:". Romans 8:26 "Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered."
What follows the Spirit's coming in these rows is prophecy, speech, judgment, strength, wisdom, anointing, renewal, fruit, and the covenant gift to the people and their seed.
